# J2 — The record crosses over

*Iteration 12. Branch `j2-record`, cut from `scaffold-12`. Spec: F2 in
`iteration-12-ideas.md`, Life A1/A2 and Systems A3 in `iteration-12-pm/`.*

Until now the founder's record stopped at the Life tab. Notoriety was
read by the crime sweep and the espionage trace and by nothing else. An
open case cost the company nothing until the verdict. A beef, a
cancellation or a firing with cause never reached a board, a term sheet
or a candidate. This lane joins them up. The board reads the papers,
recruiters ring your ex-employees, and fame makes everything you hide
easier to find.

A founder with no case, no beef, no cancellation, no mean act on staff,
no firing with cause, no guilty verdict and no fame gets +0 on the board
line, a name of 0, asks at exactly the rolled salary and a spotlight of
×1.0. The fixtures and pacing bots did not move.

## What I built

### The engine

- **`FounderStanding.swift`** (pure; all of it J2's). `StandingBoardLine`,
  `StandingName`, and the `FounderStanding` enum:
  - the court record: `openCases`, `guiltyVerdicts`;
  - `boardLine`, `keyPersonClauseApplies`, `keyPersonPrice`;
  - `vouchingAlumni`, `name`, `ask`, `refusingCandidateID`;
  - `spotlight`, plus the copy.

  It also adds `GameState` readers for the app: `standingName`,
  `standingAsk(for:)`, `standingRefuses`, `standingSpotlight`,
  `standingDiscoveryChance` and `standingBoardLine`. Nothing in the file
  draws or mutates.
- **`Balance/BalanceConfig+Standing.swift`**: `StandingRecordBalance`, 19
  keys, each decode-if-present against the shipped default. It is one
  property, `founderStanding`, under the J2 marker in `BalanceConfig`,
  and `"founderStanding"` is appended at the end of `Balance.json`.
- **`Systems/InvestorSystem.swift`**, J2 regions:

    review.
  - **the key-person clause** is applied to the rolled cheque after all
    three `investorRNG` draws, so no draw is added.
  - the review records `founderQuarter` and emits
    `.standingFounderQuarter` when the line is non-zero.
  - a `#if DEBUG` extension at the end of the file runs the real
    `quarterlyReview` for the screenshot pass.
- **`Investors.swift`**:
  - `BoardReview.founderQuarter` and
    `InvestmentOffer.standingKeyPersonClause`. Both decode if present and
    are encoded only when non-default, via hand-written coders that write
    the same keys in the same order the synthesized ones did.
- **`Interactions.swift` / `Systems/InteractionSystem.swift`**:
  - `InteractionState.standingMeanDays`: the days a `.mean` interaction
    was used on an employee, pruned to the 180-day window. Decoded if
    present, written only when non-empty.
- **`Systems/HiringSystem.swift`**:
  - the interview refuses the refusing candidate.
- **`Systems/EmployeeSystem.swift`** (my own J2 pair at the point of use):
  - `hire` refuses them too, and pays `standingAsk`.
- **`Crime.swift`**:

- **`Espionage.swift` / `Systems/EspionageSystem.swift`**:
  - `traceChance` gains a defaulted `spotlight`. The operation rolls it,

### The app

- **Hiring sheet** (`Screens/Team/HiringSheet.swift`):
  - one line at the top, `YOUR NAME: +20% ON ASKS` (with
    `· 2 ALUMNI VOUCH` when anyone vouches). It is drawn only when the
    name is above zero or someone vouches.
  - under it, a sentence that names the candidate who will not come in.
  - every salary reads `$889/wk · was $742` once the name is on it.
  - the refusing candidate's Interview and Hire are disabled, with *"They
    rang someone who used to work for you."*
- **Cap table** (`Screens/Business/InvestorsView.swift`):
  - each review prints `THE FOUNDER'S QUARTER: +20` under its note.
  - above the history, the next review's line is shown before it lands:
    *"They read the papers. Next review: +20 for an open case and 3
    rounds of a public beef."*
  - the term-sheet card prints `KEY-PERSON CLAUSE: −15%` with a sentence.
- **The term-sheet prompt** (`Components/DecisionSheet.swift`, own J2
  pair) appends *"Key-person clause: −15%. They read about the case."*.
  This is the sheet that pauses the clock, so it is the one players read.
- **Espionage rows** (`EspionageCard.swift`): a `Spotlight ×2.00 · the
  famous are easier to follow` line under the odds whenever the spotlight
  is above one. The printed trace odds already include it.
- **Crime ledger** (`CrimeCard.swift`, `CrimeScreen.swift`): the exposure
  line and each record row read `standingDiscoveryChance`, so the number
  on screen is the number the sweep rolls. The exposure line also says
  *"Fame puts ×1.50 on it."*
- **`EventCopy`**: two journal lines.

## The numbers

| Mechanic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Board line | +8 per open case with the founder as defendant, +12 per guilty verdict heard this quarter, +4 per round of the running beef, +6 for an unanswered cancellation; cap +25 |
| Key-person clause | No board seat and an open case: cheque ×0.85, same equity, so the valuation is 15% lower |
| Name | notoriety ×0.5, +6 per firing with cause, +3 per mean act on staff in 180 days (cap 24), +10 per guilty verdict, −4 per fame level, −3 per alumnus at rapport ≥ 60; clamped 0…100 |
| Asks | salary × (1 + name/200), at display and at hire; a name of 40 is +20% |
| Refusal | At a name ≥ 50, the best CV (skill total) in the pool will not interview or be hired; friends never refuse and never pay the premium |
| Spotlight | 1 + 0.25 × fame level: Known 1.25, Followed 1.5, Notable 1.75, Famous 2.0, A name 2.25. Multiplies crime discovery (laundering included) and espionage trace, before their ceilings |
| Rung drop | A fine, a court-ordered settlement or a sentence, or a trace: fame drops to the foot of the rung below |

## Deviations

1. **The refusal also blocks the hire, not only the interview.** A
   candidate who will not come in for an interview does not sign either.
   Friends hired through `FriendSystem` are exempt from the refusal and
   the premium: they already know you.
2. **"Beef rounds last quarter" means the rounds of the beef still
   running.** `FameState` keeps only the current beef and no history, so
   a beef that ended mid-quarter stops counting. A true per-quarter count
   needs a field in N4's state (follow-up 3).
3. **What counts as guilty:** a fine, a sentence, or a settlement ordered
   by the court. These are the verdicts that raise `crime_convicted`.
   Paying the other side before the hearing (`settledDay`) is not guilty.
   The verdict's day is `hearingDay`.
4. **Naming.** `BalanceConfig.StandingBalance` already exists (the
   market's standing ledger), so the block is `StandingRecordBalance` and
   the key is `founderStanding`, not `standing`.
5. **Shared files touched outside the scaffold's markers, each under its
   own J2 pair:**
   - `EmployeeSystem.hire` and the `CrimeSystem` sweep and verdict;
   - `DecisionSheet`: `investmentPrompt` gained a `balance` parameter,
     and its one caller passes it;
   - `AppRootView`: the debug task, and a DEBUG-only hold on the decision
     sheet during my screenshot flags;
   - `CrimeCard`, `CrimeScreen`;
   - the stale doc comment in `BalanceConfig+DirtyMoney.swift`.
6. **No `standing_` content events.** The two GameEvents are journal
   lines only, so no event pool changed.
7. **The rung drop does not stick forever.** Followers are untouched, so
   fame climbs back toward its target over the following weeks. The
   ideas doc says "drops one rung", not "and the audience leaves"
   (follow-up 1).

## The "how it fails" checks

The checks ran as a scratch executable, not a test. It copies the test
target's `SimRunner`, `PacingBots` and `InvestorBots` (all public API) and
lays a darker founder over `InvestorBot`, the bot that takes term sheets
and so seats boards. The founder profiles:

- **one-crime**: tries cooked books / dodged taxes from day 180, once.
- **serial-crime**: keeps trying every quarter after the first.
- **mean-boss**: argues with an employee every fortnight from day 60.
- **dark**: both.

Each profile ran ten seeds for three years from the garage, with a clean
control. Nothing from the script is committed.

| Profile | Runs with a case | …with a board seated while it was open | Reviews printing the line (avg) | Key-person sheets / offers | Hires after the first dark act, per run | Avg premium on those hires | Max name | Endings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 0/10 | — | 0 | 0/49 | — | +0.0% | 0 | alive 6, bankrupt 4 |
| one-crime | 8/10 | 5/8 | 8 (+12.0) | 4/42 | 19.4 | +6.6% | 20 | alive 8, bankrupt 2 |
| serial-crime | 10/10 | 9/10 | 37 (+13.5) | 7/42 | 20.0 | +22.6% | 100 | alive 5, bankrupt 2, ousted 3 |
| mean-boss | 0/10 | — | 0 | 0/46 | 20.0 | +11.3% | 24 | alive 4, bankrupt 6 |
| dark | 10/10 | 7/10 | 29 (+12.7) | 5/36 | 15.7 | +29.8% | 100 | alive 2, bankrupt 4, ousted 4 |

What they showed:

- **The board does see the case.** The fallback rule was "if under two in
  five runs with a case have a board seated, move the weight onto the
  haircut". The result is 5/8, 9/10 and 7/10, so the board line carries
  the weight as written and the haircut stays secondary: 4–7 clause
  sheets per 40-odd offers. The ousting column is the join doing its
  job. The clean control is never ousted; a serial offender is ousted in
  3 of 10 runs, and the dark founder in 4.
- **Founders keep hiring after the record starts.** The fallback rule was
  "if under two hires a run, move the name onto rivals' poach odds". The
  result is 15.7–20.0 hires a run after the first dark act, so the asks
  stay where they are. A single crime costs about +7% on every later
  hire; a fortnightly argument, about +11% (the mean-act cap is +12% on
  its own).
- **What it doesn't show:**
  - **Serial offenders saturate at a name of 100** and refuse for most
    of the run (3,195 and 4,465 refusing days across ten runs).
    Notoriety maxes out, and guilty verdicts never leave the name (the
    case list is capped at 12, not windowed). This may be too harsh
    (follow-up 2).
  - **Fame drops: zero.** No bot posts, so the spotlight and the rung
    drop are shown by screenshot, not by the check.
  - **mean-boss goes bankrupt 6/10 against the control's 4/10.** Most of
    that is N2's morale hit from arguing, not the premium.

## Follow-ups

1. **Make the rung drop stick.** Cut followers as well, or give N4's fame
   target a penalty window. Both are N4/J1 territory in `FameSystem`.
2. **A window on guilty verdicts in the name.** Right now a serial
   offender saturates at 100 for the rest of the run. Something like
   "+10 per guilty verdict in the last two years" would let a reformed
   founder climb out, the way notoriety already decays.
3. **Beef history.** A per-quarter round count needs N4 to keep closed
   beefs, or at least `roundsSince(day:)`.
4. **The next-review preview on the pitch room's board counterpart**:
   M2's `PitchRoom` could quote the line in the board's opening.
5. **Pixel dressing for the name line.** It uses `Theme` typography on
   the existing sheet rather than a pixel strip.
6. **`make strings`** at the merge picks up the new copy.
7. **Pre-existing warning, not mine.** `EventCopy.swift:179` "switch must
   be exhaustive" lists only N1's `crime…` cases.

## Debug flags

All DEBUG only, parsed in `DebugLaunch`'s J2 region and sent through
`GameAction.standingDebug` by a task in `AppRootView`. Each is re-sent
until the state shows it, so a fixture swap cannot eat it.

- `-autoStanding <name>`: notoriety, then mean acts on staff, then
  firings with cause, enough for a name of about `<name>` after fame and
  alumni. Pair with `-autoRoute hiring`.
- `-autoBoardReview case`: seats a board if there is none, opens a case
  (cooked books, through `CrimeSystem.raiseCase`) and a three-round beef,
  then runs the real quarterly review today. It lifts the board card onto
  a sheet. Pair with `-autoRoute investors`.
- `-autoBoardReview offer`: opens a case and writes a term sheet today,
  key-person clause and all. It needs a fixture with no board seat, e.g.
  `release-garage-day40`.
- `-autoSpotlight <level>`: fame at the foot of a level (`known` …
  `star`). Pair with `-autoRoute spy -autoSpyCard`.
- `-autoRoute hiring` and `-autoRoute investors` (also `standing`,
  `board`, `boardreview`) are new route names.
- While any J2 flag except `offer` is set, the decision sheet is held
  back, so fixture questions do not cover the shot.
