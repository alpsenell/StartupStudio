# J1 — Doors

*Iteration 12. Branch `j1-doors`, cut from `scaffold-12`.*

Nobody asks permission. Four one-time doors from the company game into
rooms that already exist and would otherwise sleep until the player went
looking: **the shark** calls a founder near the wall who never opened
Finances, **somebody at the launch party** offers a founder who has not
been home before midnight in a month, **a journalist** wants a quote
after a launch reviewed 80, and **the home** wants a number when a parent
has a fall. Each is one question with a fourteen-day deadline. Yes wakes
the room; no, or silence, leaves it asleep for the rest of the run.

Bundled with it: Life B5 (the launch-day sheet's *Tell people*, and the
rooms surfaced from the moments that want them) and Meta B1 (eleven coach
tips keyed to the state of the run instead of chapter-1 goals, plus one
per open door).

## What I built

### The engine

- **`Doors.swift`** — `DoorKind` (shark, vices, fame, care), `DoorChoice`
  (accept, decline, lapsed, and the care door's careHome / careSpareRoom /
  careSibling), `DoorRecord`, `DoorState` (decode-if-present,
  encode-when-non-default, `.empty`), `DoorRefusal`, and the pure
  `DoorRules`: every threshold as a file constant, `meets(kind:…)`,
  `weeklyBurn`, `runwayIsShort`, `bestReview`, `careAge(seed:)`,
  `careSubject`, `refusal`, `flag`.
- **`Systems/DoorSystem.swift`** — runs last in `Reducer.systems`, every
  day: the 28-day crunch window (a `UInt32` bit mask, recorded only from
  day 62 and cleared for good once the vices question has been asked),
  the deadlines (a door past its day closes as `.lapsed`), the spare
  room's weekly evening, and from day 90 at most one new door a day, with
  a phone message and a `.doorOpened` event. `answer` routes a yes into
  the room's own function; `debugOpen` is `#if DEBUG`.
- **The identity gate: `.armDoors`.** `DoorSystem.run` returns on its
  first line until `state.doors.armed`, and the only thing that sets it
  is `GameAction.armDoors`, which the app's HUD sends once per engine.
  See deviation 1.
- **Marked J1 regions in the rooms' own files**, each one function:
  - `DirtyMoneySystem.doorOffer` — the lane wakes exactly as
    `.noticeFinancesOpened` would wake it, and the man who called *is*
    the offer on the table (`.theShark`, the usual cheque formula, the
    usual `offerDays`), with no weekly roll because he has already rung.
  - `AssetsSystem.doorEngage` — `noticeOpened` (the screen's own
    engagement), then the vice with the highest `perLaunch` (drink, +6)
    starts at what one launch party adds.
  - `FameSystem.doorOpenFeed` — the journalist's piece runs with your
    quote: +240 followers, which is what moves `state.fame` off `.empty`.
    The post itself is drafted by the app and posted through the ordinary
    `post` on a tap.
  - `FamilyDramaSystem.doorCare` — `openRoom`, then the parent's
    `careSinceDay` (so the room never asks twice), and one of: the home's
    weekly bill (`careWeeklyBill`, the `family_care` flag, the existing
    `.familyCareStarted` event); the spare room (no bill; an evening a
    week, booked by `DoorSystem`); the sibling (no bill; bond −20).
- **`GameAction`** `.armDoors`, `.answerDoor(kind:choice:)`,
  `.openDoor(kind:)` (debug builds only); **`Reducer`** one system and
  three handlers; **`GameEvent`** `.doorOpened`, `.doorAnswered` (both
  `.info` by the default arm: they never pause); **`GameState`** the
  `doors` slot, its coding key, decode and encode.
- **Nothing draws.** Every condition is a read; the care age is a private
  `SeededRNG` off `state.seed` (the `FamilyKin.derive` pattern), never a
  state stream. No door touches `narrative.lastFiredDay` or
  `pendingChoice`, and none pauses.
- **No balance keys.** The thresholds are file constants in `DoorRules`,
  as the systems report allows ("0–2; the thresholds can stay file
  constants"), so `BalanceConfig` and `Balance.json` are untouched.

### Content — 4 `door_` life events

Appended at the end of `LifeEvents.json`, each gated on a flag only a
*spoken* answer raises (`door_shark_declined`, `door_vices`,
`door_fame_declined`, `door_care`) — a lapsed door raises nothing, so a
bot's pool can never widen: Denny's Christmas card, the napkin number
texting at two in the morning, the piece that ran without you, the photo
from the home.

## The four doors

| Door | Opens when | Yes | No / silence |
|---|---|---|---|
| The shark | armed, day ≥ 90, dirty money never noticed, runway ≤ 4 weeks (cash ÷ the HUD's weekly burn; in debt counts) | Dirty money wakes, Denny's offer on the table | Finances stays quiet |
| The launch party | armed, day ≥ 90, Assets not engaged, 21 of the last 28 days on crunch (`effectiveSchedule`) | Assets engaged, drink +6 | nothing |
| On the record | armed, day ≥ 90, feed never used, a launch reviewed ≥ 80 or reputation ≥ 60 | +240 followers, compose with the post drafted | the feed stays empty |
| The care bill | armed, day ≥ 365, family room never opened, a living parent at the seed's fall age (69–72) | the room opens at the care beat: the home (−$900 a week), the spare room (an evening a week), or the sibling (bond −20) | the room stays shut |

Fourteen days to answer, one door opening per day at most, each door once
per run, never while away or inside.

## Deviations

1. **The identity gate is `.armDoors`, not "a run that never meets a
   door".** Built as specced, the doors opened in bot and replay runs —
   crunch-hire meets the vices condition on day 90 on every seed — and
   two pins moved: `ReducerTests.eventLogNeverExceeds500Entries` (the
   500-entry window shifted by the door events) and
   `ReleaseFixtureGenerator.theCommittedFixturesStillDecodeAndStillMatchTheirBriefs`
   (`release-studio-day400` and `release-campus-day900` replayed a phone
   message and a door record their committed bytes do not have). Rather
   than re-pin, the doors now wait for the app: the HUD sends
   `.armDoors` once per game, which asks "is somebody playing", not "did
   they open the room". Every bot, replay, fixture and engine test runs
   unarmed and writes exactly what it wrote. A real player's save gains
   `"doors":{"armed":true}`.
2. **The care age is 69–72, seed-derived, not `familyDrama.careAge`
   (78).** The parents start at 66 and 68 (`FamilyKin`), so 78 is ten
   years away in a two-to-five-year run: the door would be dead on every
   seed. The fall age is a private stream off the seed, the way the
   family itself is derived.
3. **The door is a pushed page, not a modal sheet.** LifeScreen's J1
   regions are a destination, a card, a destination case and the route
   consumption — a push. It is drawn on pixel paper like one.
4. **Three marked lines in J6's `NoticeRail.swift`, with the lead's
   go-ahead.** The rail builds its queue privately, its deferred row only
   recalled a `pendingChoice`, and `activeTip` reads `CoachTip.all`,
   which `TutorialScriptTests` pins at the chapter-1 six — so neither a
   door nor a state tip could reach it from J1's own files. See *Shared
   files touched*. The deferred row is handed a title, not the notice
   id, so its *Answer* finds the door by its rail title among the doors
   open today (`DoorRail.route(forTitle:state:)`) rather than changing
   the row's signature.
5. **B5's "links" are tips, and *Tell people*.** The wedding, the burnout
   and the launch party are not story beats with a sheet of their own
   (the wedding is a relationship-stage change, the burnout a
   `.founderAway`, and no life event is a launch party), so each is
   surfaced the way B5 asks — "a rail line with a button" — as a
   state-keyed tip. `EventPresenter` needed no change.
6. **The shark's offer does not wait for the weekly roll.** "As if
   Finances had been opened" alone would leave a yes waiting on a 45%
   weekly roll that also needs cash under a month of operating cost; the
   man who called is put on the table directly, deterministically.
7. **No balance block.** All thresholds are `DoorRules` constants.

## The "how it fails" check

A scratch executable outside the repo (`SimRunner` and `PacingBots`
copied from the test target, the engine by path), no test added: the ten
`BalanceTargetsTests` seeds, 730 days, rivals off, each bot wrapped to
send `.armDoors` on day 1 and never answer. A door that opens is exactly
a run that met its condition.

Runs meeting each door's condition, out of 10 (median opening day):

| Bot | Shark | Vices | Fame | Care |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| solo-slow | 0 | 0 | 0 | 5 (365) |
| crunch-hire | 10 (106) | 10 (90) | 7 (359) | 5 (365) |
| saas-builder | 9 (90) | 0 | 8 (465) | 4 (365) |
| neglectful | 1 (581) | 10 (90) | 0 | 6 (365) |

The same run with the first fall age (69–71): care 8, 8, 7 and **10** in
10 — a nag by the doc's own line — and every other column identical.
That is why the fall age is 69–72 (deviation 2).

**What it showed.**

- **Fame is in the band** (7–8 in 10 for the two bots that ship well, 0
  for the two that do not), and it arrives late — days 239 to 730 —
  which is when a journalist would call.
- **Care is in the band** after the change (4–6 in 10), always at day 365
  or 728 — the father's birthday in the calendar year.
- **Vices is a switch, not a rate:** every seed of a bot that crunches,
  no seed of a bot that does not, always on day 90 (the window is full
  from day 62). That is the spec — "crunch-hire bots meet this on most
  seeds" — and it is what a founder who has lived on crunch for a month
  should get. A player on normal hours never sees it.
- **The shark is loud for a growing company:** 10 and 9 in 10 for the two
  growth bots, most of them on day 90 exactly — a garage that hires has
  four weeks of runway or less for most of its first year. Over 8 in 10
  is the doc's "nag" line. I have left the spec's threshold (it is the
  office scene's own "runway is short" line, and the door still asks
  once per run) and put the tighter option in follow-up 3.
- None of the doors is dead: every one opens for at least one bot shape
  on at least 4 seeds in 10.
- Bots never answer, so every door above closed as `.lapsed`; the `door_`
  events were never eligible.

## Follow-ups

1. **Carry the three rail hooks through J6's rework** at merge (see
   *Shared files touched*). If J6's one queue replaces the rail's
   sources, a door is a `PendingChoice`-shaped entry with severity
   `.info`, its `respondByDay`, the default `.lapsed`, and the route
   `.door(kind)`; once the row knows its notice id,
   `DoorRail.route(forNoticeID:)` can replace the title lookup.
2. **Tip dismissal is per install** (`GameSettings.dismissedTips`), so
   dismissing `tip.door.shark` in one run hides the next run's shark tip
   too. Per-run dismissal would need the seed in the key.
3. **The shark is loud for a growing company** (see the check): at the
   spec's four weeks it opens for nearly every growth bot, most on day
   90. If playtests agree, three weeks — "three weeks from the wall" —
   or a falling-runway condition.
4. **`OfficePressure` could show an open door** (systems B5's photographer
   and black car are the same idea); the office scene is PixelKit's.
5. **The spare room's evening** is booked, not shown: the week card could
   say where it went.
6. **One spare room, two uses.** A parent given the spare room at the care
   door and W2's in-laws row ("The spare room is empty · Offer it") do
   not know about each other (`05-care-spare.png`): the in-laws can
   still be offered a room that is taken. `FamilyDramaSystem.spareRoom`
   should refuse while `state.doors.careTakesAnEvening`, and the row
   should say who is in it — W2's function and row, not J1's.

## Debug flags

- `-autoRoute door -autoDoor shark|vices|fame|care` — opens that door
  today through the engine's debug action and lands on its sheet.
- `-autoDoorAnswer accept|decline|careHome|careSpareRoom|careSibling` —
  answers it once the sheet is up, through `.answerDoor`.
- `-autoTip <id or goal key>` — forces a coach tip's trigger on (for the
  day the rail is hooked up).
- `-autoTellPeople` — puts the launch-day sheet up for the newest release,
  so *Tell people* can be photographed (`-autoRoute launchday` is the war
  room's moment, a different screen).
