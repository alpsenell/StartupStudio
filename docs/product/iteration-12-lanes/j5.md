# J5 — Announce the date, and premium means something

*Iteration 12. Branch `j5-announce`, cut from `scaffold-12`. Builds F5
("Announce the date") and I1 ("Premium means something") from
`iteration-12-ideas.md`, with the numbers from `iteration-12-pm/company.md`
A2 and B1.*

A build with an ETA can be given a ship date. Saying it out loud holds the
hype (1% a day, not 2%) and makes every campaign on it land ×1.25; the
newspaper prints it and the war room counts down to it. Miss it and the
paper runs a correction with a new date, at reputation −4 and hype ×0.6;
miss that and it is −8, ×0.4, and nobody prints a third. The copycat reads
the paper too: an announced product is ripe at four weeks, not eight.
Premium, meanwhile, finally has a number that depends on the product: its
demand follows the reviews, so a review of 85 earns 1.28× standard's
revenue per buyer — and each live bug costs it twice.

## What I built

### The engine

- **`Announce.swift`** — `AnnounceRefusal` (eight reasons, each a
  sentence), the pure `Announce` half (the announced decay rate, the
  campaign factor, what each slip costs, the "74% instead of 55%" read off
  the balance, the three flags), `Product.isAnnounced` / `wasAnnounced` /
  `announceIsVoid`, and on `GameState`: `announceRefusal`,
  `announceEarliestDay`, `announceProposal` (ETA + slack, never inside the
  minimum notice), `announceDaysLeft`, `announceIsBehind`,
  `announcedBuilds`, `announceInterviewWarmth`.
- **`Systems/AnnounceSystem.swift`** — the daily system (appended under
  the J5 marker in `Reducer.systems`): a build still in development the day
  after its date has slipped; a product that shipped on or before its date
  gets one line in the paper the next day; the `announce_live` flag stands
  while any date does. The two actions: `announce` and the debug-only
  `forceSlip`. Draws nothing; skips every product nobody announced.
- **`Systems/RivalSystem+Announce.swift`** — `announceRipeDay`, read from
  the one marked line in `RivalSystem.copycatCheck` (the guard's
  `info.launchDay <= ripeDay` became `<= announceRipeDay(ripeDay, for:
  product, balance)`; it returns `ripeDay` untouched for a product nobody
  announced).
- **`Product.swift`** (J5 regions) — `announcedDay: Int?` and `slips: Int`,
  decode-if-present, written only when set (`encodeIfPresent`, and `slips`
  only when non-zero).
- **`Systems/MarketingSystem.swift`** (J5 regions) — the decay reads
  `Announce.hypeDecayRate(for:)` (the ordinary rate, returned untouched,
  for an unannounced build); one-shot campaigns and daily social pushes
  on an announced build in development are multiplied by 1.25, with the
  multiply skipped entirely when the factor is 1.
- **`Systems/ProductSystem.swift`** (J5 regions) — I1: weekly sales read
  `economy.demandFactor(for:reviewScore:)` and
  `economy.liveBugSalesPenalty(for:)`; both return the table's own number
  for every tier but premium, and for premium while the curve is off.
- **`Systems/PitchSystem.swift`** (own J5 pair, after N4's warmth line) —
  the launch-week interview starts `interviewWarmth` (12) warmer when the
  product's date holds (or it shipped on it) and 12 cooler when today's
  ETA is past it or it already slipped. Exactly 0 for a product nobody
  announced.
- **`GameAction`** `.announceShipDate(productID:day:)`,
  `.announceForceSlip(productID:)` (DEBUG only, like W4's
  `serveSentence`); **`Reducer`** system + handlers; **`GameEvent`**
  `.announceMade`, `.announceSlipped` (`newDay: nil` when it voids),
  `.announceKept`. A slip is `.notable` (it stops the clock, within the
  pause budget); naming or keeping a date is `.info`. The severity lines
  sit in their own J5 pair inside `GameEvent.severity`, after the WS-B
  block — the scaffold had no marker there.
- **Balance** — `BalanceConfig+Announce.swift`: `AnnounceBalance` (one
  defaulted property `announce` under the J5 marker in `BalanceConfig`,
  every key decode-if-present, the block itself optional through a
  `KeyedDecodingContainer` overload) and `AnnouncePremiumCurve` (one
  defaulted property `premiumReviewCurve` in `EconomyBalance`, next to the
  price-tier keys, off by default). `Balance.json`: `"announce"` appended
  at the end; `"premiumReviewCurve"` inside `"economy"` after
  `premiumChurnPenalty`.

### The app

- **`Screens/Products/Announce/AnnounceSheet.swift`** — the press release
  on pixel paper: the build, today's ETA ("Skills grow and people leave;
  the ETA assumes neither"), and the four lines of the trade, every number
  read from the balance. Then one row per date — on the ETA, +7, +14 —
  each carrying its own slack, risk and distance ("14 days of slack: room
  for a bad fortnight · 37 days out · the usual"), and *Stay quiet* with
  what that costs. A refused date shows its sentence instead.
- **`AnnounceViews.swift`** — `AnnounceCard` on a build's page (under the
  forecast, above Ship it): the *Announce for <day>* button with the trade
  under it, or the standing date, its countdown, and — when today's ETA is
  past it — how late and what the slip will cost. `AnnounceCountdownLine`
  in the war room's countdown panel: "Promised in 37 days", and whether the
  gate makes it — and, when true, "the ship gate makes it; the finished
  build does not" (see the check below). `AnnounceRoute` for
  `Route.announce`.
- **`AnnounceEventPresenter.swift`** — the three events' journal lines,
  read by `EventCopy`'s J5 region. The slip is the paper's lead that week
  (the `NewspaperComposer` J5 region ranks it +25, a new date +15), and the
  latest issue's small print lists every standing date ("Pulse is promised
  for March 18, 23 days away.").
- **`LiveOps.swift`** — `priceCaption(for:balance:reviewScore:)`. With the
  score and the curve on, premium says what it earns *here* ("+28% revenue
  per buyer at a review of 88 · each live bug costs twice as much"; under
  70: "under 70 it drives buyers away"); budget and standard add
  "premium would earn +N% at a review of S" when premium would beat them.
  Without a score, or with the curve off, the caption is the old one word
  for word. `ProductDetailScreen` passes the score (J5 pair).
- **`AnnounceRail.swift`** — the rail notice, built with
  `RailNotice.deferred(id:title:daysLeft:category:)` for every date within
  a fortnight. **Not wired** — see follow-up 1.
- `Route.announce` (AppRouter J5 regions, Products tab),
  `ProductsScreen` J5 pair (consumes it, and the debug room below),
  `AppRootView` J5 pair (starts the debug hands), `WarRoomScreen` J5 pairs
  (the line; the panel's accessibility label reads the date after the ETA).

### Content — 10 `announce_` events

Appended at the end of `Events.json`, every one gated on a flag only this
feature raises:

| Gate | Events |
|---|---|
| `announce_live` + a build in development | the fan site's countdown clock, the stand-up question ("is the date on the website a real date?"), the reseller's pre-orders (sets `announce_preorders`), the reporter who wrote it in pen, the rival that announced the week before yours |
| `announce_slipped` | the eleven-page forum thread, the correction framed in the kitchen, the investor's "Fwd:" |
| `announce_slipped` + `announce_preorders` | the reseller wants its $5,000 back (clears the flag) |
| `announce_void` | "A journalist asks when it ships, then says: actually, don't." |

## The numbers

| | Quiet | Announced |
|---|---|---|
| Hype decay | 2% a day (55% kept over 30 days) | 1% a day (74% kept) |
| Campaigns (one-shot and daily push) | ×1 | ×1.25 |
| Copycat ripe after launch | 8 weeks | 4 weeks (kept, slipped or void) |
| Launch-week interview | — | +12 warmth on track, −12 behind or slipped |
| First slip | — | reputation −4, hype ×0.6, new date = today's ETA + 14 |
| Second slip | — | reputation −8, hype ×0.4, announcement void |

A date must be at least 21 days out (`minLeadDays`), no more than 14 days
past today's ship-gate ETA (`maxSlackDays`), and can only be given while
that ETA is at least 14 days away (`earlyLeadDays`). The last two came out
of the check below.

Premium demand is `min(0.8, max(0.3, 0.6 + 0.02 × (review − 75)))`:

| Review | Premium demand | × price 1.6 | vs standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 0.30 (floor) | 0.48 | −52% (and the under-70 drag on top) |
| 70 | 0.50 | 0.80 | −20% |
| 76 | 0.62 | 0.99 | about even |
| 80 | 0.70 | 1.12 | +12% |
| 85+ | 0.80 (cap) | 1.28 | +28% |

Live bugs cost premium 2 × 0.015 = 3% of sales each (the 60% cap is
unchanged). The 0.85 share weight is untouched, so a copy in the topic
still takes more from premium than from standard: premium has a window.

**The floor (0.3) is mine.** The spec's line goes to zero at a review of
45 and negative below it, which would delist a badly reviewed premium
product in its first week. The ideas doc and the lens are silent.

## How it fails — the check, and what I changed

Spec: "compare actual ship day against the ETA taken at 50% progress over a
debug run; if 90% or more ship on time, tie the bonus to announcing early."

I ran it as a scratch SwiftPM executable against this branch's packages
(not a test; kept as `iteration-12-lanes/j5/announce-check.swift`): a
simple studio bot through the real reducer on the shipped balance, 30 seeds
× 3 years, three studio shapes (solo founder; a crew of three; a crew of
six with desktop tools), 521 builds. For each build it takes the ship-gate
ETA (what `ShipETA` and the sheet use) at 25% and 50% of the way to the
gate, then the day the gate opened and the day every pool was full.

| At 50% progress | Solo (182) | Crew of 3 (279) | Crew of 6 (60) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate day − ETA, median / max | 0 / +17 | 0 / +18 | 0 / +18 |
| Gate by ETA + 0 | 81% | 73% | 75% |
| Gate by ETA + 7 | 91% | 94% | 93% |
| Gate by ETA + 14 (the sheet's usual) | 94% | 99% | 97% |
| Gate by ETA + 28 | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Days from 50% to the gate ETA, median | 36 | 20 | 35 |
| Finished build by the ETA + 14 date | 0% | 5% | 0% |
| At 25%: sheet date (ETA + 14) on time | 95% | 97% | 95% |

**What it says.** The ETA is right to the day (median error 0, even from
25%), so builds do not run late by much and "skills grow" does not make
them early by much either. With two weeks of slack 94–99% ship on time —
over the spec's 90% line — and at four weeks nobody ever missed. Worse, a
crew of three is within three weeks of the gate at half-way more than half
the time, and a date given then for a build that could almost ship now is
all upside.

**What I changed (the spec's remedy, sized by the data).**
1. *Only an early announcement.* A date can be given only while today's
   gate ETA is at least `earlyLeadDays` (14) away; after that the sheet
   says "It could nearly ship already. A date this close is a launch, not
   an announcement." That removes the zero-risk case.
2. *No free slack.* A date can be at most `maxSlackDays` (14) past the ETA
   (the sheet dropped its +28 row; the engine refuses a later date as
   `tooFar`). The sheet's three rows are now on the ETA (~80% on time),
   +7 (~93%), +14 (~97%): the player buys safety with a date further out,
   which is fewer hype-hold days and a later ship.
3. *Said out loud.* The real cost the check found is that the date is the
   ship **gate**: a finished build almost never makes it (0–5%). The war
   room now says so when it is true — "the ship gate makes it; the finished
   build does not" — which is the spec's decision: crunch, ship it rough,
   or slip.

Even with both rules, +14 is ~97% safe. If playtests say announcing is
still free, the next lever is the default: propose +7, not +14 (follow-up 4).

## Identity at the default

- No bot announces (`grep announceShipDate` finds nothing in the test
  targets) and no bot prices premium (`RivalFightBots.swift:83` sets
  budget, and only that); the pacing suite never reaches the new code.
- `Announce.hypeDecayRate` returns `balance.hypeDecayRate` itself for an
  unannounced build; the campaign multiply is skipped at factor 1;
  `demandFactor(for:reviewScore:)` / `liveBugSalesPenalty(for:)` return the
  table's own numbers for every tier but premium; `announceRipeDay`
  returns `ripeDay`; the interview adds nothing at 0.
- `AnnounceSystem.run` draws nothing, and skips every product with
  `wasAnnounced == false`. No new `rng` / `worldRNG` / `socialRNG` draws.
- Product keys are written only when set; the `announce_` events are all
  flag-gated, so a clean run's event pool is the pool it was.
- `EconomyBalance.default` has the curve **off**, so every test economy
  (`TestBalance.neutralEconomy` builds on it) prices premium at the flat
  0.6 it always did; the shipped `Balance.json` turns it on.
- Result: the four fixtures, `BalanceTargetsTests` and
  `InvestorTargetsTests` did not move (engine suite green at 943).

## Deviations

1. **The rail carries nothing yet.** `NoticeRail.swift` is J6's and its
   queue is private to it; `AnnounceRail.notices(state:)` builds the
   `deferred` notices and the one line that wires them is follow-up 1.
2. **Two refusals the spec did not have** (`tooLate`, and `tooFar` as a
   cap on slack): the "how it fails" check's remedy, above.
3. **The premium floor of 0.3**, above.
4. **`GameEvent.severity`** has no scaffold marker; the three lines are in
   their own J5 pair after the WS-B block.
5. **The interview reads the date as warmth**, not as a new exchange. The
   pitch room's exchanges are M2's catalog; a warmth line needed only a
   marked edit where N4's already sits.
6. **A voided announcement can not be re-announced** (`AnnounceRefusal
   .void`). The spec says "void"; I read it as final for that build.
7. **The screenshot room is a fixture.** `-autoRoute warRoom` opens the
   room on `WarRoomFixture`'s own engine (5 days out — too late to
   announce), so `-autoRoute announceroom` opens the same fixture at 85
   days out and announces on it.

  can take a date, with its sheet open.
- `-autoRoute premium` + `-autoPremium` — the best-reviewed release, priced
  premium, on its page.
- `-autoAnnounce` / `-autoAnnounce slip` / `-autoAnnounce void` — on
  launch, announce the war room's build (or the soonest announceable one)
  at the usual slack, then miss it once or twice through
  `.announceForceSlip` (DEBUG only).
- `-autoRoute announceroom` — the war room on its fixture engine at 85
