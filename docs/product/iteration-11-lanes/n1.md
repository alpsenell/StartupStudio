# N1 — Crime, scandal and the courtroom

*Iteration 11, wave one. Branch `n1-crime`, cut from `scaffold-11`.*

## What the founder can now do

Six offences, a notoriety meter, a weekly chance of being found out, a
case with a hearing four to eight weeks out, three things to do before it
(settle, hire a lawyer, pick a defence), a courtroom with three exchanges
and a graded verdict, a sentence that hands the company to a caretaker,
and a suit the founder can bring against a rival with the same machinery
reversed.

**Identity at the default.** `GameState.crime` is `.empty` until an
offence action is applied, is encoded only while non-empty, and
`CrimeSystem.run` returns on its first line while it is empty. No tick, no
bot and no fixture reaches a line of the lane. Every gain is *added* once,
at the moment the player commits — there is no multiplier a clean run
passes through. Draws are `socialRNG` only (one word per open record entry
on the weekly sweep, one per courtroom exchange, one for the NDA poach,
one for the planted story's trace, one for the hearing date, one for a
bribed review's rewritten blurb); `rng` and `worldRNG` are never touched,
and the UUID the poached engineer carries is minted from `socialRNG` so
the candidate-pool stream is undisturbed.

## The offence table

Gains are the shipped balance (`"crime"` in `Balance.json`,
`BalanceConfig+Crime.swift`). "Discovery" is the *weekly* base chance per
open record entry, before notoriety warms it (`× (1 + notoriety/100 ×
1.2)`), age cools it (linearly to 25% over 40 weeks) and a Legal
department halves it; the product is capped at 35% a week.

| Offence | Gate | Cost | Gain | Notoriety | Discovery/wk | Gravity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cook the books | A released product, a term sheet or a round | — | Any term sheet arriving in the next 13 weeks reads ×1.35 on valuation and amount | +14 | 3.0% | 0.90 |
| Dodge the tax | A trading quarter with revenue | — | Half the notional quarterly bill (19% of 13 weeks' sales and contract revenue) stays in the company, posted as "Tax provision released" | +12 | 3.5% | 0.80 |
| The NDA poach | A rival, $2,500 wallet | $2,500 | 68% (0.45 × 1.5) a named senior lands at the top of the candidate pool, better than the desk's best by +14 skill; the rival loses 3 strength | +10 | 4.0% | 0.45 |
| Bribe a journalist | $4,000 wallet, no envelope already out | $4,000 | One outlet's review of the next launch comes back +12 points, blurb rewritten to match | +9 | 2.5% | 0.50 |
| Fake the demo | A build in development | — | +30 hype now; ship inside 14 days and live bugs are ×2.2 on launch day | +11 | 2.8% | 0.65 |
| Plant a story | A rival, $1,500 wallet | $1,500 | Their reputation −12; 35% it is traced, and then their strength +8 | +13 | 4.5% | 0.60 |

Notoriety decays a point a week and is capped at 100. Discovery only rolls
while the record has an open entry and no case is pending; at most one case
is raised a week.

**Turn yourself in** is a seventh button on the record: it raises the case
today with a third of the evidence, which is the only way to choose when.

## The case

- Hearing 4–8 weeks out (one `socialRNG` word).
- Evidence 0…1 = `gravity × age × (1 + notoriety/100 × 0.4) × (Legal ? 0.8 : 1)`, clamped 0.1…1.
- Settlement price = `(9,000 × gravity + 0.6 × gain) × (0.5 + evidence)`, floor $1,200. Wallet first, company for the rest.
- Lawyers, out of the founder's own wallet: duty solicitor (free, weight 0, no objections), high-street firm ($3,500, weight 0.18, one objection), the one on the building ($12,000, weight 0.38, two objections).
- Defences: *It never happened* (opening −14, swing ×1.35, rewards Deny), *Everybody does this* (−4, ×1.0, rewards Explain), *Not my department* (−8, ×1.1, rewards Blame the CFO), *Full cooperation* (+6, ×0.75, rewards Apologise).
- The newspaper leads with the case: `.crimeCaseRaised` is `.critical`, and `CrimeEventPresenter` gives it a real sentence. The office thread and the partner both post the day it is raised.

## The courtroom

Three exchanges. Each costs one `socialRNG` word whatever is said.

`landChance = 0.44 + skill/220 + lawyer.weight (+0.18 on your own line) − evidence × 0.30`, clamped 0.10…0.90. An objection is the lawyer's own: `0.44 + weight × 1.6`.

| Exchange | Graded on | Landed | Missed | Also |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deny it | Conversation | +26 | −29.9 | the wide one both ways |
| Explain the numbers | Finance | +18 | −10.8 | |
| Apologise | Conversation | +14 | −4.9 | |
| Blame the CFO | Leadership | +22 | −22 | every employee's morale −6 |
| Let the lawyer object | Conversation | +20 | −10 | needs an objection left |

All of it is then multiplied by the defence's swing, and by 1.2 when the
exchange is the one the defence is built on. Opening standing is
`defence.openingStanding − evidence × 34`.

**Verdict bands**, on final standing (−100…100):

| Standing | Verdict | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 42 | Acquitted | Company reputation +3, notoriety −6 |
| 4 … 42 | Fined | `14,000 × gravity + 0.5 × gain`, reputation −9 |
| −34 … 4 | Settled, with a gag order | `9,000 × gravity × 1.4 + 0.6 × gain`, reputation −4.5 |
| < −34 | Sentenced | 3–16 weeks, reputation −13.5 |

Not walking in is a verdict too: a day past the hearing the court hears it
in absentia at the opening standing minus 20.

**Prison** is `SabbaticalSystem.crimeBeginSentence` (N1's marked region
there): the away window with `awayReason "Inside"`, the caretaker
autopilot handed to the highest-bond employee, the weekly decisions and
the report on the way out. What the *sentence* costs on top is
`CrimeSystem.servingTime`: affection −0.55 a day and board pressure +4 a
week. Wave two's *Inside* lane replaces the middle of it with a place.

**Suing a rival** files from their profile: $8,000 of company money, the
same hearing machinery with `isFounderSuing`, evidence from how much they
have shipped. Win and `RivalSystem.crimeAwardDamages` pays
`strength × 90`, takes 8 reputation and the newest thing off their shelf.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **The offence buttons are in one room, not six.** The brief wanted them
   in the finance ledger, the hiring sheet, the war room's press strip and
   the launch flow. Every one of those files belongs to another lane this
   round and none of them carries an N1 marker, so rule 10 says a change
   there is a follow-up. They are gathered on `CrimeScreen` instead; the
   two that are about a *person* (the planted story, the suit) do live
   where they belong, on the rival's profile.
2. **There is no quarterly tax bill, and there never was.** The game has
   never posted one — its weekly operating cost has always been the whole
   of "what running this costs" — and adding one would have changed the
   ledger of every run ever recorded. So `FinanceSystem.crimeQuarterlyTaxBill`
   derives a *notional* bill from the trailing quarter's sales and contract
   revenue, shows the player the number they are about to lie about, and
   the **dodge** is what posts: one credit, labelled the way the accountant
   labelled it. Nothing is read or posted in a run that never dodges.
3. **Cooking the books lifts the offer, not `companyValuation`.**
   `GameState.companyValuation` is a shared function with no region of
   mine; a term sheet arriving while the accounts are warm is revised in
   place by `CrimeSystem.spendBooks` instead. Same fiction, no shared-file
   edit.
4. **Rivals have no named staff**, so the NDA poach mints a named senior
   *candidate* on the hiring desk (in `HiringSystem`'s region) rather than
   moving a person who does not exist.
5. **`EventPresenter` got one line of mine.** `EventCopy`'s switch has no
   N1 region and its `@unknown default` would have printed "Something
   happened" for fifteen events — including the newspaper's lead. One call
   to `CrimeEventPresenter.describe` sits in its own marked region at the
   top of `EventPresenter.describe`; all the words are in my own file.
6. **`Route.rivalSuit` and `-autoRoute suit`** were added (my AppRouter
   region) because no command line can name a rival's UUID, and the
   profile is where two of the six offences live.
7. **`FounderSkillSet.crimeValue(for:)`** duplicates `PitchRoom`'s
   internal `value(for:)` rather than widening M2's file.

## The screenshot pass

`-autoCase [offence]` commits one of the six — waiting, if the run cannot
yet afford it — turns the founder in so the case is listed rather than
rolled for, waits out the hearing, and walks in. `-autoLawyer <tier>`,
`-autoDefence <line>` and `-autoCourtSay deny,explain` dress the room.
Every step is a real `GameAction` through the ordinary reducer; there is
no back door into `state.crime`. `-autoRoute crime` / `courtroom` / `suit`
land on the three surfaces.

Two things a later lane should know: `-autoAnswer` only starts from HQ's
root task, so a pass that lands on Life never had it — `CrimeDebug` starts
it itself; and on a long wait it can stall on a beat whose first option it
cannot clear, so the crime pass answers with the *last* open option (the
passive one) while it waits.

   for money.

   reads oddly; the report needs an "inside" variant, which is wave two's
   *Inside* lane.

## Seams for wave two

- `CrimeState.notoriety` (0…100) and `CrimeState.cases` keep their names
  and meaning, as promised. `LegalCase` decodes every N1 field with a
  default, so a case raised by another lane's seam loads.
- **W1 (dirty money)** wants `CrimeSystem.commit` as the shape of an
  offence and `Crime.settlementPrice` for what a threat is worth; a
  laundering offence is a seventh `CrimeOffence` case plus a balance
  block, and the way out through the courtroom is
  `CrimeSystem.confess(entryID:)`.
- **W2 (family drama)** wants `LegalCase.isFounderSuing` for a custody
  hearing: the same room with a different opener and different verdict
  copy. `Crime.verdict` and `Crime.penalty` are pure and take the balance,
  so a second band table is a parameter, not a fork.
- **W3 (espionage)** wants `Crime.discoveryChance` and
  `Crime.evidenceWeight` — both pure, both taking a `CrimeRecordEntry` —
  and `CrimeSystem.raiseCase(against:)` for a counterintelligence hit.
  `state.crime.notoriety` is the shared currency.
- **W4 (inside)** wants `CrimeState.sentenceUntilDay`,
  `SabbaticalSystem.prisonAwayReason` ("Inside") and
  `CrimeSystem.servingTime`, which is the one place the sentence's daily
  cost is written.
