# N5 — The office has secrets

*Iteration 11, wave one. Branch `n5-secrets`, cut from `scaffold-11`.*

## What this lane is

Six slow-burn threads that run **behind** the founder rather than in front
of them. Each one starts on its own conditions, drops three clues the
player can actually read — a journal beat, a message from a third party, a
change in the room, a line in the ledger — and then does the thing it was
always going to do if nobody stops it. The founder's answers are six
actions on a card on the Team tab, each with its cost on the button and,
when it cannot be taken, the reason in place of the cost.

## The gate (identity)

`OfficeSecretsState.watching` is the first thing `OfficeSecretsSystem.run`
reads and the only thing it reads until it is true. The app sets it from
the Team tab (`.watchTheOffice`, sent from `TeamScreen`'s `.task`) and
nothing else does — the same shape `IncidentSystem` uses with
`hasOpenedProducts`. A pacing bot, a fixture replay and every headless
pass never switch tabs, so:

- `state.secrets` stays `.empty` and is never encoded,
- no roll, no read and no write in this lane ever runs,
- `rng`, `worldRNG`, `socialRNG` and `investorRNG` are untouched by it in
  every case — the weekly start roll and the picks inside a thread come
  from a **private stream** built in place from `state.seed`, the day and a
  per-purpose salt, and thrown away (`OfficeSecretsSystem.stream`).

The 42 new `office_*` company events are all `followUpOnly`, so the
company-event roll's eligible set — and therefore its weighted pick — is
byte-for-byte what it was. The six new `StaffEventKind`s have defs gated on
`office_confrontation`, a flag nothing ever raises, so the weekly staff
roll can never pick one either; they arrive only when the founder presses
*Say it to their face*, which raises the moment by `defID` through the
existing `StaffEvent` machinery (same sheet, same two answers, same
deadline).

## The six threads

| Kind | Starts when | Stage 1 clue | Stage 2 clue | Stage 3 clue | Ending if ignored |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **mole** | a rival exists, somebody is on payroll, and there is a topic worth selling | *journal* — a rival's changelog quotes a build number that never left the room | *office* — Sunday badge-ins with nothing shipped; **a shredder appears by the printer**; a colleague texts about it | *ledger* — two 02:40 taxis billed to a finished project | the strongest rival ships your topic (a real `RivalProduct` on their shelf, reputation −4, hype −12) and the mole walks |
| **romance** | two people on payroll with a bond ≥ 55 and different seniority | *journal* — three glowing reviews in the same handwriting | *office* — **two people at one desk**; a colleague mentions the shared car | *ledger* — an off-cycle raise signed on a Friday | the couple splits, the junior one leaves, everybody's morale −8 |
| **embezzlement** | five or more hired and cash ≥ 6× `embezzledPerStage` | *ledger* — unitemised expenses (real `LedgerEntry`, real cash) | *ledger* — four hotel nights in a city you have never sold in; **the shredder**; finance asks about "the offsite" | *ledger* — a supplier nobody can find, invoiced twice | a final write-off of 3× the stage amount, reputation −5, and they resign before you notice |
| **clique** | four or more hired and somebody hired inside 60 days | *office* — lunch leaves at half twelve without one person; **two at one desk** | *phone* — the new hire asks which channel they should be in | *journal* — their work was rewritten over a weekend, unmentioned | the newest hire leaves, morale −8, reputation −2 |
| **unionDrive** | six or more hired, a round closed inside a fortnight, average morale < 65 | *office* — **the meeting-room door shut** every Thursday at six | *office* — a card-signing form face down in the printer tray; **the shredder** | *ledger* — an advisor's invoice, misaddressed | recognised the hard way: everyone's salary +6%, morale +6, crunch is dropped, `office_union_recognised` set |
| **coup** | a co-founded run, the co-founder still here, five or more hired, and board pressure ≥ 30 or their bond ≤ 45 | *journal* — two board calls you were not on; **the closed door** | *office* — a deck called *Leadership options* left on the screen; a colleague was asked how they would vote | *journal* — the votes counted twice, and rounded up | **only in a co-founded run**: the vote goes through and the run ends `.oustedByBoard`. Anywhere else the count is short and the co-founder leaves |

At most one thread runs at a time, none before day 120, none under
headcount 4, each kind at most once per company, and 90 days between one
closing and the next being allowed. Stages are 12 days apart.

## The responses

| Response | Cost | What it does | Refused when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ask around** | one evening | names the people, pulls the next stage to two days out | no evening left this week |
| **Hire a PI** | $2,200 from the founder's own **wallet** | names them and lands every remaining stage at once | the wallet cannot cover it |
| **Say it to their face** | free | raises the kind's staff moment on the person at the front of the thread, through the existing sheet; closes the thread as *faced* | nobody is named yet |
| **Take it to HR** | free | ends it cleanly: team morale +4, the person being frozen out or reporting wrongly is moved | there is no People & HR department |
| **Make a deal** | $4,000 of company cash (mole, romance, clique), $2,000 (union), $8,000 (coup), nothing up front (embezzlement — they repay what they took) | the kind's own settlement: a counter-offer, a moved reporting line, a repayment plan, a bigger table, voluntary recognition, or a title and a slice with +12 board pressure | the company cannot cover it |
| **Leave it alone** | free | the ending arrives in three days, and the founder chose it | never |

Each is used at most once per thread, and a used one keeps a tick.

## Deviations and re-pins

1. **The clue props are drawn beside the room, not inside it.** The brief
   pointed at `OfficeSceneInput` in PixelKit's `OfficeSceneView.swift`, but
   PixelKit belongs to no lane this round and the office scene has snapshot
   coverage. The clues are instead a strip of pixel vignettes inside
   `OfficeCard`'s own marked region, above the room: the two people at one
   desk are PixelKit's own `SpriteLibrary.person` and `desk`; the shredder
   and the closed door are hand-drawn from the master palette's exact RGBA
   values (the ramps are `internal` to PixelKit, so they are copied
   verbatim rather than imported). **Follow-up:** move the three props into
   `OfficeSceneInput`/`OfficeDirector` so they stand in the room itself.
2. **One existing assertion re-pinned**, following the precedent already in
   the same test: `ContentVarietyTests.followUpsResolve` now unions
   `OfficeSecretsSystem.scheduledEventIDs` into `companyTargets`, exactly
   as it already unions `ChildhoodSystem.stageBeatIDs` for the life beats.
   No new test, no changed count.
3. **No test counts changed.** 943 engine, 52 content, 34 save, 385 app.
4. `fire with cause` is N2's; the endings and the confrontations use plain
   quits and the staff sheet's `noticeReason`, never a cause line.
5. The strip sits **above** the pixel room rather than below it, because a
   clue you have to scroll past the whole scene to find is not a clue.

## Seams for wave two's espionage (W3)

- **`SecretThread` is a generic slow-burn machine.** Kind, stage,
  `nextStageDay`, clues, `named`, props, `topicID`, `taken`, `usedResponses`
  and an ending — nothing in it is specific to a thread the *player's* staff
  are running. A rival running one against you is a new `SecretKind` case
  plus a `canStart`, a `cast`, a `beat` and a `finish` arm.
- **`OfficeSecretsSystem.stream(state:salt:)`** is the private stream every
  new draw should use. Add a salt, take what you need, throw it away.
- **`OfficeSecretsState.watching`** is the identity gate: reuse it rather
  than adding a second one, and the espionage lane inherits the guarantee
  that a bot never sees any of it.
- **`SecretResponse` + `OfficeSecretsSystem.refusal`/`respond`** are the
  counterintelligence menu already: a new response is a case, a refusal
  reason, an arm in `respond`, and a row that appears on the card with its
  cost. `SecretsCard` needs no structural change.
- **`OfficeSecretsSystem.leakRoadmap`** is a worked example of a rival
  shipping something without touching `worldRNG` — copy it for a bought
  roadmap or a hacked storefront.
- **`SecretKind.eventID(_:)`** gives the `office_*` id convention, and
  `scheduledEventIDs` is the one place a new suffix has to be listed for
  the content suite to accept it.
- **`SecretFlag`** (`office_union_recognised`, `office_secret_handled`) and
  the staff defs' `office_union_answered` / `office_coup_settled` are
  narrative flags content can gate on.
