# W4 — Inside

*Iteration 11, wave two. Branch `w4-inside`, cut from `scaffold-11b`.*

## What the founder now serves

N1's courtroom could already hand down a sentence, and until now that
sentence was a sabbatical nobody booked: the founder was away, a caretaker
ran the company, and the weeks passed off screen. This lane is what the
weeks are made of.

- A **full-screen mode** while the sentence runs, presented from
  `AppRootView`'s W4 region the way the incident room and the war room are
  presented — over whichever tab the player was on, at window level.
  Unlike the incident room the clock *keeps running*: the way out of this
  screen is time, so the room carries the speed control and the day counter
  rather than an hour button and a close button.
- **A day with a choice in it.** Five things to do with today; a day nobody
  chooses is a day with the head down. Each button prints exactly what it
  will spend and what it will buy, from the same pure function
  (`Prison.consequence`) the engine spends it with.
- **A cellmate**, drawn on `socialRNG` the morning the gate closes, who
  becomes an address-book `Contact` with the new `inmate` archetype and the
  rapport the weeks earned.
- **A wing to join or refuse**, which changes what the yard costs and what
  the parole board thinks of you.
- **A parole board** at the halfway mark, in the courtroom's grammar (it
  reuses N1's `CrimeBenchView` as it stands).
- **A wall**, once, with real odds and a doubled sentence if it does not
  work — and, if it does, a life on the run with a case that never closes.
- **38 `inside_` life events**, gated on flags no clean run ever sets.
- **A release sheet** that reads the caretaker's report as a release rather
  than as a postcard.

**Identity at the default.** `GameState.prison` is `nil` until a court
sends the founder down (or `-autoInside` serves a sentence in a debug
build), it is encoded only while non-nil, and every line of `PrisonSystem`
is reached from exactly one place: `CrimeSystem.servingTime`, which itself
returns on its first line unless `state.crime.sentenceUntilDay` is set. No
bot, no fixture replay and no save written before this existed reaches a
line of the lane. Draws are `socialRNG` only: three words for the cellmate
on arrival, one a day for whether the day went wrong, one per exchange at
the board, one for the wall.

## The day menu, with its meter effects

`Prison.meterEffect` and `Prison.consequence`; every number is
`"prison"` in `Balance.json` / `BalanceConfig+Prison.swift`.

| Today | Meters | Also | Can go wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep your head down | energy +1.2, mood −0.8 | nothing on your record | — |
| Work in the library | energy −0.5, mood +1.0 | a library day (parole credit +3.2 each); every 7 buys +1 finance on release | — |
| The yard | energy +0.6, health +1.4, mood +0.5 | wing standing +3.0, cellmate bond +1.6 | 10% base |
| The phone call home | mood +0.8, relationships +2.2 | affection +0.9, a message on the phone from a child or the partner | — |
| The deal | energy −1.0, health −0.6, mood +1.4 | wing standing +7.0, wallet +$120 | 28% base |

Under all five, every day: energy −0.3, health −0.35, mood −0.9,
relationships −0.55 (N1's `insideAffectionPerDay`, kept, because that is
what N1 wrote the sentence costs at home) and the partner's affection
−0.6. The board's patience still rises 4 a week (N1's number, applied from
my region in `servingTime`). A day that goes wrong is an infraction:
mood −6, health −4, a line in the log and on the phone.

The trouble chance is `base × gang × standing`: in with the wing multiplies
it by 0.45, out multiplies it by 1.6, and a full standing meter takes 60%
of what is left off. The yard at 10% base reads 3% once you are in and
have walked it a while.

## The gang

`Prison.gangName` picks one of five names off the day the sentence started
(no draw). On day 4 the cellmate says they would like a word, and the room
asks once:

- **In** — standing +22, the yard's trouble ×0.45, `inside_gang` raised
  (three life events read it), and a favour owed on the outside
  (`PrisonState.favourOwed`, which W1's demand machinery or a contact
  asking for a job can call in; see follow-ups).
- **Out** — standing −6 and the yard's trouble ×1.6 for the rest of it.
- Either way the board reads it: being in costs 10 points of parole
  standing.

## Parole

Listed the day the founder is halfway through, and heard once. The board
opens itself when it is listed (a hearing is not something a player can be
trusted to remember) and stops the clock while it sits.

Opening standing = `−6 − 13×infractions + 3.2×libraryDays +
1.6×callsHome − 4×dealDays − 10 (if in with the wing) + 40×(progress −
0.5)`, plus 6 for each of the three things the caretaker held together
(headcount, cash, a product shipped) — `Prison.caretakerCredit`, which is
the caretaker's report read as evidence.

Three exchanges out of five, each said once:

| Say | Graded on | Landed | Missed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Say you're sorry | conversation | +22 | −18 |
| Talk about the company | leadership | +18 | −14 |
| Show them the certificate | technical | +20 | −6 |
| Talk about your family | conversation | +16 | −9 |
| Let the folder speak | finance | +6 | −3 |

`landChance = 0.46 + skill/210 − 0.06×infractions`, plus 0.02 a library
day on the certificate and 0.03 a call home on the family, clamped
0.08…0.92. At 28 or better they let you out that day; below it the
sentence runs to its date and there is no second board.

## The wall

One attempt, ever. `chance = 0.16 + 0.22×(standing/100) +
0.008×libraryDays − 0.12×progress`, clamped 0.02…0.60 — so it is a thing
you do early, with people behind you, and the more of the sentence you have
already served the less anybody believes you meant to serve it.

- **It works**: `awayReason` becomes "On the run", the prison stays on the
  state with `onTheRun` set (the cover closes: the founder is outside), the
  sentence is cleared, notoriety +20, and a `LegalCase` with kind
  `"escape"` is listed for `day + 99,999` — a case that never closes,
  which also means no further offence can be committed while it stands
  (N1's `.casePending` gate). Four `inside_run` life events live out there.
- **It does not**: what is left of the sentence doubles (the away window
  and the caretaker's weeks are extended with it), two infractions, health
  −6 and mood −12.

## Release

Three doors out — served it, paroled, over the wall — all through
`PrisonSystem.release`, which clears `sentenceUntilDay`, keeps N1's
`.crimeReleased` event and its phone line, copies the caretaker's
`SabbaticalReport` onto `PrisonState.report` (the sabbatical slot gets
reused by the next trip), puts the cellmate in the address book, adds the
library's finance points, and writes a memory to every child's ledger.

The **"inside" report variant** is `Prison.releaseHeading`, which
`SabbaticalSystem`'s W4 region calls `prisonReportHeading`: the same
numbers, the same log, headed like a release ("*Nala kept the lights on* —
8 weeks inside. It cost the company money, and it is still here.") rather
than like a postcard. `InsideReleaseScreen` puts it over the caretaker's
before/after, the log of the weeks and the person who came out with you.

While a sentence runs, the sabbatical's own daily meters — the beach's
energy, health, mood and the partner's affection *climbing* — are
suppressed by the same region (`SabbaticalSystem.prisonHoliday`), because
a sabbatical the founder did not book is not a holiday. Everything else the
caretaker does is untouched: the weekly call, the log, the report.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **`NetworkingSystem` got one line of mine, and it had to.** The brief
   asks for a seventh `ContactArchetype`; `NetworkingSystem.rollContact`
   rolled its archetype over `ContactArchetype.allCases`, so a seventh case
   would have moved every venue roster in every run ever recorded — rule 1,
   broken by rule 12's own instruction. The rooms now roll over
   `ContactArchetype.partyKinds` (declared in *my* region of
   `Networking.swift`: the same six, in the same order); the change in
   `NetworkingSystem` is that one identifier, inside a W4 marker, with the
   reason written above it. `OriginTests` and the pacing bots are green,
   which is the proof.
2. **`state.prison` outlives the sentence.** The scaffold says the slot is
   `nil` unless the founder is inside. It is `nil` until the first
   sentence, and afterwards it stays with `releasedDay` set so the Life
   tab can show what the weeks came to and the biography has something to
   quote — the same shape `life.sabbatical` uses. Everything that gates on
   "the founder is in a cell" reads `prison.isInside`, including the
   full-screen cover, so the difference is invisible to every other lane.
3. **The report variant's words live in `Prison.swift`.**
   `SabbaticalSystem` is internal to the engine and the sheet that reads
   the heading is in the app, so `prisonReportHeading` in the W4 region
   there is a one-line forwarder to `Prison.releaseHeading`.
4. **`servingTime` delegates rather than being replaced in place.** My W4
   region is the first thing in the function: it calls `PrisonSystem.serve`
   and returns its events when there is a sentence, leaving N1's original
   body reachable only when there is not (which is never — it is the
   fallback for a save mid-sentence written before this lane). The two
   costs N1 wrote, the relationships slide and the board's patience, are
   applied from inside the region so nothing is charged twice.
5. **The room presents its own decision sheets.** The clock runs while the
   founder is inside, so life beats keep firing; `AppRootView` is *behind*
   the cover and cannot show a sheet over it, so `InsideScreen` presents
   `DecisionSheet` from the same `DecisionPrompt.pending` the root reads.
   Without this the room wedges on the first `inside_` beat, which is how
   it was found.
6. **`ChildMemory` kinds are strings.** The ledger is L3's file and
   `ChildMemoryKind` is theirs; N2 already writes free-string kinds
   (`people_warm`), so the lane writes `inside_away` and `inside_home` the
   same way rather than widening somebody else's enum.
7. **No `Route` for the sentence itself.** `-autoRoute inside` lands on the
   release sheet; the sentence is a mode, not a destination, and while it
   runs there is nothing to route to.

## The screenshot pass

`-autoInside <weeks>` serves a sentence through the ordinary reducer (a
DEBUG-only `.serveSentence`, which does exactly what a verdict does:
`sentenceUntilDay` plus `SabbaticalSystem.crimeBeginSentence`). Then
`-autoInsideDay <choice>` picks the same thing every morning,
`-autoInsideGang join|refuse` answers the wing when it asks, `-autoParole`
waits for the halfway mark and walks in, `-autoParoleSay a,b` says two
things to the board, `-autoEscape` goes over the wall, and
`-autoInsideScroll wing|out` points a headless pass at the half of the room
below the fold. Every one of them is a real `GameAction`.

Three things a later lane should know.

1. The day the gate closes is a critical event and it stops the clock, so
   a headless pass has to press play (the pass does; a player has the
   speed control in the room's own top bar).
2. Nothing may present itself into the cover's arrival animation — the
   room waits 700ms before it will show a sheet, and skipping that wedges
   the transition. This is how the room's own decision sheet was found to
   be needed at all.
3. **`-autoInside` is unreliable when a decision sheet is already up at
   launch.** The flag is sent from two places (the root's task and the
   Life tab's card, because the root's engine can still be the one the
   fixture is about to replace), and on a fixture that opens with a
   pending prompt neither fires. A pass that gets a clean launch works
   every time; one that lands on a poach offer never goes inside. The
   fix is probably a launch-time hook that runs after the session has
   settled, which is `DebugLaunch`'s territory, not mine.

## Follow-ups

1. **The favour owed is recorded and never called in.**
   `PrisonState.favourOwed` is set by joining the wing and nothing reads it
   yet. W1's `Demand` machinery is the right home for it ("a favour owed on
   release that lands as a `money_`-style demand"); failing that, the
   cellmate contact asking for a job.
2. **The escape case has a date on it, 99,999 days out.** `LegalCase`
   requires a `hearingDay` and N1's hearing clock hears anything whose day
   has arrived, so "never" is spelled as a very large number.
   `LegalCase.hearingDay: Int?` would say it properly, in N1's file.
3. **A pause inside the room has no reason line.** The notice rail lives
   under each tab's HUD, and the cover has neither. The player sees the
   clock stop and has to press play. The room should carry the rail.
4. **The cell is one room.** `HomeSpriteLibrary` could give it a proper
   pixel backdrop (a bunk, a door, a landing) instead of the flat wall,
   window and floor line this lane draws itself. That is N3's file's
   neighbour, not mine.
5. **Nothing else reads `inmate`.** An ex-cellmate should be a worse hire
   and a better fixer than the archetype currently implies; the hiring desk
   treats them as an operator.
6. **A second sentence overwrites the first.** The state holds one; a
   founder sent down twice keeps only the second one's log.
7. **`-autoInside` needs a settled launch** — see the screenshot pass
   above. Worth fixing when somebody touches `DebugLaunch`'s startup.
8. **A prompt can hide the board.** The room presents its own decision
   sheets and its own parole sheet, and iOS shows one sheet at a time; if
   a beat is open on the day the board sits, the board waits for the
   player to press the button. It should queue behind the beat instead.

## Seams for whoever comes next

- `PrisonState.isInside` is the one fact to gate on, and it is false the
  moment the founder is out however they got out.
- `Prison` is pure and public: `meterEffect`, `consequence`, `wrongChance`,
  `paroleOpeningStanding`, `caretakerCredit`, `paroleLandChance`,
  `escapeChance`, `releaseHeading`. Nothing in the app does arithmetic the
  engine does not.
- `PrisonSystem.beginSentence(weeks:)` is the whole of "send the founder
  down" for anybody who wants to: it sets `sentenceUntilDay` and hands the
  keys to the caretaker exactly as a verdict does.
- `PrisonSystem.servingFlag` / `servedFlag` / `gangFlag` / `runFlag` are
  the four narrative flags the `inside_` catalog is gated on.
