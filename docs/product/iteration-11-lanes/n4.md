# N4 — Fame and the feed

Branch `n4-fame`, cut from `scaffold-11`. No merge, no push.

## What this lane is

The founder now has a public feed. They post a take, a launch, an office
photo or a subtweet at a named rival; each post rolls a reach, brings in
followers, and collects replies from the world. Followers and recent reach
make fame; fame decays. Fame buys hype on every build, applicants nobody
asked for, a journalist who sits down warm, and — at each of five steps —
a book deal, a podcast, a keynote and a television slot. It also buys a
rival who answers back in public, and, once the founder is notable enough
to be worth the trouble, an old post surfacing with three bad ways out.

## The formulas

**Reach** — one `socialRNG` word, everything else is state:

    audience = reachBase + ceiling × f / (f + ceiling/perFollower)
    fameMult = 1 + fame/100 × reachFameSpan
    roll     = rollFloor … rollCeiling, uniform          (one word)
    reach    = audience × fameMult × kindMult × newsMult × roll
    viral    = roll ≥ viralRoll  →  reach × viralMultiplier

Shipped: `reachBase 420`, `reachPerFollower 0.6`,
`reachAudienceCeiling 25,000`, `reachFameSpan 3`, roll `0.45…2.0`,
`viralRoll 1.85`, `viralMultiplier 5`. Kind multipliers: take ×1,
launch ×1.35, photo ×0.75, subtweet ×1.7, reply ×1.1. `newsMult` is 1.3
for three days after an `.industryNews` beat and 1 otherwise.

**The audience saturates, and that is the balance argument of this lane.**
The first version was linear in followers, and the `-autoFame` pass hit
18.8 million followers inside two game years — every post buys followers,
which buy reach, which buy followers. The curve above has slope
`reachPerFollower` at zero (so the first hundred are as hard as they
look) and an asymptote at `reachBase + ceiling` (so the thousandth post
reaches barely more than the five-hundredth). The same pass now settles
around 130k after the same run, which is a very online founder rather
than a head of state.

**Followers** — `gain = reach × followersPerReach`, at 0.02. A
thousand-reach post is twenty people.

**Fame** — a slow function of both, approached and leaking:

    followerScore = min(75,  sqrt(followers) × 0.55)
    reachScore    = min(25,  reachInLast14Days / 3,500)
    target        = followerScore + reachScore
    fame'         = clamp(fame + (target − fame) × 0.12 − 0.07)

Ten thousand followers reads 55 on the follower half; the fortnight's
reach adds up to 25 more. The 0.07 daily leak is half a point a week, so
a founder who stops posting slides a level in about two months.

## What fame buys, at which level

| Level | Fame | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown | 0 | Nothing. Every multiplier in the game reads exactly 1. |
| Known | 8 | `fame_public` events. Hype: every build in development gains `fame × 0.05` a day — 0.4/day here, ~5/day at fame 100. |
| Followed | 20 | `fame_followed` events. One unasked-for applicant a week, +1 per 18 further points of fame, max 3, pool capped at 12. The podcast. |
| Notable | 38 | `fame_notable` events. The book deal. A journalist opens at `fame × 0.45` warmth (17 here, capped at 40). The cancellation roll starts: 10% a week, ×(1 + over/100 × 1.5), needing 12 posts on the record and a 12-week cooldown. |
| Famous | 58 | `fame_famous` events. The keynote. Two inbound applicants a week; journalist warmth 26. |
| A name | 78 | `fame_star` events. Television. Three applicants a week; journalist warmth 35. |

The hype, applicant, warmth and cancellation numbers are all exactly zero
at fame zero, which is what keeps the identity.

**The beef.** A subtweet at a named rival opens one; the rival answers
from `Feed.json`. Escalating buys `260 × round` followers and costs the
company `1.2 × round` reputation and the founder 3 mood; letting it go
costs the last word and returns 1.5 mood. Five rounds and the world stops
watching; a fortnight of silence ends it by itself.

**The cancellation.** Apologise: −12% followers, −4 fame, −1 reputation,
−6 mood. Double down: −28% followers, −2 fame, −5 reputation, −2 mood.
Delete: −20% followers, −8 fame, −3 reputation, −9 mood. Every price is
printed on its own button before it is pressed.

## Identity

- `FameSystem.run` returns on its first line while `state.fame == .empty`,
  which is every run in which nobody has pressed *Post*. `GameState`
  writes no `fame` key.
- Every `fame_*` life event gates on `flagsAll: ["fame_public"]` or a
  higher step's flag, and those flags are raised only by
  `FameSystem.raiseLevels`. `NarrativeSystem.rollLifeEvent` filters
  eligibility **before** the weighted pick and draws exactly one word
  either way, so 39 new defs change no draw in a run that never posts.
- No `rng` or `worldRNG` draw anywhere in the lane. `socialRNG` is
  touched only inside `post`, `answerBeef`, `fameInbound` and the weekly
  cancellation roll, all of which sit behind the empty-state gate.
- `NewspaperIssue.beefColumn` is `nil` in a week with no posts, so the
  front page is the page that shipped.
- 943 / 52 / 385 unchanged, including `OriginTests` and the pacing bots.

## Deviations and things left out

1. **`Rival.grudge` is N2's and is not on this branch.** A subtweet
   therefore opens the beef and prints the press line but does not raise
   a grudge. The follow-up is one line in `FameSystem.openBeef`, marked
   in place with a comment: `state.rivals.bumpGrudge(rival, by:)` once N2
   has merged. The brief anticipated this ("else a press line").
2. **No standalone screenshot of `FameCard`.** It sits at the bottom of
   the Life tab's *You* section and `simctl` cannot scroll; the card's
   content (the standing strip, the meter with its ticks, the waiting
   notices) is the top of `feed.png`. `fame-card.png` is the Life root
   with the pass running — the phone card shows the feed's own message
   ("You: Posted. 51,635 reached, 1033 new f…"), which is the card's
   neighbour.
3. **The newspaper's beef column is below the fold** in
   `newspaper-beef.png` — its heading and first lines are visible at the
   bottom of the page. The lead of that same issue *is* the beef
   ("COBALT ROW ANSWERED YOU IN PUBLIC"), which is the column's story
   told above the fold.
4. **`-autoCompose`** is a flag the brief did not ask for. A headless pass
   cannot tap *Say something* and the compose sheet is half of what this
   lane looks like; it is in the N4 region of `DebugLaunch` and consumed
   only by `FeedScreen`.
5. **`Fame.postBlocker` and `Fame.fill` are public wrappers** over
   `FameSystem`'s internal versions, because the compose sheet and the
   newspaper composer live in the app target.
6. The lane adds no tests, per `CLAUDE.md`. No count was re-pinned.
