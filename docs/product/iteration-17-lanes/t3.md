## What was built

**W1 — severance or cause.**

- `.fire(employeeID:payNotice:)` — `payNotice: Bool? = nil`. `nil` is the
  old free firing byte for byte (an optional keeps the synthesized
  encoding of the old case identical); the resignation sheet's "Let them
  go", S2's debug seed and every test keep it. With `true` the company
  pays `min(4, quarters served)` weeks of salary (`Notice pay: <name>`,
  payroll category) and the firing is refused when the cash is short
  (`severanceNoticeBlocker`). No room morale, the alumnus stays warm.
- For cause (the existing `fireWithCause` interaction) stays free and now:
  everyone who stays −4 morale; the name +6 and the alumnus lost as
  before; and when the person's morale was over 50, one `socialRNG` word
  on the tap: at 10% a wrongful-dismissal claim — a `LegalCase` of kind
  `wrongfulDismissal`, eight weeks of their pay to settle, heard at the
  courtroom's shortest listing, evidence = their morale (0.3–0.9). It runs
  on the courtroom's own machinery: `settle` pays it company-first;
  `deliver` hands it to `SeveranceSystem.deliverClaim` — found for you and
  nothing is paid, found against and the award is 1.5× the settlement.
  Civil either way: never a conviction, a sentence or a record flag.
- `.layOff(employeeIDs:)` — every picked person's notice, the room −2 a
  head capped at −10, reputation −1 per three, and a seated board watching
  headcount adds its review's miss step (weighted by the share of seated
  boards that watch headcount, never past oust − 1). Refused while the
  notice total is more than the cash.
- **The layoff sheet** (`Screens/Team/LayoffSheet.swift`, pixel panel,
  paper rows, pixel button): tick people newest first, each row with level,
  weeks here, pay, morale and their notice; "What it costs" / "What it
  saves" / "Or" (the with-cause alternative) totalled live; the button
  prints "Let 6 go for $0" and what it closes. Reached from the Team tab's
  new *Let people go* row (with "6 over the Loft's desks" when the move
  down is refused) and from a *Choose who goes* link under S2's refusal,
  which opens the sheet with the number to go and the reason.
- **Two priced answers** everywhere the player fires one person: the manage
  sheet's new *Letting them go* section, the Team tab's swipe dialog, and
  the People menu's "Let them go" row (terms now print the notice).

**J5 — the lesson's price.**

- Under every "X teaches Y …" line (the move preview, the office move
  mode, the Desk section): Y's fair pay a quarter out, Y's place on the
  recruiters' list now → then (the poach score term for term, including
  K3's options and S1's door), and "By then Y reads as senior: expect them
  to ask for the title" when the quarter's lesson crosses a rung.
- The mechanical half: in `SeatingSystem.teach`, a lesson that carries a
  student past the bar of the rung above their title schedules the

  `SocialSystem.isEligible` lets a seated person whose skills read above
  their title ask whatever their tenure. Both read `seatingIsSet`, empty
  on every bot and fixture. No draw.

## Deviations from the spec

- `claimChance` 0.1, not 0.2 — the spec's own remedy (measured above).
- The hearing is listed at `crime.hearingWeeksMin` weeks rather than drawn
  in the 4–8 week band, so the claim costs exactly one `socialRNG` word.
- The claim's loss in court is 1.5× the settlement (`claimLostFactor`) —
  the spec says "settled or fought" without pricing the fight.
- The promotion ask's gate is "seated, skills a rung above the title",
  not "currently a student": `isEligible` has no balance to recompute the
  lessons. The ask itself is only scheduled by a lesson that crosses.
- "Promote them" on `promotionDemand` still means +18% salary, morale and
  loyalty (the content's own outcome), not a level change; unchanged.
