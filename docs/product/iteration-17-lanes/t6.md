## What was built

**G3: people away from the desk.**
- *Send on a course* sits on the manage sheet beside the instant workshop, one row per skill.
  - The button prints the price, "away 10 days" and the skill gain.
  - The footer names the build the person is on and the ship date it moves to ("no later than 18 Feb instead of 10 Feb"). It adds: "The skill stays; so does the fair pay it earns them — expect the raise talk."
- The course costs $2,400 × `hrTrainingCostFactor` ($1,680 with People & HR). The person is away 10 days and comes back with **+9** in the chosen skill (see Measurements). Fair pay rises with the skill, so `raiseRequest` follows.
- While away, a person produces no points and no research and does not grow. They are not counted for product or contract crowding, or in a promoted lead's span. Their morale target is +2.
- Around the team:
  - The build card and the Now card print "Sofia away until 14 Mar" under the crew line.
  - `ShipETA` and `BuildETA` leave the absent person out.
  - The Team row shows an *AWAY* chip.
  - The feed says who left, who came back, and from what.

**G3: the holiday question.**
- `holiday_request` is appended to `StaffEvents.json`, with a new rolled kind `StaffEventKind.holidayRequest = "holiday_request"`.
- It is doors-gated in content: its def requires the `holiday_askable` flag, which `AwaySystem` raises only while `doors.armed`. No bot's staff stream can ever roll it.
- The two answers:
  - *Ten days a year, take them* sets the policy. Every non-founder is away 10 days from each hiring anniversary (never in their first year). Everyone's morale target is +4. Everyone the rule answers for gets +8 loyalty, once; that includes later hires, who are added to the policy's beneficiaries, which is also what reversing the rule costs.
  - *Take them when it's quiet*, made the rule with the existing "…and make that the rule" answer: nobody is away, everyone's morale target is −3, and `burnoutWarning` weighs ×1.5.
- Both rules land on the policies card with their day and who asked. The card adds a line: "Next away: Priya 17–26 Mar · … · morale target +4 for everyone", or the strict rule's costs. Reversal works at the card's usual price, unchanged.

**J3: the launch without its founder.**
- On a ship day with `life.isAway` **and doors armed**, `hypeAtLaunch` is ×0.85. The ship logs `.launchWhileAway`.
- The vice week counts that launch as a party nobody threw. It is never counted twice with K7's kept diary date.
- The launch-day sheet says "You were away for this one. Hype ×0.85, and no party."
- Two away rows print the clash from the ETA before the tap: the vacation and family-holiday cells on the weekend card, and the sabbatical's length card. The line reads "Round 16 ships on day 4 of the sabbatical · hype ×0.85, no party".

**J6: homes that read the map.**
- `home.farPairs` gains `["midtown", "oldTown"]`; no pair was removed.
- A school-age child with a **Midtown** home gains +1 bond a week and, once, the memory "walks to school past the park". This is the spec's remedy; see Measurements.
- The move sheet gains a third column, "Nearby":
  - Old Town: the co-working mixer
  - Suburbs: the hacker house
  - Midtown: the conference bar and the park on the school run
  - Tech Park: demo day
  - Downtown: the rooftop party
- The city map's district panel prints "A home here is a walk from …". When tonight's room is in the founder's home district, it adds "Tonight's room is a walk from home."

## Measurements (the "how it fails" checks, measured first)

All measurements came from a throwaway SwiftPM executable in the scratchpad, not a test and not committed. It used the shipped balance.

**M1 — does any pacing seed ship while the founder is away?** Yes. I ran the ten pacing seeds × every shipped bot (solo-slow, crunch-hire, saas-builder, neglectful, contract-grinder, ship-fast, balanced, investor, acquirer, buyback) for 730 days:

| balance | ships | while the founder was away | reasons |
|---|---|---|---|
| rivals off (the pacing gates) | 2,399 | 53 | 48 hospital, 4 burnout, 1 family emergency |
| shipped | 1,530 | 42 | 37 hospital, 2 burnout, 3 family emergency |

So the factor, the event and the vice read are **gated on `doors.armed`**, as the K1 rescue is. Bots never plan a vacation (0 vacation days in 200 runs), so the PM's rate check ("ship days inside a vacation, under one per run") cannot be read from bots.

On the studio fixture, 14 launches in 400 days is one about every 28 days. A 7-day week away then catches a ship about 25% of the time. A player who takes a week off each quarter would meet about one clash a year, about two per two-year run. That is above the PM's "under one per run" line, so the hype factor stays. This is an estimate, not a bot measurement.

**M2/M5 — the course on `release-studio-day400`: every builder sent in week one.** Ten builders were sent, costing $16,800.

| | Round 16 gate | Round 17 gate | weekly points the week they are back |
|---|---|---|---|
| no course | day 403 | day 404 | 232.7 |
| course, +12 (spec), real action | day 413 (+10) | day 414 (+10) | 252.3 (**+8.4%**) |
| course, +9 (shipped) | day 413 (+10) | day 414 (+10) | 248.0 (+6.5%) |

- A simulation before building (idle for 10 days, then the skill added) read +7.7%. The real feature reads +8.4%, and the difference is the +2 morale target while away.
- +8.4% is over the PM's 8% line, so I applied the remedy, **+9**.
- The other remedy, fifteen days, keeps +12 and simulates +7.5% for a 15-day slip. I did not pick it because it lengthens the slip without changing the gain much.
- The course at +9 is still worth more than the workshop's +6, and it now costs a 10-day slip.

**M3/M6 — do scheduled holidays land inside a ship window on the fixture?**

## Deviations (the repo won, or a number was measured)

- **Course boost is +9, not +12**, measured as above.
- **"Ten days a year, take them", not "Four weeks a year".** The spec's own schedule is ten days (`holidayDays 10`), and the button must print the true number.
- **The networking room costs no evening today.** `NetworkingSystem.talk` and the weekend spend none, so there is nothing to waive. The district panel prints "a walk from home" instead. S4's *Visit* row in `CityWhoIsHere.swift` is not mine and is unchanged.

- **The launch-day sheet opens at ship time, as it always has, not "when they are back".** The sentence is on it.
- **Days away are engine days.** Output runs every day; there are no working days. A course or a holiday booked on day D covers D+1…D+10, and the person is back on D+11.

- **The first holiday comes at the first hiring anniversary**, so a person hired after the rule is set waits a year.
- **The course preview on the manage sheet** projects at today's crew rates and ignores the boost, so it reads "no later than".

**The content gate leaves the staff roll byte-identical for bots** (the coordinator's check). There are three pieces of evidence:
- **The order of operations.** `SocialSystem.pickKind` builds `eligible` from `content.staffEvents` with `StaffEventKind(rawValue:) != nil && isEligible(...)` (lines 271–274). Only then does it sum the weights (line 291) and draw one word. Without `holiday_askable`, `isEligible` fails on `flagsAll`, so the holiday def is filtered out *before* the draw rather than weighted after it. The def is also last in `StaffEvents.json`, so even an eligible holiday def could not reorder the others.
- **A before/after run (M8).** The ten pacing seeds × every shipped bot (730 days, rivals off) ran once with the bundled catalog (34 staff defs) and once with `holiday_request` removed (33). **100 of 100 final states are byte-identical** as sorted-key JSON; the runs logged 237 staff moments in their capped logs.
- **The pinned fixtures.** The engine suite passes unchanged, including the committed fixtures (`FixtureGenerator`'s scaffold5 garage fixtures, `ReleaseFixtureGenerator.theCommittedFixturesStillDecodeAndStillMatchTheirBriefs`, `FullLoopDeterminismTests`).
