## What was built

**The expo (G2).** A show called DevWorld on day 182 of every year
(`Expo.day(year:)`, the ceremony's arithmetic: `(year − 1) × 364 + 182`).
From 28 days before it the queue carries `QueueKind.expo` ("Expo in N days",
notable, a room — the rail's *Answer* lands on HQ) and the Now card shows a
row: the date, the booth and hallway prices, the crash line, and *Plan the
expo*. The sheet (`Screens/Business/ExpoSheet.swift`, pixel paper) prints
the poster — what a booth buys, the crash at 25 bugs, "the copycat is ready
3 weeks after launch, not 8, and reviewers expect +3", who goes and what it
costs — then every build in development with its quality so far and open
bugs (a crash warning over 25, "the press's nod" from 60), *You* or *A
marketer*, and the answers with the price now and what it closes later:
*Take a booth · $15,000* (loft $2,500 / studio $7,500 / campus $15,000; the
garage has no booth), *Work the hallway · $900*, *Skip it*.

A booth is paid when booked and shown on the day (`MarketingSystem.runExpo`)
with whatever the build is by then; until the day it can be pointed at
another build or person for free ("Show Round 51 instead · free"). On the
day: hype `30 × TraitEffects.campaignHypeFactor × (0.7 if a marketer
pitches) × (0.5 for the hallway or an unstaffed stand)`, halved again by a
crash (open bugs > 25); reputation +3 when quality so far ≥ 60, −3 on a
crash (both × the hallway's 0.5). The founder at the stand spends that
day's evening and 8 energy; if the founder has no evening left or the
marketer has gone, the stand is unstaffed and works as the hallway. Either
way the build is public: `Product.expoDay` is written, the copycat reads it
where it reads `announcedDay` (`RivalSystem.announceRipeDay`), and its
review expectation rises +3 at ship. `GameState.expo` keeps the last year
shown or skipped and the booking. The `conference_booth` event is untouched.

**Pre-orders (G6 + P4).** On an announced one-time build at least 21 days
from its date, the *Ship date* card's row under the date offers *Open
pre-orders · +$2,478 now* (the garage fixture's Quiet 1), with a sheet that
prints the forecast's launch, the slice sold now and its price, the slips'
refunds in dollars and the quality the buyers are sold on. Cash lands now
and a ledger line posts. The launch weeks deliver them: each sales week's
first buyers are the pre-orders still owed, so the week's units stand and
its revenue counts only the rest (`AnnounceSystem.deliverPreorders`, one
negative ledger line a week until they are out). J5's first slip refunds a
third of them (`refundPerSlip` 0.34) in cash on the same day as its −4; the
void refunds the rest and costs −4 more. Once per product. The launch-day
sheet prints what went out and, when the reviews land under the quality the
pre-orders were sold on, "Overpromised: they were sold a 72. The press gave
it 64." Subscriptions refuse ("Nobody pays a year up front…"); annual
prepay is a follow-up.

## Measurements first

All from a scratch SwiftPM executable over this branch's packages (the real
reducer, the shipped balance; not a test, not committed): J5's harness for
G6 (30 seeds × 3 years × three crew shapes), the release fixtures for G2
and P4.

### G2 — the expo against a launch event, `release-studio-day400`

The fixture's three builds ship 18–29 days after day 400, so the expo's 30
is applied at day 400 (closer than the spec's six weeks, which only makes
it stronger); the launch event's 40 a week before each ship. No marketer on
payroll, so the trait factor is 1.00.

| Build | Hype at launch: expo / event | Launch-week units per $1,000: expo ($7,500) / event ($5,000) | 26-week revenue per $1,000: expo / event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 15 (productivity) | 20.9 / 34.7 | +2.4 / +6.8 | +$80 / +$242 |
| Round 16 (travel) | 16.7 / 34.7 | +0.5 / +2.0 | +$14 / +$38 |
| Round 17 (music) | 18.5 / 34.7 | +0.9 / +3.0 | +$37 / +$104 |

**The expo does not win per dollar** (about a third of the launch event's
return on every build), so hype stays at 30 — the spec's 30 → 20 remedy is
not triggered. Neither buy pays back in 26 weeks on this fixture ($7,500
for +$598 at best); the expo's value is the reputation (+3 for a build at
60) and that it is open from the loft, where the launch event is not, and
to the garage's hallway.

The review expectation +3 never bound on the fixture: a variant with the
hype only (no `expoDay`) produced identical reviews, because the studio's
builds sit above what the press expects.

**The copycat's window never cost share.** The copycat launches after each
ship were identical in every variant (finance at +89…+100 days, music at
+138…+149, the same rival launches in topic) at four weeks — so the spec's
remedy applies: `expo.copycatDelayWeeks` 4 → 3. Re-run at three weeks, the
lists are still identical: the copycat targets the player's *best-reviewed*
ripe product, and on this fixture that is an older hit, not the build just
shown. Follow-up 7.

### G6 — the slip rate of announced builds (J5's harness)

Each build announced at 25% or 50% of the way to its gate, at the sheet's
usual slack (+14), with pre-orders opened the same day.

| Crew | Announced at | Ships at the gate on the date: slipped / void | Ships only when finished: slipped / void |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo founder | 25% | 3% / 0% (265) | 99% / 98% (163) |
| Solo founder | 50% | 3% / 0% (266) | 100% / 99% (174) |
| Crew of 3 | 25% | 4% / 0% (336) | 90% / 50% (230) |
| Crew of 3 | 50% | 1% / 0% (311) | 96% / 65% (206) |
| Crew of 6 | 25% | 7% / 0% (60) | 93% / 85% (59) |
| Crew of 6 | 50% | 0% / 0% (60) | 98% / 86% (58) |

A founder who keeps the promise by shipping at the gate slips 0–7% of the
time — **under 10%, so the price falls 0.8 → 0.65** (the spec's remedy).
Waiting for the finished build slips almost always (J5's own finding: the
date is the gate, not the finished build), which is where pre-orders bite:
the void refunds everything and costs −4 on top of J5's −8.

### P4 — what pre-orders net on the garage

With G6's base as written — 30% of the forecast's *launch week* — the
garage fixture's Quiet 1 took **$80** (50 units at $1.59 of a 168-unit
week) against an $11,750 wallet; J5's harness medians were $57–$80 against
wallets of $19,000–$29,000. **Under P4's $500 line: decorative.** The
repo is why: the adoption ramp spreads a launch over `8 − marketing/16 −
hype/25` weeks, so week 0 is about a seventh of the peak, and 30% of a
seventh of a $1.99 app is nothing.

P4's named remedies (read followers; loft only) do not fix a base that is
decorative at every tier, so the units read (`launchWindowEstimate`) sums
the forecast's launch **window** — the weeks until the ramp peaks, which is
exactly week 0 when the ramp is one week (the old instant peak) — at the
**finished build's** quality (`ShipForecast.crewCeiling`, "the best this
crew can make"), because that is what pre-orders sell; today's forecast
reads a half-built score (27 on Quiet 1, 37 days out). Re-measured:

| | Quiet 1, garage fixture | Solo founder (harness) | Crew of 3 | Crew of 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-order cash | **$2,478** (1,916 units at $1.29 of a 6,388-unit, 7-week window) | median $2,189 | $1,644 | $2,584 |
| Against the wallet | $11,750 (21%) | $26,693 | $23,122 | $21,165 |
| First build of each run (the garage) | — | $2,596 of $19,168 | $2,584 of $19,168 | $2,584 of $19,168 |
| Weeks of sales to deliver them | — | median 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Launches "overpromised" when shipped at the gate | — | 100% of 265 | 92% of 311 | 98% of 60 |

With a $500 press release's hype, Quiet 1's pre-orders are $2,741 — hype
moves them a little, the forecast's quality most. Over P4's $500 line at
every tier, and at the garage — "the garage's whole problem is cash before
launch" — about a fifth of the wallet. The overpromised row is the join
J5 predicts: a founder who keeps the date ships at the gate, rough, and the
reviews land under the finished build the buyers paid for. It is a line on
launch day and nothing else (P4's −3 reputation would fall on nearly every
promise-keeper); the rate for builds shipped finished was not measured.

## Deviations (the repo wins, or the lead's scope)

- **`company.lastExpoYear` is `GameState.expo`** (`ExpoState { lastYear,
  booking }`): `Company` is T7's this round and the scaffold's top-level slot
  is mine. Encoded only once the player books or skips.
- **Booked in the window, shown on the day.** The spec's sheet "on the day"
  cannot work at 4× without a pause, and no engine event may pause on the
  default path; so the booth is booked any day in the 28-day window (paid
  then, re-pointed free) and the demo reads the build on the day — which is
  the spec's "a build decision three weeks out".
- **The garage has no booth** (the `conference_booth` event is loft+ too);
  the hallway is open to everyone.
- **The copycat read is in `RivalSystem+Announce.swift`** (`announceRipeDay`,
  one T5 pair) — J5's extension, not T4's `RivalSystem.swift`; no line of
  T4's file changes.
- **Delivery lives in `AnnounceSystem`** (my file, after `postWeeklySales`
  on the same day), so T2's `ship` carries only one T5 pair — the review
  expectation +3 — not the delivery.
- **"They count for standing and the judge"**: standing pays on the launch
  fee and the review, and `AwardsJudge` reads review scores; units enter
  neither, so there was nothing to wire. The weeks' units stand as sold.
- **Expo events do not pause** (`.info` by the severity switch's default);
  a crash leads the paper instead (`NewspaperComposer` T5 block: crash
  +25, a demo +15, a pre-order void +20).
