# L5 — A side project that is yours

Branch `l5-sideproject`, from `scaffold-9`. Five tracks, four chapters
each, one at a time, paid for in evenings.

## What I built

**Engine**

- `SideProject.swift` (mine, reshaped). `SideProjectTrack` (novel, band,
  marathon, weekendApp, restaurant) with a display name and the verb its
  button uses. `SideProjectDriver` — the seven things a track can run on
  (the five founder attributes plus `health` and `energy`), resolved
  against `LifeState` in one place. `CompletedSideProject` (track +
  `finishedDay`). `SideProjectState` grew `completedTracks`,
  `lastSessionDay` and `sessionsThisProject`, and its `track` became
  optional so the *record* of what the founder made survives finishing or
  abandoning a project — the state is non-`nil` forever once anything has
  happened, `track` is `nil` between projects. Hand-written Codable,
  every field `decodeIfPresent`, and `completedTracks` /
  `sessionsThisProject` are only *encoded* when non-empty, so a state that
  never engaged encodes exactly as it always did.
- `GameState` queries: `sideProjectChapter`, `sideProjectSessionValue`,
  `sideProjectSessionsLeft`, `sideProjectWorkBlocker`,
  `sideProjectStartBlocker`, `sideProjectSessionConsequence` (the string
  the button wears: `−1 evening · −$40 → $2,110 · −6 energy · +5 mood`).
- `Balance/BalanceConfig+SideProject.swift` (new): `SideProjectBalance`
  with `TrackDef` / `ChapterDef`, the shipped catalog, the attribute curve
  and the marathon's gym knobs, plus the `KeyedDecodingContainer` overload
  that makes the `"sideProject"` key optional. Wired into `BalanceConfig`
  as an inline-defaulted stored property (the `codebase` / `origins`
  precedent — an extension cannot add one), inside a
  `// MARK: L5 (side project)` region I added.
- `Systems/SideProjectSystem.swift` (new): `start` / `work` / `abandon`,
  their three blockers, `settle` (pays every chapter the progress crossed,
  shared by the evening and the marathon's daily creep), `pay`, and a
  daily `run` that returns on its first line unless a marathon is under
  way. Plus `SideProjectState.biographyLines(balance:)`.
- Markers: three cases in `GameAction`, one system line and three handler
  arms in `Reducer`, all inside the L5 regions.

**App** — `App/Sources/Screens/Life/SideProject/`

- `SideProjectCard.swift` — the card in the *You* section. Three states:
  never started (an invitation and the five track icons), under way (the
  pixel vignette, the chapter strip, tonight's button with its price on
  it, the evening pips, "about N more evenings to finish this chapter, at
  how good you are right now"), finished-and-idle (what they made, and the
  offer of the next one). Also carries `SideProjectWorkButton`, which is
  the shape every action on the tab has: title, consequence, and the
  refusal in warning orange when there is one.
- `SideProjectScreen.swift` — pushed from the card. Under way: the big
  vignette, what the track runs on, the evening button, the four chapters
  as rows (done / current / ahead) each with its blurb, its evening count
  and its payout chips, and *Put it down* behind a confirmation that
  states the deal. Idle: the five tracks as cards, each stating its whole
  cost in evenings, its drivers, and what its last chapter pays — before
  a single evening is committed.
- `SideProjectPresentation.swift` — icons, the scene lookup, `driverSummary`
  / `costSummary` / `payoutChips`, `SideProjectChip`, `ChapterStrip`.
- `SideProjectDebug.swift` — `-autoSideProject <track>`. It *plays*: it
  sends `.startSideProject` once and then `.workOnSideProject` on a loop,
  refused by exactly the gates a player meets. No state is written behind
  the reducer's back.
- Markers: `Route.sideProject` + its tab in `AppRouter`, the route name
  and the flag in `DebugLaunch`, the destination / card / destination-switch
  / two route-consumer arms in `LifeScreen`, and in
  `FounderBiographyView.lifeCard` a `// MARK: L5` region that is one
  computed property calling `biographyLines` and a `ForEach` over it.

## The catalog numbers

Attribute curve `attributeStrength: 0.6` (×0.7 at zero, ×1.3 at a hundred,
centred on `founder.skillMidpoint`, so an untrained founder needs exactly
the evening counts below), `minFactor: 0.4`. Marathon creep
`gymProgressPerDay: 0.02` while a gym session is within `gymWindowDays: 4`
or the gym is the standing plan.

| Track | Runs on | Evenings | $/night | Chapters (evenings) | Finish pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Novel | Conversation | 20 | free | 4 / 6 / 5 / 5 | $4,000 · +4 rep · **Press Contacts** · +12 mood · +4 rel |
| The Band | Conversation + Leadership | 19 | $40 | 3 / 5 / 5 / 6 | $1,500 · +3 rep · +14 mood · +6 rel |
| The Marathon | Health | 18 | free | 3 / 5 / 6 / 4 | −$120 entry · +1 rep · +10 health · +16 mood · +5 rel |
| The Weekend App | Technical | 17 | $60 | 3 / 4 / 5 / 5 | $9,000 · +2 rep · **Talent Magnet** · +10 mood |
| The Restaurant | Market Sense + Finance | 20 | $220 | 4 / 5 / 5 / 6 | $18,000 **or** −$9,000 · +2 rep · +4 mood |

Two perks, both real `ProgressionPerk` ids, both earned over ~20 evenings.
Money in the wallet, reputation on the company, meters on the founder;
mid-track chapters pay small versions of the same. The restaurant is the
money track: it takes $220 a night and $2,000 for the lease before it pays
anything, and its last chapter is the lane's only random draw — one
`socialRNG.nextUniform()` at `upsideChance: 0.55`, reachable only by a
founder twenty evenings deep.

Every chapter posts one line to `.office` and one to `.partner` on the
phone. Eleven of the twenty chapters also carry a press headline, posted as
`.industryNews` — flavour only, changes nothing.

## Identity at the default

`life.sideProject` is `nil` for every fresh game, every old save and every
pacing bot. `SideProjectSystem.run` returns on its first guard;
`BalanceConfig.sideProject` is never read; nothing encodes. `swift test`
in TycoonEngine is unchanged at 943, including the balance-target and
pacing suites. No new `rng` / `worldRNG` draws; the one `socialRNG` draw is
described above. No new tests anywhere, and no existing expectation re-pinned.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **No new `GameEvent` cases.** `GameState.swift` reserves an
   iteration-9 region for L2 only, and every alternative meant editing
   `EventCopy`, `EventPresenter`, `severity` and the newspaper composer —
   all unowned. So a chapter's press line reuses `.industryNews` (already
   "flavour only, never pauses, changes nothing", which is the right
   weight), and everything else surfaces through `PhoneState.post` and the
   card. `work` / `start` / `abandon` return `[]` or a single
   `.industryNews`.
2. **`SideProjectSheet` is `SideProjectScreen`, pushed not sheeted.** The
   scaffold reserved an L5 case in `LifeScreen.LifeDestination` and in the
   `navigationDestination` switch, and `-autoRoute sideproject` is a route;
   both say push. The card is still the entry point.
3. **`completedTracks` is `[CompletedSideProject]`, not `[String]`.** It
   carries the day, which is what lets the biography say *when* ("in year
   1") and the Finished list show a day.
4. **`SideProjectState.track` is optional.** The doc's field list assumed a
   project is always current; a record that outlives the project is the
   point of the feature.
5. **No new PixelKit sprites.** PixelKit is not in my ownership row, and
   the palette-baseline test guards it. Each track names an existing
   `ActivitySceneStyle` in its catalog entry, and all five are distinct:
   novel `hobby` (the desk by candlelight), band `friends` (people in a
   room), marathon `gymSession`, weekend app `cinema` (a lit screen in the
   dark), restaurant `restaurant`. The scene name is a balance field, so a
   later lane that draws real track art only has to change the catalog.
6. **`BalanceConfig.swift` got one stored property.** It is not in my
   ownership row and has no L5 marker, but Swift extensions cannot add
   stored properties and the brief says to wire the block in. It is five
   lines inside a `// MARK: L5 (side project)` region I added next to
   `origins`, so a merge conflict with L6's identical need is trivial.
