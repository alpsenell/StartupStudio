# L6 — The sabbatical

Branch `l6-sabbatical`, cut from `scaffold-9`. The founder hands the company
to somebody they trust, disappears for four to twelve weeks, and watches it
from the phone.

## What is in it

### Engine

`Packages/TycoonEngine/Sources/TycoonEngine/Sabbatical.swift` (owned, rewritten)

- `SabbaticalState` — the trip that is running, or the last one that ran.
  Keeps the scaffold's `caretakerID` / `sinceDay` / `untilDay` and adds
  `caretakerName`, `weeks`, `weeklyCost`, the opening `SabbaticalSnapshot`,
  the decision `log`, the `started` / `shipped` / `hired` / `lost` lists,
  `lastDecisionDay`, `endedDay` and the `report`. `isActive` is
  `endedDay == nil`; **nothing in any system reads the slot once it is
  false**, so a finished sabbatical is a memory, not a modifier. Every key
  but the scaffold's three decodes if present, so old saves load.
- `SabbaticalSnapshot` — cash, reputation, headcount, average morale,
  products live, taken the morning the founder leaves and the morning they
  come back.
- `SabbaticalLogEntry` (ordinal-identified, no UUIDs) and `SabbaticalReport`
  with a `headline` the card and the biography can quote.
- `SabbaticalEffects` — the two dials that reach other systems, both exactly
  neutral when nobody is away.
- Queries on `GameState`: `isOnSabbatical`, `sabbaticalCandidates`,
  `tenureWeeks`, `caretakerBlocker(_:balance:)`,
  `sabbaticalBlocker(weeks:balance:content:)`. The Life tab's greyed rows
  and disabled button read these, so the button and the engine cannot
  disagree about a rule.

`Systems/SabbaticalSystem.swift` (owned, new) — runs in the L6 region of
`Reducer.systems`, last but for L7's slot:

1. **Recovery** while away: energy, health, mood and (if there is a partner)
   affection each day. The founder's output is already zero — the trip sets
   the engine's existing `awayUntilDay` / `awaySinceDay` / `awayReason`
   (`"Sabbatical"`), so the office and home scenes, `EmployeeSystem`'s
   "founder has not been seen" morale hook and every `life.isAway` check
   behave exactly as they do for a hospital stay.
2. **The caretaker's week** — one decision at most every
   `decisionIntervalDays` (7), chosen by their revealed traits:
   - a **Flight Risk** may take the call they have been half-listening to
     (`flightRiskLeaveChance`, 0.12 per decision) — they leave the roster
     through the ordinary departure path (`.employeeQuit`,
     `SocialSystem.friendDeparted`, `NetworkingSystem.departed(reason:
     .poached)`) and the founder flies home that day;
   - **ship** a build that clears the founder's own code gate times
     `shipFactor` (1.15) — a **Speedster** uses `speedsterShipFactor` (1.0)
     and gets it out of the door as soon as it compiles;
   - **hire** the best candidate (`hireChance` 0.2 per decision) when the
     office has room and cash covers `hireRunwayWeeks` (26) of the new
     payroll;
   - **start the next build** when the bench is idle — one of the three
     best type × topic pairings, picked with one `socialRNG` draw;
   - **train** whoever is furthest behind (a **Mentor**'s first instinct;
     a Mentor also teaches on top of the course, `mentorSkillGain`);
   - otherwise **praise** the quietest person, which is free and is what a
     caretaker mostly does.
   A **Grumbler** in charge adds `grumblerMoralePerDay` (−0.15) to everybody
   else's morale, every day — no decision needed, that is the point of them.
3. **The phone** — one line a day to `.office` through
   `PhoneState.post(_:from:day:)`: the decision when there was one, and
   otherwise a deterministic "weather" line keyed off the day (cash, morale,
   days left), never a draw.
4. **The return** — `finish` seals the trip on `untilDay` (`LifeSystem`
   raises `.founderBack` in the same tick; this system does not duplicate
   it), writes the closing snapshot and the `SabbaticalReport`, and posts a
   last line to the phone.

Actions in the L6 regions of `GameAction` / `Reducer.apply`:
`.startSabbatical(caretakerID:weeks:)` and `.endSabbaticalEarly`.

`Balance/BalanceConfig+Sabbatical.swift` (new) — `SabbaticalBalance`, wired
into `BalanceConfig` with an inline default inside a `// MARK: Iteration 9 —
L6` region (so `"sabbatical"` stays an optional key) plus the
`KeyedDecodingContainer` overload. The block is also written out explicitly
in `Resources/Balance.json` after `"origins"`.

Two read-mostly files carry a marked region each:

- `Systems/InvestorSystem.swift` — the quarterly review reads the board's
  patience through `SabbaticalEffects.boardPatienceWeeks`, which halves it
  while somebody is away and returns the same `Int` otherwise.
- `Systems/RivalSystem.swift` — `poachCheck` multiplies its threshold by
  `SabbaticalEffects.poachChanceFactor` (×1.5 away, ×1 otherwise). The
  `worldRNG` draw itself is unchanged and still happens in the same place,
  so the stream never moves.

### App

- `App/Sources/Screens/Life/Sabbatical/SabbaticalCard.swift` — the You-section
  card in three states: the pitch (with the best would-be caretaker and their
  blocker), the countdown (progress bar, the last two log lines, *The log*
  and *Come home* with the bond cost on the button), and the return banner
  with the report's headline.
- `SabbaticalScreen.swift` — pushed by `Route.sabbatical` /
  `LifeDestination.sabbatical`: the pixel-paper header, the caretaker list
  (portrait, level, tenure, a bond bar notched at the gate, the reason a row
  is greyed), the weeks stepper with `−$2,000 → $23,000 in your wallet`, the
  *Hand X the keys · 6 weeks · −$3,000* button with its refusal underneath,
  and a "what you are risking" card that says the founder produces nothing,
  the board's patience reads as 50% of itself, and rivals are ×1.5 more
  likely to call. While away it becomes the countdown, the full log and the
  *Cut it short* card.
- `SabbaticalReportSheet.swift` — the return sheet, built like
  `WeeklyReportSheet`: pixel headline, then-and-now numbers, what the
  caretaker shipped / started / hired / lost, and the log.
- `WorkScheduleCard.swift` (owned) gets the *Not here at all* row inside a
  marked region — the third setting on a card that is already about how much
  of the founder the company gets.
- Markers only in `AppRouter.swift` (`Route.sabbatical` + its tab),
  `LifeScreen.swift` (card, destination, switch, and the `-autoRoute`
  landing in `consumeRoute`, which had no scaffold marker so the insert is
  wrapped in `// MARK: L6`), and `DebugLaunch.swift` (route names plus the
  pass driver below).

## Gates, costs and rules I chose

| Knob | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `minTenureWeeks` | 26 | Half a year on the payroll before you get the keys. |
| `minBond` | 50 | A caretaker is a relationship, not an org-chart position. |
| `minHeadcount` | 2 | Nobody minds an empty office. |
| `minWeeks` / `maxWeeks` | 4 / 12 | The brief's range. |
| `shipLockoutDays` | 14 | No launch in the diary — see the deviation below. |
| `weeklyCost` | $500 | Wallet money, charged up front; a four-week trip is $2,000. No refund if you come home early. |
| `healthPerDay` / `energyPerDay` / `moodPerDay` / `affectionPerDay` | 0.7 / 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.4 | A month off is worth roughly a full meter. |
| `decisionIntervalDays` | 7 | One call a week, at most. |
| `shipFactor` / `speedsterShipFactor` | 1.15 / 1.0 | A Speedster ships it; everybody else waits for a bit more polish. |
| `hireChance` / `hireRunwayWeeks` | 0.2 / 26 | A caretaker hires rarely, and only with half a year of runway. |
| `grumblerMoralePerDay` | −0.15 | ≈ −1 a week across the room. |
| `mentorSkillGain` | 2.5 | On top of the course the company paid for. |
| `flightRiskLeaveChance` | 0.12 | Roughly one trip in five with a Flight Risk in charge ends badly. |
| `boardPatienceFactor` | 0.5 | "Board patience halves while the founder is away." |
| `poachChanceFactor` | 1.5 | Recruiters read the trade press. |
| `earlyEndBondPenalty` | 25 | Being trusted and then checked on. |

Identity holds because every one of these is read only while
`state.life.sabbatical?.isActive` is true, and that is only ever true
because the player pressed a button. The pacing suite is unmoved: 943 tests,
same numbers.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Double-logging.** Caretaker decisions go through `Reducer.apply`, which
  calls `state.logEvents` itself, so `SabbaticalSystem` discards `apply`'s
  return value and returns only the events it raises itself
  (`.founderAway`, `.founderBack`, and the departure trio when a caretaker
  walks). Consequence: a caretaker's ship does not enter the day's `events`
  array and so cannot pause the timeline — deliberate, since the player is
  on a beach.
- **No new `GameEvent` cases.** The trip reuses `.founderAway` /
  `.founderBack`, which already exist for holidays and hospital stays, so
  `GameState.swift` is untouched outside L2's region.
- **Draws.** One or two `socialRNG` uniforms on a decision day, never on any
  other day. The one exception is `ProductSystem.startProduct`, which mints
  the product id from `rng` exactly as it does for every product a player
  ever starts — a caretaker starting a build is a player action taken by
  proxy, and it draws where a player action draws. No run that never takes a
  sabbatical draws anything new.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **The fortnight lock-out is "a build that is not finished yet".** The
   brief says "no build shipping in the next fortnight". Taken literally
   that bans sabbaticals outright for a studio with a dozen engineers, whose
   ETAs are always inside two weeks, and worse: a finished build nobody has
   shipped sits at an ETA of zero forever and locks the feature for good. So
   the gate refuses only when a build lands inside `shipLockoutDays` **and is
   not already shippable** — if it is finished, the answer is "ship it, then
   go", and the blocker says which build and how many days.
2. **The slot stays non-nil after the trip.** `LifeState.sabbatical` is the
   scaffold's `SabbaticalState?` and I may not add fields to `LifeState`, so
   the report lives on the slot with `endedDay` set. `isActive` is the
   thing every system asks; other lanes reading `state.life.sabbatical !=
   nil` as "a sabbatical happened" still get a sensible answer.
3. **The early end refunds nothing.** The weeks are paid up front, which is
   what makes the wallet gate honest, and coming home early already costs
   bond. Documented on the card.

## For the pass to be repeatable

`-autoSabbatical [weeks]` (DEBUG, in my `DebugLaunch` region) spends the
founder's evenings on the longest-serving employee — one-on-ones, mentoring,
coffees, all ordinary `GameAction`s through the ordinary reducer, no back
door into state — until the engine says they qualify, then hands them the
keys. It also keeps the pass alive the way `startAutoAnswering` does
(answering prompts, clearing the awards sheet, resuming a paused clock,
borrowing when the unattended company runs dry), because that loop starts
from HQ's root and a pass that lands on Life never shows HQ. The
`away.png` shot additionally used a *build-artifact* fixture: the bundled
`release-studio-day400.json` inside `build/…/StartupStudio.app` was patched
to bond 80 / wallet $25,000 / cash $300,000 so a four-week trip fits inside
the ~100 days an unplayed fixture survives. Nothing in the repository was
changed for it.
