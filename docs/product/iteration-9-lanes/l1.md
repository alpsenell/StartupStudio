# L1 — The phone

Branch `l1-phone`, from `scaffold-9`. Simulator `ws-l1`.

## What it is

Everything that used to arrive as a sheet and vanish now also lands as a
message in a thread with the person it is about, the founder's answer lands
as a reply, and a deadline that answered for them leaves **Seen. No reply.**
in the thread forever. The threads are scrollable history; the Life tab
carries an unread badge; a thread exports as a 1080×1350 card.

The phone is a **mirror, not a mechanism**. Nothing in this lane moves a
meter, spends an evening, changes a price, or draws a random number. Every
message is a line of copy written next to something the game already did.

## What I built

### Engine

**`Phone.swift`** (mine, reshaped freely; `post` keeps its scaffold
signature so L3–L6 can call it).

- `PhoneMessageKind` (`said` / `unanswered`) on `PhoneMessage`, hand-written
  `Codable` that omits the default, so a bubble costs no extra bytes.
- Message ids are now `last.id + 1` rather than `messages.count`, so they
  stay unique after a thread trims. `PhoneThread.historyLimit = 60`
  messages; older bubbles scroll away, which is what stops a ten-year run
  writing a novel into the save.
- `PhoneCounterpart.sortKey` / `.personID`; `PhoneState.byRecency`,
  `markRead`, `isAsking`, `askingThread`; `PhoneThread.lastMessage` /
  `.lastDay`.
- `GameState.phoneName(for:)`, `.phoneSeed(for:)`, `.phoneRelation(for:)` —
  the app never has to know how to find a partner, a child, an employee or
  an alumnus by counterpart.

**`Systems/PhoneSystem.swift`** (new).

- `PhoneSystem.run` in the L1 region of `Reducer.systems`. It does exactly
  one thing on its own: on the last day of each week the office texts the
  week's numbers, summed from the ledger
  (`"Week 57 closed. In $17,137, out $20,780, -$3,643 net. Cash $74,119."`).
- `PhoneMirror` — the whole mapping from "something happened" to "somebody
  texted", called from five marked hook sites (below).

**Marked hook regions** (`// MARK: Iteration 9 — L1` … `// MARK: end L1`),
each a single call:

| File | Hook |
|---|---|
| `Systems/NarrativeSystem.swift` | `fireCompany` (raised + landed), `fireLife` (raised + landed), `resolve` (the answer, or the marker) |
| `Systems/RelationshipSystem.swift` | the partner's single warning, in their own words |
| `Systems/SocialSystem.swift` | a staff moment raised (both raise sites) and answered |
| `Systems/NetworkingSystem+Alumni.swift` | the boomerang: a quarterly check-in, and the day they turn out to have founded something |

**Which thread a beat lands in** (`PhoneMirror.counterpart`):

1. `pending.childID`, when that child exists → `.child(id)`.
2. `partner_*` plus `dating_app_match`, `holiday_alone`,
   `vacation_never_taken` → `.partner`, unless the founder is single.
3. `kid_*` plus `kids_first_word`, `newborn_sleepless`,
   `recital_vs_dinner`, `school_run`, `sunday_lunch` → the same child
   `FamilyCalendar.fill` names (nearest birthday).
4. `friend_*` plus the nine friend-flavoured ids → `state.life.friends`'s
   first friend. Empty today, so friend beats stay off the phone until L4
   lands; then they light up with no further work.
5. Company beats → `.office`, **only when somebody other than the founder
   is on payroll** (see "Identity at the default" below).
6. Anything else → nobody, and the phone stays out of it.

**Action**: `.markPhoneThreadRead(counterpart:)` in the L1 region of
`GameAction` and `Reducer.apply`. Pure bookkeeping.

### App — `App/Sources/Screens/Life/Phone/**`

- **`PhoneCard.swift`** — the three newest threads under the week on the
  Life tab, one line each, unread bold with a count pill, a `WAITING`
  badge on the thread that owes an answer, "Open the phone · 78 unread".
  The card does not draw at all for a founder nobody has texted.
- **`PhoneScreen.swift`** — the whole list inside a pixel handset
  (`PhoneHandset`: speaker slot, bitmap status line `MESSAGES … 78 NEW ·
  16 FEB · Y2`, a rule, then the rows).
- **`ThreadView.swift` / `ThreadContent`** — the conversation on pixel
  paper: the counterpart's bubbles left in `pixelPaper`, the founder's
  right in `pixelAccent`, the *Seen. No reply.* marker greyed and italic
  on the right where the answer should have been, `PixelText` day rules
  between days. Opening it sends `markPhoneThreadRead`.
- **`PhoneReply.swift`** — the reply buttons. Two things ask the founder
  questions and both of them text, so the thread unifies them: a narrative
  `pendingChoice` (→ `.resolveChoice`) and a `pendingStaffEvent` in an
  `.employee` thread (→ `.resolveStaffEvent`, with the def's own labels and
  details, including the "…and make that the rule" third answer where the
  kind carries a policy block). Each button carries its consequence
  (`−$500 · time off · loyalty way up`), a closed option shows its reason
  greyed, and the header shows the deadline (`1 day left`). The firm answer
  is red, the open one accent — the decision sheet's own fills.
- **`PhoneThreadCardView.swift`** — the share card: the last eight bubbles
  on `ShareInk` paper with the portrait, the relation, and the
  `ShareFooter`, at `ShareRenderer.cardSize`.

**Marker-region edits elsewhere**

- `LifeScreen.swift`: `PhoneCard` under `ThisWeekCard`, the two

- `AppRootView.swift`: `.badge(engine.state.life.phone.unreadCount)` on the

- `ShareRenderer.swift`: `ShareCard.phoneThread(engine:counterpart:)` with

### Debug flags (all `#if DEBUG`, all in L1 markers)

- `-autoRoute phone` — land on the phone.
- `-autoThread [n]` — also open the nth thread by recency (default 0).
- `-autoThreadAsking` — open whichever thread is waiting on an answer.
- `-autoShareThread` — open the share card over the thread.
- `-autoDeferBeats` — a headless pass keeps the decision sheet off the
  screen for the questions the phone can answer, so the thread's own reply
  buttons are what gets photographed. This is the one place I touched
  `startAutoAnswering`'s body (one condition, inside L1 markers).

## Identity at the default, and the fixtures

The lane's one real design decision. `PhoneSystem`'s weekly office line and
company-beat mirroring are gated on **`state.employees.contains { !$0.isFounder }`**:
the office thread opens with the first hire and not before.

Without that gate a solo garage run posted four office messages a month and
`OriginTests.aGarageIsByteIdenticalToTheScaffold` failed on all three
fixtures — the `scaffold-5` contract that a garage produces the same bytes
on day 0 and after a month of play. With it, that suite passes untouched:
a founder alone in a garage has nobody to text them the numbers, every life
beat maps to a partner/child/friend they do not have, and `LifeState`
writes no `phone` key at all. Rule 1 holds exactly.

**Re-pinned expectation.** `ReleaseFixtureGenerator.theCommittedFixturesStillDecodeAndStillMatchTheirBriefs`
compares the committed store-screenshot saves byte-for-byte against a
replay. The two staffed fixtures legitimately gained phone threads, so I
regenerated them the documented way
(`REGENERATE_RELEASE_FIXTURES=1 swift test --filter regenerateReleaseFixtures`):

| Fixture | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `release-garage-day40` | 1 person, unchanged | **unchanged** (no phone at all) |
| `release-studio-day400` | 12 people | 154,355 bytes, 10 threads |
| `release-campus-day900` | 35 people | 247,580 bytes, 11 threads |

No test count changed anywhere; no test was added or removed. The fixture
files are data, not expectations — the test that reads them is unchanged.

**One localisation fix rather than a re-pin.**
`StringsAuditTests.testNoNewUnlocalizedPixelChrome` pins the number of bare
`PixelText(text: "…")` literals at 46. My two new ones (`Reply` in the
thread, `Messages` on the phone and the share card) took it to 48. The test's
own instructions say to localize rather than raise the bar, so all three
sites are now `String(localized:comment:)` with the pixel-face warning in
the comment, and **the baseline stays at 46**.

## Numbers I chose

| Thing | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `PhoneThread.historyLimit` | 60 messages | ~14 months of a busy office thread; caps the save |
| Office thread gate | `headcount > 1` | see above |
| Weekly office post | `day % 7 == 6`, `day > 0` | Sunday; week 1 has no numbers worth sending |
| Boomerang check-in | every `AlumniTuning.quarterDays` (91) | a day the alumni drift already computes; no new arithmetic |
| Founding text | `AlumniTuning.foundingDays` (180) | ditto |
| Share card bubbles | last 8 | the shape of the conversation without shrinking the type |
| Phone card threads | 3 | the brief |

None of these is a balance value; nothing in `BalanceConfig` changed, and
there is no `BalanceConfig+Phone.swift` to add.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **The office thread is gated on a first hire** (above). The brief says
   "the weekly report's one-line summary posts to `.office` every Sunday";
   it does, from the first hire onward.
2. **The weekly line is composed in the engine, not from the app's
   `WeeklyReport`.** `WeeklyReport` is an app type and `PhoneSystem` runs
   in the engine; the line is summed from the same `state.ledger` rows the
   report reconciles against, so the two can never disagree.
3. **Non-question life events post only where they have a counterpart.**
   The brief says "non-question life events post one line"; a beat about
   nobody (`tax_refund`, `parking_ticket`) has no thread to post to, and
   inventing a "notes to self" thread would have made the phone a diary.
4. **Employee threads answer staff moments too.** The brief only asked
   that staff moments *post* to `.employee(id)`. Since the employee thread
   is by far the most common one in a real run, leaving it unanswerable
   would have made the reply buttons a rarity; `PhoneReply` covers both
   sources through the engine's own actions.
5. **The badge is in `AppRootView.swift`, not `GameShell.swift`** — that
   is where the Team tab's badge lives (`.badge(EmployeeStatus.attentionCount…)`),
   so mine sits beside it, in a marked region.

## Follow-ups for other lanes

- **L4 (friends).** `PhoneMirror.friendEventIDs` already routes the nine
  friend-flavoured life events to `state.life.friends.friends.first`, and
  `GameState.phoneName/phoneSeed/phoneRelation` already handle `.friend`.
  Once friends exist, pick the *right* friend rather than the first: either
  set `PendingChoice.childID`'s equivalent for friends (a new optional on
  `PendingChoice`, which is not my file) or replace the
  `friends.first` line in `PhoneMirror.counterpart` — that line is inside
  `PhoneSystem.swift`, which is mine, so ask me or edit it in a merge.
- **L3 (children).** `PhoneState.post(_:from:day:fromFounder:eventID:)` is
  unchanged and is what the memory ledger should call. `.child(id)` threads
  already draw the child's `appearanceSeed` portrait and read the name from
  `family.children`.
- **L2 (life score).** If the biography wants "the last thing your partner
  said", `state.life.phone.thread(with: .partner)?.lastMessage` is a pure
  read.
- **L6 (sabbatical).** The caretaker's daily one-liner should go to
  `.office`; that thread is already open whenever there is a caretaker to
  hand the company to (headcount > 1), so no gate work is needed.
- **`make strings` at the merge.** My three new keys (`Reply`, `Messages`,
  and the reply-button accessibility strings) are not in
  `App/Resources/Localizable.xcstrings` yet: running `make strings` on this
  branch also swept in ~265 keys from iterations 7 and 8 that were never
  synced, which is not my diff to make. Run it once on `iteration-9` after
  all seven lanes are in. Nothing breaks meanwhile — `String(localized:)`
  falls back to the key.
- **Nobody's file, but worth a PM decision.** `PhoneCounterpart` has no
  case for "the world" (a tax refund, a parking ticket). If a later round
  wants those on the phone, the cheapest shape is a `.self` case rather
  than reusing `.office`.
