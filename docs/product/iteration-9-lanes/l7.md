# L7 — Furnish the home

Branch `l7-furnish`, cut from `scaffold-9`. Possessions stopped being a
line in a list: they are objects in the pixel home now, standing in named
places, next to a poster from a season you saw out and a trophy from an
ending you reached.

## What I built

### Engine — `HomeDecor.swift` (rewritten, L7 owns it)

- **Slots.** Twelve ids, `wallA…D`, `shelfA…D`, `floorA…D`, unlocked by
  home tier: the studio has three (`…A`), the apartment six, the house
  nine, the penthouse twelve. `HomeDecor.slots(for:)` is the gameplay
  truth; PixelKit owns the pixel anchor for the same ids.
- **Catalog.** `DecorItem` (id, name, the one slot *kind* it fits, source,
  a one-line note). Nineteen things: the five shop possessions, the plant,
  the record player, the awards pennant, five season posters (one per
  `SeasonTwist`), six ending trophies (one per `EndingKind`).
- **`HomeDecorState`** grew `arrangedFor` (the tier the arrangement
  belongs to) beside `placed`, both decode-if-present. `placed(in: tier)`
  returns nothing when the tier does not match — *moving home keeps the
  items and empties the slots*, without touching the `upgradeHome` path
  and without throwing the record away if you move back.
- **Rules,** all pure: `fits`, `firstEmptySlot`, `place`, `remove`. A
  place moves the item out of any slot it was already in and evicts
  whatever was standing there.
- **Actions** (`GameAction` L7 region, handled in the `Reducer` L7
  region): `.placeDecor(slot:itemID:)`, `.removeDecor(slot:)`. No events,
  no meters, no money, no RNG. A shop item must be in `possessions`;
  earned decor is gated by the ledger, which the engine cannot see, so
  the app only ever offers what has been unlocked.
- **`LegacyLedger.unlockedDecor: Set<String>`** (decode-if-present, merged
  in `merged(with:)`) plus a derived `availableDecor`: what has been
  written, plus a trophy for every ending in `endingsReached`, plus the
  plant (everybody gets one), plus the record player once `hall` has
  anything in it. Deriving the trophies means a ledger written before this
  iteration already owns the ones it earned.
- **Buying places it.** `LifeSystem.buyItem` ends with: if the home has an
  empty slot the thing fits, it goes straight in. Marked
  `// MARK: Iteration 9 — L7` inside that function (see deviations).

### PixelKit

- **`HomeDecorSprites.swift`** (new): `SpriteLibrary.HomeDecorName`, whose
  raw values *are* the engine's decor ids, and `SpriteLibrary.homeDecor(_:)`.
  Nine new sprite functions, palette-only: espresso machine (10×12, two
  steam frames), gaming console + pad (14×5, two frames), road bike
  (18×12), watch on its stand (8×11), the car in model form (14×6), record
  player (14×8, the label turns), awards pennant (16×9), season poster
  (14×18, five glyphs × five palettes), ending trophy (9×13, one shape ×
  six metals) — plus the 16×3 shelf plank, which is only drawn when
  something is standing on it, so an empty wall stays an empty wall. The
  plant reuses the existing `plantHome` prop, so no new art for it.
  Deliberately **not** added to `SpriteLibrary.HomePropName`: that enum's
  count is pinned by `HomeSpriteLibraryTests` and I was not going to
  re-pin a test to add decor.
- **`HomeDecorSlots.swift`** (new): `HomeDecorSlotKind` (wall / shelf /
  floor), `HomeDecorSlotFrame` (id, kind, anchor, tap box) and
  `HomeSceneComposer.decorSlots(for:)` — the anchor table, chosen by hand
  against each tier's `Layout` so nothing lands on the window, the stove,
  the door frame, the bed or a child's rug.
- **`HomeSceneComposer.compose`** gained a seventh parameter,
  `decor: [String: SpriteLibrary.HomeDecorName]`, on a new overload; the
  old six-parameter one forwards `[:]`, so **every existing caller
  compiles and draws exactly what it drew before**. Wall and shelf decor
  is placed after the lamp, floor decor after the armchair — in front of
  the furniture, behind the people.
- **`HomeHitRegion.Kind.decorSlot(String)`**, and `hitRegions` /
  `hitTest` gained `decor:` and `emptyDecorSlots:` (both defaulted off).
  Filled slots always get a region; empty ones only in the furnish sheet,
  so the Life tab's room does not turn into a grid for VoiceOver.
- **`HomeSceneView`** gained `decor`, `emptyDecorSlots`, `decorLabel`
  (the app names the slots; PixelKit only knows ids) and `scale`, and a
  real finger layer — the accessibility overlay was `allowsHitTesting(false)`
  and `onTapRegion` had no way to fire from a tap. All defaulted; the
  tap layer only exists when a caller passes `onTapRegion`.

### App

- **`FurnishSheet`** (`App/Sources/Screens/Life/Decor/FurnishSheet.swift`):
  the room at three points per pixel in a horizontal scroller (the house
  and the penthouse are wider than a phone), every empty place marked with
  a dashed box and the one you are working on with a solid one, tappable
  in the picture and as a row of chips under it, then a shelf of
  everything you own or have earned — sprite, "earned from" caption, and a
  button that says where it is going (`→ the corner by the window`,
  `In place`, `Nowhere yet`). Removing says `Put the road bike away`.
- **`DecorPresentation`**: the seam between engine ids and PixelKit
  sprites, the "what may I place" filter, VoiceOver's line per slot, and
  the `-autoDecor` showroom. Also the two `GameSession` unlock hooks.
- **`HomeCard`**: the scene now draws the decor, and a *Furnish* row under
  it says how full the home is ("2 of 6 places taken") and opens the
  sheet.
- **`ShoppingSheet`**: every row now says where the thing would go and
  what the wallet does — `$9,750 → $9,150 · it stands on a shelf`, and
  once owned, `It stands on a shelf at home`.
- **Rewards.** A finished season writes its poster
  (`GameSession+Season.swift`, marked region); a night the company won
  something writes the pennant (`AwardsNightSheet`, marked region);
  ending trophies and the record player are derived from the ledger.
- **Routing / debug** (marked regions): `Route.furnish` → the Life tab;
  `-autoRoute furnish` opens the sheet; `-autoDecor` fills every slot with
  one of everything for a headless pass (display only, nothing written);
  `-autoHome <tier>` draws the home card and the furnish sheet at that
  tier whatever the save says, so one pass photographs all four rooms.

## Deviations, and why

1. **`LifeSystem.swift` is L3's file** and I put four lines in it, inside
   `// MARK: Iteration 9 — L7` markers at the end of `buyItem` — the brief
   asks that buying an item that fits an empty slot places it
   automatically, and `buyItem` is the only place that can. It is in a
   function L3 has no reason to touch (the child paths are elsewhere), so
   the merge should be clean.
2. **Ending trophies are derived, not written.** The brief said the
   endings flow should add an id to `unlockedDecor`. Deriving them from
   `endingsReached` in `LegacyLedger.availableDecor` needs no edit to
   `record(_:balance:)` (L2's marked file), cannot drift out of sync, and
   retro-fits every ledger that already exists. The stored set is still
   there and still written — by the season and awards hooks.
3. **No new `HomePropName` cases.** See above: the enum's count is pinned
   by an existing test, and the rule says re-pin only when the feature
   genuinely changes what a test counts. Decor lives in its own
   `HomeDecorName` enum instead.
4. **`sportsCar` is a model of the car**, on a shelf. A sports car does
   not fit in a studio flat, and the joke is better.
5. **The furnish sheet draws at a fixed ×3 in a scroller** rather than the
   card's fit-to-width, which floors the house and the penthouse to ×1 and
   makes a room you cannot see the things in. The Life tab card is
   unchanged.
6. **PossessionsCard is untouched** — it lives in `WeekendRecapCard.swift`,
   which no lane owns; the shelf in the furnish sheet is the new home for
   that list, and the old card still works.

## Identity at the default

No balance value was added. A save with no decor draws the room it always
drew: `compose` without `decor` is byte-for-byte the old function, the
new hit regions are off unless a caller asks for them, and the two new
actions are the only way anything gets placed. No `rng` / `worldRNG`
draws (no RNG at all — nothing here is random). Everything new decodes
as its default when absent: `HomeDecorState.arrangedFor`,
`LegacyLedger.unlockedDecor`.
