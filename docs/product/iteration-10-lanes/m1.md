# M1 — The feature board

Branch `m1-featureboard`, from `scaffold-10`. A product is now assembled
from feature cards on a board rather than a focus slider alone: the hand
comes from the tech tree, the crew's strengths and the market's appetite
this quarter; the board's fit, synergies and appetite hits multiply quality
at ship; the review quotes the cards by name; a copycat rival lifts the
best one; a sequel starts with its parent's board already laid out; and the
storefront lists what the thing actually does.

## What I built

### Content

- `Packages/TycoonContent/Sources/TycoonContent/FeatureCardDef.swift` —
  the card as content: `id`, `name`, `blurb`, `fitsTypes`, `fitsTopics`,
  `unlockedBy` (a tech node id), `leansOn` (design/code/polish),
  `synergies` (card ids, read symmetrically) and `appetiteTag`. Every field
  but `id`/`name` is decode-if-present, so a card that fits everything is
  two keys long.
- `Packages/TycoonContent/Sources/TycoonContent/Resources/Features.json` —
  **59 cards**. Every topic is served by 29–33 of them, every product type
  by a mix of generic and specific, and 9 sit behind a tech node
  (`ui_toolkit`, `telemetry`, `security_compliance`, `internal_tooling`,
  `game_engine`, `cloud_infrastructure`, `ai_pair_programmer`).
- `ContentCatalog` gains `features` and `featureCard(_:)`, decoded with
  `decodeResourceIfPresent`, so a bundle without the file reads as a game
  with no boards.

### Engine

- `FeatureBoard.swift` (reshaped; the scaffold's `FeatureCard` survives as
  a `typealias` onto `FeatureCardDef`): `slots(for:balance:)`,
  `appetite(state:balance:)`, `hand(for:state:content:balance:)`,
  `reading(for:…)` → `FeatureBoardReading` (per-card `FeatureCardReading`
  with a `verdict` in words), `place`/`remove` returning a typed
  `FeatureBoardRefusal`, public `refusal(placing:…)` / `refusal(removingSlot:…)`
  dry runs so the screen can put the true reason on the button, and
  `inheritedBoard(…)` for sequels.
- `Product.features: [String]` with a hand-written `encode(to:)`: the key
  is written **only when the board is non-empty**, and decoded
  if-present — so a run that never places a card produces byte-identical
  JSON and the `OriginTests` / `ReleaseFixtureGenerator` fixtures pass
  untouched.
- `ProductSystem.ship` multiplies quality by the board; `startProduct`
  gained a `balance:` parameter and lays out the inherited board;
  `ReviewContext` gained `bestFeature` / `worstFeature`.
- `ShipForecast` gained `featureMultiplier` and `featureSummary`, both
  neutral (1 and `nil`) on an empty board, and a `limitingFactor` line for
  a board that is actively costing score.
- `Reviews.swift`: `{feature}` and `{misfit}` tokens, and two new callouts
  (`featureLed`, `featureMisfit`) placed **after** every callout that
  existed before them, so a launch with no board earns exactly the callout
  it earned before. `Reviews.json` gains five lines for each.
- `Rival.swift`: `RivalProduct.copiedFeature: String?`, hand-written
  decode-if-present, encoded only when present (synthesised
  `encodeIfPresent`). `RivalSystem`'s copycat pass (marked M1 region)
  records the card it lifted off the player's best release.
- `BalanceConfig+FeatureBoard.swift` + the `"featureBoard"` key in
  `Resources/Balance.json` (appended after `"sabbatical"`, behind a
  `"_iteration10Blocks"` marker).
- Actions `.placeFeature(productID:cardID:slot:)` and
  `.removeFeature(productID:slot:)` in the M1 regions of `GameAction` and
  `Reducer`. **No `GameEvent` case**: a board is a plan, not news — see
  the deviations.

### App

- `App/Sources/Screens/Products/FeatureBoard/FeatureBoardScreen.swift` —
  pixel chrome throughout (`PixelPanel`, `PixelSectionTitle`, `PixelText`,
  `PixelButtonStyle`): a verdict panel with the multiplier on it, the
  quarter's appetite as chips, the slots as a grid of pixel cards each
  saying what it is doing there, and the hand as rows whose button carries
  its own consequence ("Place · hot · ×2").
- `FeatureBoardAutoRoute.swift` — the `FeatureBoardLink` navigation value,
  the `FeatureBoardLinkButton` card for the detail screen (with the
  board's current worth on it), and the `-autoRoute featureboard` /
  `-autoFeatureBoard` headless pass.
- `ProductDetailScreen`: the board card above Focus, the board's line in
  the ship-forecast card, and the board's worth in the ship confirmation.
- `NewProductFlow`: a "Next: the board" card on the details page saying how
  many slots the chosen type has, and Start now pushes the board inside the
  sheet with a Done button rather than dismissing.
- `StorefrontScreen`: a "What it does" card listing the placed cards with
  their blurbs, on both the released and the coming-soon page. Absent for
  an empty board.
- Marker regions consumed: `AppRouter` (`Route.featureBoard(productID:)`
  and its tab), `ProductsScreen.consumeRoute`, `DebugLaunch` (route names
  and flags).

## The multiplier band

A card is worth `fitValue` (1.0) when it belongs on both the type and the
topic, `-fitValue` when it belongs on neither, 0 when it is generic or fits
one of the two; `+synergyValue/2` per partner already on the board (so a
pair is worth 0.5 once); `+appetiteValue` (0.5) for riding a tag the
quarter wants. The board's total is divided by the slot count and read as

```
qualityMultiplier = clamp(1 + qualitySwing × (total ÷ slots),
                          1 − qualityPenaltyCap, 1 + qualityBonusCap)
                  = clamp(1 + 0.10 × perSlot, 0.90, 1.12)
```

so the band is **×0.90 … ×1.12**, and an empty board is **exactly 1.0** by
construction — the total of no cards is zero, whatever the numbers say.
That is the identity the pacing bots and the byte-identical fixtures need,
and it does not depend on any tuning. A realistic full six-slot board with
three synergies and two appetite hits reaches the +12% cap; a board of
cards that belong on neither the type nor the topic hits the −10% floor.

No RNG stream is touched: the quarter's appetite is SplitMix64 over the
seed and the quarter number, plus the tags of the hottest booming topics,
capped at four.

## Deviations, and why

- **No `GameEvent` case.** The M1 region in `GameState.GameEvent` is
  untouched. Placing a card moves no meter and spends no time; making it an
  event would put "you placed Dark Mode" in the journal and force every
  exhaustive `switch` over `GameEvent` in the app to learn a case for it.
  The consequences that *are* news (the review that quotes the card, the
  rival that copies it) already have events.
- **`FeatureCard` lives in TycoonContent** as `FeatureCardDef`, with the
  scaffold's engine-side name kept as a `public typealias`. The catalog
  decodes it, and `ContentCatalog` cannot import the engine.
- **The synergy "lines drawn between placed cards"** are badges (`×2`) and
  a named list on the card's back rather than literal drawn lines: on a
  4–6 card adaptive grid the lines would cross the cards themselves at
  phone width. The information — which cards pair, and how many — is on
  every card.
- **The hand is ordered, not filtered, by the crew's skills.** A hand of
  ten from a catalog of fifty-nine is already a real constraint; ranking by
  fit, appetite and the pool the crew is strongest in means a studio of
  designers sees a designer's hand, without hiding a card the player has
  researched and can see in the catalog.
- **The board locks at 90% of the design pool**, not at a phase boundary:
  there is no explicit "design phase ends" moment in the engine, and a
  sequel inherits 35% of its parent's design points, which a stricter gate
  would lock on day one.
- **`Rival.swift` is not in anybody's ownership table**, and
  `copiedFeature` had to live on `RivalProduct` for the copycat region to
  record anything. It is additive and decode-if-present. Flagged below.

## Debug flags added

`-autoRoute featureboard|features|featuredetail|featurestore`, and
`-autoFeatureBoard` as a synonym for the first. All three start a build if
the generated game has none, place the first three cards of the hand, and
land on the screen named; `featurestore` also ships the build as soon as
the engine allows and routes to the released page.
