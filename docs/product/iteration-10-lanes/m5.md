# M5 — The morning desk

*Iteration 10, lane M5. Branch `m5-desk`, cut from `scaffold-10`. Simulator
`ws-l5`.*

## What it is

A one-minute ritual at the front door, and the same desk from the HUD's
date mid-run. Three papers — **one message**, **one decision**, **one
tap** — derived from the slot's state without advancing anything. Clear
all three and the day (`yyyymmdd`) is stamped; the streak lives across
runs in the ledger and pays in decor, founder looks and a masthead
flourish. Never a stat.

## What was built

### Engine — `Packages/TycoonEngine/Sources/TycoonEngine/MorningDesk.swift`

The whole lane's model, in the file the scaffold reserved:

- **`DeskPart`** — `message`, `decision`, `tap`, with the papers' order
  and headings.
- **`DeskState`** (the `state.desk` slot the scaffold declared) — grew
  `progressDay` and `doneParts` beside `clearedDays`, with a hand-written
  Codable: every key decodes as its default when absent and is written
  only when it is not, so a save from before the desk loads unchanged and
  a run that never opens the desk writes no bytes. `clearedDays` is capped
  at 200. `mark(_:on:)` returns true on the third paper — the one moment
  the ledger wants to hear about.
- **`DeskDay`** — `yyyymmdd` arithmetic. The engine never reads a wall
  clock: the app stamps today in the *player's* calendar and hands the
  integer in, and the day arithmetic (gaps, months) is done in a fixed UTC
  gregorian calendar so it is the same everywhere. This is the whole of
  the desk's dependency on real time.
- **`DeskStreakChange`, `DeskRewardKind`, `DeskReward`, `DeskRewards`** —
  the reward table and the ladder's next rung.
- **`LegacyLedger.recordDeskDay(_:)`** and **`deskStreakIsLive(on:)`** —
  the streak rules (below), including writing earned decor ids into
  `unlockedDecor` the way the season and awards hooks do.
- **`DeskTapKind` / `DeskTapCard` / `MorningDesk.tap(in:balance:on:)`** —
  today's one tap: praise the quietest person off the praise cooldown, or
  a coffee with the least loyal person off the social cooldown when the
  company can cover the round, alternating by the day's parity, falling
  through to *water the plant* (no action at all). A solo founder always
  gets the plant, which is why the plant exists.

### App — `App/Sources/Desk/**` (new)

- `MorningDeskBoard.swift` — the three papers assembled from the state
  (the message from `PhoneReply`, the decision from the Business tab's own
  `Desk.items`, the tap from the engine), plus the session's side:
  `deskBoard()`, `deskDo(_:clearing:today:)` (sends the paper's ordinary
  action, records the paper, saves the slot, and moves the ledger when
  that was the third), `deskMarkEmpties(_:)` and `deskLiveStreak`.
- `MorningDeskSheet.swift` — the desk: a pixel header with the greeting
  and the streak pill, three tilted pixel papers whose buttons each state
  their consequence, a paper that flies off to the right and lands as a
  line on the done pile, the **DESK CLEARED** stamp with what the streak
  did and what it earned, the six-rung reward ladder, and the reminder.
- `MorningDeskCard.swift` — the front door's card: streak pill, three
  ticks, one button, and a line about what is still waiting.
- `DeskReminder.swift` — `UNUserNotificationCenter`, one repeating daily
  notification at the hour the desk was last cleared. **Permission is
  requested from the desk's own button and nowhere else**; there is no
  notification code on any other path, and a player who never presses
  *Remind me tomorrow* never sees the system prompt. No Info.plist usage
  string is needed for notifications, so `project.yml` is untouched.

## The streak rules

Recorded by `LegacyLedger.recordDeskDay(today)` when the third paper goes:

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| First cleared day ever (`deskLastDay == 0`) | streak = 1 |
| The day after the last one (gap 1) | streak + 1 |
| Exactly one day missed (gap 2), month's sick day unspent | streak + 1, `deskSickDayMonth` = this month |
| Gap 2 with the month's sick day already spent, or gap ≥ 3 | streak = 1 |
| The same day again | nothing (the day is already counted) |
| A day earlier than `deskLastDay` (clock moved back, another time zone) | nothing |

`deskBestStreak` is the high-water mark and is what rewards are read
from, so a lapsed streak never takes a reward away. The front door shows
0 once a streak has lapsed (`deskStreakIsLive(on:)`) while the ledger
keeps the best.

Papers with nothing on them — an empty inbox, an empty business desk —
are marked the moment the desk is opened, so a solo founder on a quiet
Tuesday can still keep a streak by doing the one thing that *is* there.

## The reward table

| Days | Reward | Id / seed |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Sunrise over the desk (wall poster) | decor `deskSunrise` |
| 7 | The early riser (founder look) | seed `0x0DE5_0007_5EED_0A11` |
| 14 | The morning plaque (shelf) | decor `deskPlaque` |
| 30 | The sunrise masthead (title screen) | flourish |
| 60 | The regular (founder look) | seed `0x0DE5_0060_5EED_0B22` |
| 100 | One hundred mornings (wall poster) | decor `deskCentury` |

Nothing in the table is a number in the simulation. Decor ids are written
into `ledger.unlockedDecor`; looks and the flourish are derived from
`deskBestStreak` and cost no bytes.

## Identity at the default

- The desk sends only ordinary actions (`.markPhoneThreadRead`,
  `.praise`, `.grabCoffee`, or none at all) plus `.clearDeskCard`, which
  touches nothing but `state.desk`. A run that never opens the desk is
  byte-identical: `desk` still encodes as absent.
- No new draw from `rng` or `worldRNG`, or from any RNG: the tap's choice
  is the day's parity and the state's own cooldowns.
- No system was added to the tick; nothing here advances the clock.
- One caveat, deliberately: `LegacyLedger`'s encoder is synthesized, so
  the four new ledger keys are written as `0` on every save, the way
  `unlockedDecor: []` already was. That is the ledger file, not a run.

## Deviations and judgement calls

1. **No `GameEvent`.** The M5 region in `GameState.swift` is empty. A
   cleared desk is a fact about the player's calendar, not about the
   company, so it has no place in the journal or the front page — and
   adding a case would have meant edits to `EventCopy` and
   `NewspaperComposer`, which M5 does not own. The consequence is that
   `.clearDeskCard` returns no events, so the event-driven autosave does
   not fire; `deskDo` asks the engine's own `autosave` hook for the write
   instead.
2. **Two files outside the ownership table**, both in small marked M5
   regions, both flagged here rather than done silently:
   - `Packages/PixelKit/Sources/PixelKit/HomeDecorSprites.swift`: three
     cases on `SpriteLibrary.HomeDecorName` and three switch arms built
     from the *existing* `posterSprite`/`trophySprite` builders. Without
     them the streak decor would be earnable and placeable but would draw
     nothing in the pixel home. No new art, no palette outside `Palettes`.
     PixelKit is 336 green with them.
   - `App/Sources/Screens/Onboarding/NewGameFlow.swift`: one line in the
     `looks` array. No lane owns the file and no other lane's brief goes
     near it. If the PM would rather not carry it, deleting that hunk
     removes the streak looks from the picker and nothing else breaks.
3. **A fourth ledger field.** The brief names three (`deskStreak`,
   `deskBestStreak`, `deskLastDay`); the free monthly sick day needs to
   remember *which* month it was spent in, so `deskSickDayMonth` is in the
   same region on the same decode-if-present terms.
4. **The decision's *Open it* from the front door** goes back into the
   company rather than into the section — the router lives inside the
   game and the title screen is outside it. The button says so ("Goes
   back into Meridian Labs"); from the HUD's date it says "Opens
   Rivals" and routes there.
5. **No `Route` case.** The M5 regions in `AppRouter.swift` and in the
   three `consumeRoute()`s are empty: the desk is a sheet over the front
   door and over the HUD, not a tab destination, so it needs no route of
   its own. It *consumes* routes — the decision's `Route` — rather than
   producing one.
6. **`-autoDesk cleared`** is DEBUG-only scaffolding that marks the three
   papers on open (without sending their actions) so the cleared desk
   could be photographed — `simctl` cannot tap.

## Follow-ups

- The desk is drawn as three pixel *panels*, not as a pixel desk sprite
  with paper on it. A `SpriteLibrary.deskPaper` and a small desk scene
  would be the next thing to make it feel like furniture; PixelKit's home
  and office files are not M5's.
- A cleared day is recorded in the slot that was open. Two slots on one
  wall-clock day cannot double-count the streak (`recordDeskDay` refuses
  the same day twice), but the *papers* start fresh in the second slot,
  which reads slightly oddly. Moving `clearedDays` to the ledger would fix
  it and was out of scope for the reserved `state.desk` slot.
- The reminder fires at the top of the hour the desk was last cleared. A
  player who clears it at 09:55 is reminded at 09:00. A minute would be
  easy to carry.
- The message paper answers the *pending* question; a thread with three
  unread messages is cleared by reading it. Answering more than one thing
  a morning would need the phone lane's reply queue.
