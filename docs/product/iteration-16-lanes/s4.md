# S4 — the city

*Lane S4 of iteration 16, branch `s4-city` from `scaffold-16` (c88e04d).
Owner's ask: "improvise the city layout as well, it should be more detailed
and interactive." No new tests, no engine change, no new engine state.*

## What was built

**A bigger, more detailed city.** The scene went from 224×160 to **320×224**
(`CityMapComposer.sceneSize`). It still pans in the ScrollView at whole-pixel
scale 3 on every phone (a drag, not a zoom). The street plan (`CityMapBuilder`):

- a ten-row east–west spine with a lane each way, three avenues, the Suburbs'
  residential lane, zebra crossings at every junction, and pools of lamplight
  on the pavement after dark;
- a river along the south with a quay, a bridge carrying the south avenue,
  a far bank, a boat, drifting ripples, gold reflections at night, and ice
  along both banks in winter;
- the Midtown park (hedge, gates, paths, pond, benches, a lamp), the Tech
  Park car park (painted bays, parked cars), the Downtown plaza (tiles,
  fountain, benches, lamps), and Old Town cobbles with snow in the joints in
  winter.

Each district is a set of authored **lots** (`CityMapLayout.swift`) holding
buildings in its own character (`CityDistrictArt.swift`, all master-palette
colours, shaded for the hour):

| District | Buildings |
|---|---|
| Old Town | brick rowhouses: mortar courses, tiled roofs and chimneys, arched windows, striped scalloped awnings over lit shop windows |
| Suburbs | pitched-roof houses with chimneys and cross windows, garden trees and bushes; snow on the roofs in winter |
| Midtown | mid-rises with cornices, piers, shop floors, water tanks; a café with an awning |
| Tech Park | glass boxes with mullions, floor lines, a daytime reflection streak, tenant logos, lit floors at night |
| Downtown | towers with set-back crowns, spires, dense window grids and neon bands that come on (and stutter) after dark |

Every building has two frames, the hour's lighting and a "twinkle" frame
where about one window in nine flips, on a slow `.toggle`. At night the city
lights up window by window rather than blinking all at once. Trees, bushes,
benches, the clock tower and the antenna are shaded for the hour too (the
old map left trees bright green at night). Seasonal weather covers the whole
map: snow in winter, falling leaves in autumn, blossom in spring. Lawns,
the park and the far bank follow the season.

**The traffic moves with `SimSpeed`.** `CityMapComposer.traffic(at:pace:time:)`
places five cars each way on the spine, two each way on each of the three
avenues, and the boat. Positions come from the scene clock × the game's
ticks per second: paused parks them where they stand, 1×/2×/4× run at
7/14/28 px/s. Under Reduce Motion they stay parked. Every lane wraps inside
the map, so nothing draws off the edge.

**The ambience is read from the game now.** Before, the app always drew the
map at noon, in summer, with a garage HQ. Now it uses the calendar's season
and the company's office tier, and the hour rolls on the office card's own
four-minute clock (`OfficeAmbience.timeOfDay`), starting at midday.

**Every thing on the map is a hit region with a label.** `layers(...)`
returns the sprites together with `regions: [CityHitRegion]`, built from the
same placements, so what is drawn and what is tapped cannot disagree.
`target(atX:y:in:)` returns the front-most region under the finger, falling
back to the district.

| Target | Drawn when | A second tap (or the panel button) opens |
|---|---|---|
| your office (the HQ building and its flag) | always | the HQ tab, where the office card is |
| your home (K6's pin) | the home has a district | the Life tab, where the Home card is |
| each rival's HQ building (up to 3 per district) and pin | `state.rivals`, placed in `Rival.homeDistrict` | that rival's `RivalProfileScreen`, pushed over the map (Back returns) |
| the five networking rooms: hacker house (Suburbs), co-working warehouse (Old Town), conference hotel bar (Midtown), demo hall (Tech Park), rooftop terrace (Downtown) | always; tonight's room gets a lit sign and a crowd | `NetworkingVenueSheet` when that room is open tonight |
| the hospital (Midtown) | `economy.hospitalizationDays` is not empty | Life → Assets (the doctor), via `Route.assets` |
| the courthouse (Old Town) | `crime.cases` is not empty | Life → the crime ledger, via `Route.crimeLedger` |
| the school (Suburbs; a playground otherwise) | a child is at the `.school` stage | Life → the children, via `Route.children` |
| the old office, boarded up with a "TO LET" board | the company has relocated | nothing (the panel explains) |

A first tap selects the thing's district and puts a pixel name plate over
it: the office's `OfficeFXSprites.nameTag`, e.g. "BRIGHTWATER / 2ND TAP:
PROFILE". A second tap opens it, so a stray tap never throws the player
out of a full-screen map. VoiceOver gets one element per region and opens
the thing directly. The district, office-flag and home-pin elements are
unchanged.

**The panel gains "who's here".** Selection and every existing row are
unchanged (hide, never remove). Added:

- the rival chips are now buttons that open the profile;
- a focus row for the named thing, with its action button;
- a **Visit** row for the district's room, in one of three states: "Open
  tonight: 5 people, 3 conversations left, closes in 2 days" with **Go in**;
  "Tonight's room is the Rooftop Party, in Downtown."; or "Nothing on
  tonight…" with **Plan a weekend**, which goes to Life;
- an "Around here" line listing the district's amenities, including the
  hospital, courthouse, school and old office when they are drawn.

The rent pill and the commute-from-home line were already on the panel and
stay as they are.

**Read, don't invent.** No `GameState` field was added and no engine file
changed. The rivals' districts come from the existing `Rival.homeDistrict`
(stored `hqDistrict`, else derived from `appearanceSeed`). The old office
comes from the event log: the district before the latest `.officeRelocated`,
or the founding Old Town when there has been only one move. All readers are
in `CityMapModel.swift` as `GameState` extensions on the app side.

**Debug flags** (`DebugLaunch`, between S4 markers):

- `-autoCity rich` dresses the `-autoFixture` save before it is installed:
  a hospital stay, a settled case, two relocations and a rooftop party open
  tonight.
- `-autoCityDistrict <district>` opens the map on that district's panel,
  scrolled to it.
- `-autoCityFocus <office|home|rival|venue|hospital|courthouse|school|former>`
  opens it with that thing's name plate up.

`-autoRoute city` still lands, and `router.take(.city)` in `OfficeCard` is
untouched.

## Measurements

Measured by hand with a scratch release-build harness on the Mac (the
`OfficePerfTests` pattern: compose, then draw every placement into a 3×
`CGContext`), before = a copy of PixelKit at `scaffold-16`. No test added.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Scene | 224×160 | 320×224 |
| Placements, one frame | 36 (34 unique sprites) | 126 static compose; 134 in a live frame with every landmark (88 unique) |
| Compose, warm | **6.7–7.1 ms every compose** (the ground plate was rebuilt uncached each time) | **0.11 ms** for `layers` (ground cached per hour × season) |
| Compose, cold | 14–15 ms | 15 ms, once per (reading, hour), then memoized in `CityMapSceneCache` |
| Per-frame work in the app | the whole compose on every body eval | 0.012 ms (cached layers + traffic at t) |
| Draw @3× on the CPU (`CGContext`) | 0.8–0.9 ms | 2.7 ms (2× the area, plus the full-scene weather layer); in the app this goes through `Canvas` |
| Hit regions | **5** (the district rects) | **18** on a typical board (office building + flag, home pin, 5 rival buildings + 5 pins, 5 rooms), **22** with every landmark, plus the 5 district fallbacks |

At 12 fps with a 3× scale on the iPhone the per-frame cost is the draw. The
composition itself is now two orders of magnitude under the old cost.

**None.** There are no pinned city PNGs. `CityPreviewPNGTests` writes its
previews (`city_<hour>.png`, `city_hq_<tier>.png`, `city_<season>.png`,
`hq_growth.png`) without comparing them. Their content changed (the new city)
and their suite passes unchanged. The invariants they check all hold at the
new size:

- everything stays inside the map;
- exactly one HQ, growing with the tier;
- three rival HQs for the fixture's seeds;
- exactly two `traffic` placements (the spine lanes; the new cars are `car`);
- the rects partition the map;
- the office marker sits in its district;
- the output is deterministic;
- every colour is on the master palette.

Two pinned app facts in `ReleasePlumbingTests` stayed true without editing
the test:

- `mapScale(1024) == 4`: the scale ladder is now keyed to the pinned 224-px
  reference width (`scaleReferenceWidth`), so every device keeps its scale
  and the bigger city pans.
- `panelClearance(3) == 280` / `(4) == 373`: unchanged. The who's-here block
  adds its own `whoIsHereClearance` on top.

The first run failed on exactly these three asserts before this fix.

## Deviations

- **Two taps to open**, not one: the first names, the second opens.
  VoiceOver and the panel buttons open in one step.
- **The office and the home open by switching tabs** (`router.tab = .hq` /
  `.life`) and dismissing the map. There is no `Route` for either root card,
  and the map is presented from those very cards.
- **Visit cannot pick the room.** The engine rolls the venue when a
  networking weekend resolves, and choosing one would be a new `GameAction`.
  The row says which room is open, or sends the player to plan a weekend.
- **The courthouse appears for any legal case**, criminal or a rival suit.
  The family court (W2) is not read.
- **No in-app school shot.** No bundled fixture has a child at school and
  lands on the map without a decision sheet over it. The school is in the
  PixelKit render `after-map-day-everything.png`.

## Follow-ups

1. **Pre-warm the next hour.** The first compose of each hour is about 15 ms
   (one dropped frame at the first roll of each hour, then cached). The next
   hour could be composed off the main thread before it arrives.
2. **The old office's district is inferred from the capped event log.** On a
   long run where the earlier relocation fell off the 500-entry log, it falls
   back to Old Town. A `previousDistrict` on `CityState` (encode-if-present,
   default nil) would make it exact. That is an engine change for its owner.
3. **Choose the room.** A `GameAction` to plan a networking weekend at a
   named venue would let *Visit* book the room it is on.
4. **`make strings`** at wrap time: the panel and the plates add new copy.
5. **iPad.** A 13" iPad keeps scale 4, so the 320-wide city is 1,280 points
   and pans there too. That is a choice; changing it means re-pinning
   `ReleasePlumbingTests` with the owner's word.
