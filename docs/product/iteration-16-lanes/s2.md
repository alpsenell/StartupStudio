# S2 — office downgrade

*Iteration 16, lane S2, branch `s2-downgrade` from `scaffold-16` (c88e04d).
The owner's ask: "make office downgrade option".*

## What was built

**Engine** (`OfficeDowngrade.swift`, new; `Balance/BalanceConfig+OfficeDowngrade.swift`, new)

- `GameAction.downgradeOffice` (handled in the reducer beside
  `.upgradeOffice`, inside `// MARK: S2` markers). It moves the office one
  tier down: campus → studio → loft → garage. No bot sends it.
- `GameState.officeDowngradeQuote(balance:)` holds every number the button
  and the sheet print, read from the same state the reducer reads:
  - rent now and after, in the same district, after the Ops discount;
  - property tax, when the office is owned;
  - the stored amenities' upkeep;
  - the sale price, when owned;
  - the move cost;
  - the morale drag, the room's own morale bonus before and after, and the
    morale the stored amenities give;
  - the reputation cost;
  - desks and build slots after the move;
  - whether launch events are lost;
  - every refusal.
  - `weeklySaved` = rent + tax + stored upkeep − the new rent. It can go
    negative when an owned office's tax is lower than the smaller office's
    rent, and the copy then says so ("costs $N more a week: rent again").
- **The price.**
  - The move is a flat cash cost: `moveCostBase` ($2,000) plus
    `moveCostRentWeeks` (2) × the old tier's listed rent in the district.
    That is $2,800 loft → garage, $5,600 studio → loft, $26,000 campus →
    studio (Old Town). I chose cash over "the day's build ×0" because cash
    prints as one number next to the rent it saves.
  - Everyone's morale target is `−moraleDrag` (5) for `moraleDragDays`
    (91), added in `EmployeeSystem.workplaceMoraleDelta` inside a marked
    pair beside K4's for-sale drag.
  - Reputation drops by `reputationCost` (3) on the day.
- **Owned office.** It is sold first through `CitySystem.sellOffice`, at
  today's `propertyValue`; the company then rents. This mirrors the upgrade.
- **Refusals, all listed, the first on the button:**
  - "Not during the earn-out: the buyer is paying for this office"
  - "Let N people go first: the Loft has 6 desks" (headcount includes the
    founder, like the cap)
  - "Ship N builds first: the Loft runs 2 at a time" (`buildsInFlight`,
    patches included)
  - "The move costs $X and there is $Y" (cash plus any sale proceeds)
  - In the garage the row does not exist.
- **Amenities the smaller tier cannot hold** (by `minTier`) move from
  `amenities` into `officeDowngrade.storedAmenities`. They are kept, pay no
  upkeep and have no effect: no morale, no quit-streak days, no founder
  health, no break. The next `.upgradeOffice` unpacks, free, whatever the
  new tier can hold (one marked call in `FinanceSystem.upgradeOffice`).
  Moving the storage flag this way means none of the five existing
  `ownedAmenities` reads had to change.
- **What moves on its own.** Rent (`balance.office(tier)`), the headcount
  cap, build slots, the launch-event minimum, the office morale bonus, the
  hiring tier gates and the PixelKit scene all read `officeTier` directly.
  - `milestonesReached` keeps every rung, so no goal is un-reached
    (`ProgressionSystem.reachedTier` already anticipated this).
  - Co-founders who already draw pay keep it.
- **Saved state.** One new field, `GameState.officeDowngrade:
  OfficeDowngradeState?` (move day, drag end day, stored amenities). It is
  decoded if present and encoded only when set.
- **The event.** `GameEvent.officeDowngraded(tier:day:)`, graded `.notable`.
- **The balance block.** `officeDowngrade { moveCostBase,
  moveCostRentWeeks, moraleDrag, moraleDragDays, reputationCost }` under
  `// MARK: S2` in `BalanceConfig.swift`, with every key optional. The keys
  are appended at the end of `Balance.json`.
- **The debug seed.** `GameAction.officeDowngradeDebugSeed(scenario:)`
  exists in DEBUG only, on the K7 pattern. It dresses the loaded save
  through the engine: fire the newest hires down to the smaller cap, ship
  the newest builds down to its slots, and for `owned`, own the office.

**App**

- `Screens/HQ/OfficeDowngradeRow.swift` (new), which holds three pieces:
  - `OfficeDowngradeSection` sits in `OfficeCard` inside one
    `// MARK: S2 (office downgrade)` / `// MARK: end S2` pair, right after
    the upgrade row.
    - The row reads "Move down: Loft", with "saves $1,995 a week · $5,600
      to move", "Morale target −23 (5 of it for 13 weeks) · reputation −3",
      and "Sells the office for $N…" when owned. The first refusal shows in
      the warning colour.
    - The Move down button stays tappable when refused (dimmed), because
      the sheet explains what would make it legal.
  - "In storage: Cafeteria, Shuttle Service and Gym". It stays on the card
    whenever something is boxed, including in the garage, and says what
    brings them back.
  - `OfficeDowngradeSheet` is a pixel-panel header plus four sections:
    What it saves, What it costs, What changes, and Not yet (the refusals).
    The move is priced on its own pixel button ("Move down to the Loft for
    $5,600"), with the cash after the move under it. "Stay put" cancels.
- `AmenitiesSheet` gets a marked pair: a stored amenity's button reads
  "In storage", and its reason reads "In storage since the move down: no
  upkeep, no effect. It comes back out at the Studio, free".
- `EventCopy` gets a marked pair: "Moved down to the Loft. The rent is
  smaller, and so, the team suspects, is the company." The paper uses the
  first sentence as the headline and the rest as the body. The HQ Journal
  row shows it (see `after-card-moved-row.png`).
- There is no Settings or Company-card row: the upgrade has none, so the
  move down lives only on the office card (rule 22).
- The office scene redraws at the smaller tier as soon as the move lands,
  not on the next day. The desk pill reads 6/6, and the celebration and
  the sound fire on the tier change as they already did.

## The measurement ("how it fails")

This is a throwaway executable in the scratchpad, not a test. It uses the
studio release fixture, dressed with the debug seed's `legal` scenario:
6 hires let go and 1 build shipped, leaving 6 people and 2 builds. It then
ticks two copies with no actions, one staying and one moving down on day
400.

| | cash | avg morale | reputation | weekly burn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| day 400, stay | $90,875 | 75.6 | 67.7 | $10,773 |
| day 400, moved | $85,275 | 75.6 | 64.7 | $8,323 |
| +28 d, stay / moved | $102,428 / $108,399 | 85.0 / 63.1 | 66.2 / 65.2 | |
| +91 d, stay / moved | $129,035 / $152,397 | 88.7 / 55.6 | 67.5 / 69.5 | |
| +182 d, stay / moved | $184,217 / $247,995 | 88.3 / 69.5 | 63.3 / 70.0 | |

- **The quote.** It saves $2,450 a week: rent $1,800 → $400, plus $1,050
  of upkeep. The move costs $5,600, so the move is paid back in about 2.3
  weeks. By a quarter it is +$23k, and by half a year +$64k.
- **What it costs.** The morale target drops 23 on the day: 5 of drag, 3
  from the smaller room, and 15 from the boxed cafeteria, shuttle and gym.
  - By a quarter the average morale is 55.6 against 88.7. That is still
    above the 32 quit threshold, and on this seed nobody walked (headcount
    6 vs 5).
  - When the drag ends it recovers to 69.5.
  - So both answers are defensible: money and runway against a team that
    is visibly less happy and slower.
- **A finding.** The first cut printed only the 5-point drag. The boxed
  amenities are the biggest morale cost and were not on the button, so the
  quote now carries `storedMoraleBonus`. The row and the sheet print the
  full −23, with "5 of it for 13 weeks".
- **Save checks.** The moved save round-trips through JSON with its
  `officeDowngrade` intact. A run that stayed does not encode the key.
- **Moving back up.** A move back up (with $100k added) unpacks all four
  amenities and empties storage.
- **As shipped, the studio fixture is refused on two counts.** "Let 6
  people go first: the Loft has 6 desks" and "Ship 1 build first: the Loft
  runs 2 at a time".

## Follow-ups

1. **Seating × downgrade (S1, at merge).** S1's seat map indexes desks by
   `SeatingLayout.deskCount(for: tier)` (studio 14, loft 6). A seat index
   stored at the studio can be out of range after a move down. S2's
   headcount refusal guarantees the people fit, not their seat numbers.
   The seat map should reseat, or drop, any desk ≥ the new count on
   `.officeDowngraded`, or read seats range-checked. The one-line glue
   belongs in S1's system.
2. **There is no shelve verb.** The brief's refusal was "Ship or shelve 2
   builds first", but the game has no way to shelve an in-flight build, so
   the copy says "Ship N builds first". A shelve action would be a
   product-lifecycle feature for another round.
3. **The "Amenities: N of 4 built" summary** in `OfficeCard` counts only
   the unpacked ones ("1 of 4 built" after a studio → loft move). The
   storage line directly below explains it. Changing that summary is
   outside S2's pair.
4. **Co-founders below the paid-from tier.** A company that moves back
   below `cofounderPaidFrom` (to the garage) keeps paying co-founders who
   already draw pay. The underpaid rule reads them as working for equity
   again, which is harmless because they are paid. An owner call whether
   a move to the garage should offer "back to equity".
5. **Before screenshots.** No before shots were taken. Before this lane,
   the card ends at the Next: row (`scaffold-16`) and there is no move
   down anywhere.
