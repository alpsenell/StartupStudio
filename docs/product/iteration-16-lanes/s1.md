## What was built

### Engine (`TycoonEngine`)

- **The plan.** `Company.seating: [UUID: Int]` (employee → grid desk),
  empty by default. `Company` lost its synthesized `Codable` for a
  hand-written one in a marked extension in `GameState.swift`: the same
  five keys in the same shape, plus `"seating"` as a desk-sorted array of
  `SeatingEntry` **only when non-empty**. Old saves decode an empty map.
- **The grid.** `SeatingLayout` (`Seating.swift`) mirrors PixelKit's
  desk counts (3 / 6 / 14 / 40) and columns (3 / 3 / 5 / 8). Neighbours are
  left and right in the same row. The desk *behind the founder's* is the
  last row's first; the desk *by the door* is the garage's last desk (the
  roller door) and elsewhere the last row's second (people come in at the
  front left past the founder's desk). The two are different desks in
  every tier.
- **`GameState.seatingPlan()`**: saved seats at desks that exist, then
  everybody else into the free desks by hire day (ties in payroll order).
  With an empty map this is PixelKit's own rule.
- **The actions.** `.seatingMove(employeeID:desk:)`: the first move writes
  the whole room as it sits into the map (so nobody else shifts), the mover
  goes to the desk and whoever sat there takes the mover's old desk.
  Refused with `seatingMoveBlocker` (office closed / they have left / the
  founder's desk is theirs / the tier has N desks / already there).
  `.seatingClear` empties the map. Events `.seatingMoved`,
  `.seatingCleared` (severity falls to the default `.info`).
- **The effects** (`Systems/SeatingSystem.swift`, one system appended to
  the reducer's list under `// MARK: S1`; returns at its first line while
  the map is empty; no draw anywhere):
  - *Mentor* (trait `mentor`) beside somebody weaker in the mentor's best
    skill: every week `relationships.mentorSkillGain × mentorGainScale ×
    gap / 100` (4.5 × gap / 100 shipped), never past the mentor's level.
    While teaching anybody, the mentor's own output × (1 − `mentorOutputCost`
    = 0.9): one marked factor in `EmployeeSystem.gatherCrewOutput` (so
    `BuildETA`, the Now card and the War Room read it) and one in contract
    output. Research output is not charged (follow-up).
  - *Grumbler*: each neighbour's morale −0.1 a day, applied to morale
    directly like `TraitSystem`'s room mood.
  - *Friends*: two neighbours with a bond ≥ 60 gain +2 bond a week; in
    `OfficeSecretsSystem` (only while `secrets.watching`) such a pair
    reweights the one existing start draw (romance and clique each in the
    pick twice), sorts first for the romance's pair, and is the clique's
    table. No draw added.
  - *Founder's neighbour*: +2 founder bond a week while the founder is in.
  - *Door desk*: `+doorPoachWeight` (60) on `RivalSystem.poachTarget`'s
    score, one marked line; the draw is untouched.
  - Weekly, the map forgets anybody who has left.
- **The preview.** `seatingPreview(employeeID:desk:balance:)` compares every
  effect in the room before and after the move and prints what starts and
  what stops, the mover's own first, plus the swap and the mentor's cost.
  Both the office and the manage sheet print exactly these lines.
- **Balance.** One defaulted block `BalanceConfig.seating`
  (`Balance/BalanceConfig+Seating.swift`, every key optional), `"seating"`
  appended at the end of `Balance.json`: `mentorOutputCost 0.1,
  mentorGainScale 1, grumblerMoraleDelta -0.1, friendBondGain 2,
  founderBondGain 2, doorPoachWeight 60`.

### PixelKit

- `OfficeSceneInput.seats` (empty by default) and `seatRegions` (off by
  default). `OfficeBehaviors.seating(for:)` takes the old path untouched
  when `seats` is empty; otherwise it seats the named occupants and fills
  the rest by the same rule. The director, the bug hunt, presence and the
  plans all go through it, so they follow the plan.
- `OfficeHitRegion.Kind.desk(Int)`: every grid desk's cell, furniture
  priority, only in `seatRegions` scenes. `pressed == .desk(i)` outlines the
  desk and monitor as the founder's desk already was. VoiceOver: "Desk 9,
  Freya Novak's" / "Desk 12, empty".

### App

- **Office card.** A *Desks* row under *Amenities* with what the plan is
  doing ("2 teaching · 1 pair of friends together", or "Where they sat
  down. Seat somebody and neighbours start to matter."). It turns on the
  move mode: taps pick a person (outlined), then a desk or a person to swap
  with (the desk outlined); the pixel strip under the scene says where they
  sit now, then prints the preview and **Move Emeka to desk 9** / *Not
  there*. *Let everyone sit anywhere* (a confirmation that counts what
  stops) tears the plan up. Bugs still squash in the move mode; nothing
  else opens.
- **Manage sheet.** A *Desk* section (a marked pair, after K3's): the
  desk, who is beside them, what their neighbours do to them now, the room
  as a pixel-framed grid in the office's own columns (initials, the door
  desk tinted), and on picking a desk the same preview on the move button.
- **Crew line.** K3's `LadderCrewLine` gains a second factor: "5 people ·
  ×0.71 each · lead: none · teaching: Sofia ×0.90". The lead's relief and
  the mentor's cost are separate factors in the engine and on the line.
- **Journal.** Lines for both events (`EventCopy`, S1 pair).
- **Newspaper photo** honours the plan (one marked line).

## Measurements

path (not a test, not committed), shipped balance, `release-studio-day400`.

**The PM's check: one mentor adjacency over 26 weeks** (Sofia Sato,
mentor, coding 96.1, beside Freya Novak, coding 29.3):

| | |
|---|---|
| The lesson alone, 26 weekly lessons | 29.3 → 75.9, **+46.6** (week 1 +3.0) |
| The full sim vs no plan, week 8 | 33.0 → 52.5, **+19.5** (Sato was poached in week 9 of that run — the bot answers no poach — so the curve flattens there) |
| Other pairs, lesson alone | Priya Rossi → Tariq (design 24.8): +26.6; Sato → Ingrid (63.1): +23.0; Priya → Aylin (60.4): +1.5 |

Far above 3 points, so the PM's fallback (double the gain, 20% cost) was
**not** applied. The trade on the Now card: a fresh build of Sato's
product ships in **13 days with no plan, 14 with her teaching** (crew points
13.45 → 12.85 a day, −4.5%). With Sato put on the Now card's own build
(`-autoSeating now` vs `dress-now`) the crew line shows the factor but the
remaining work rounds to the same "about 12 days" — the price is a day on a
whole build, not on the last week of one.

**The spec's other failure: is the best arrangement obvious?** Sato at
every desk on the same fixture: she teaches somebody at 11 of 13 desks
(her coding beats everyone's), from +0.5 a week (beside Liam) to +5.5
(desk 3, between Aylin and Sofia J.); the only ways to keep her full output
are an empty desk or the one behind the founder's, and the desk that
teaches Emeka (+3.6) is the door desk. So the choice is *who* learns and
*whether to pay*, not *where*; the arithmetic favours teaching: the
student's extra output passes the mentor's 10% after about 4–5 weeks. Left
as shipped because the lead's rule is "under 3 → double"; see follow-ups.

**Save round trip:** the fixture decodes and re-encodes byte-stable with
no `"seating"` key; a seated state round-trips its seven entries.

## Deviations

- Keys: six, not five — `mentorGainScale` (1.0) is the knob the PM's
  "double it" check would have turned, so the tuning is a JSON edit.
- "Grumbler drags the morale target": implemented as the spec's
  "−0.1 morale a day" on morale itself (a −0.1 *target* would be invisible
  against targets of 60 ± 12); same key, printed as "−0.1 morale a day".
- The founder's neighbour is the desk *behind* the founder's (the
  founder's desk stands alone in front of the grid); the founder cannot be
  moved.
- The effects run as their own system at the end of the reducer's list
  (under an S1 marker), not inside `TraitSystem` — same place in the day,
  no edit to `TraitSystem.swift`.
- A mentor teaches any neighbour weaker by a point or more; the gain is
  proportional to the gap, so a near-equal neighbour learns next to
  nothing (+0.1 a week) but still costs the 10%. The preview says so.
- `AppRouter` needed no S1 pair: the `s1-…` routes are read by the office
  card, like K6's.

## Follow-ups

1. **Owner call on the mentor's strength.** +46.6 points in 26 weeks is
   well past the PM's bar and makes teaching the default answer outside a
   ship window. `mentorGainScale 0.5` (≈ +28) or a gain on the student's
   headroom rather than the gap would make "seat the mentor by the flight
   risk" a real alternative. Not changed without a word.
2. Research output does not pay the mentor's 10% (products, patches and
   contracts do).
3. The desk grid in `SeatingLayout` duplicates PixelKit's capacities and
   columns (the engine cannot import PixelKit); a change to either must
   change both. An existing test does not pin it and none was added.
4. On moving to a bigger office the plan keeps its desk numbers, which sit
   somewhere else in the new grid; people keep their neighbours only in
   the garage → loft move (both three columns).
5. The share-card office photo goes through the newspaper's scene builder
   and honours the plan; the title screen's garage and the War Room scene
   do not (no plan to honour there).
6. `make strings` for the new copy at merge.
