# K4 — deals and exits

*Lane K4 of iteration 15, branch `k4-deals` from `scaffold-15`, simulator
`ws-l4`. Specs: meta A2 (the for-sale sign), meta A4 (sell up before the
receiver), company C5 (buy them with paper). No test was added.*

## What was built

### Engine (`Packages/TycoonEngine`)

- **`Deals.swift` (new, the lane's file).** `DealListing`, `DealBid`,
  `DealSellUpOffer`, `DealStockTerms`, and the pure queries the reducer and
  the app share: `dealListingBid(number:)`, `dealNextBid`, `dealNextBidDay`,
  `dealListingBlocker`, `dealMoraleTargetDrag`, `dealPoachFactor`,
  `dealLaunchHypeFactor`, `dealSellUpOffer`, `dealStockTerms(for:)`,
  `Rival.dealFounderName(content:)` (named from the stored appearance seed,
  no stream read), `RaisedRound.isDealPaper`, `LegacyRun.dealBankruptcyHaircut`.
- **The for-sale sign.** `GameAction.listForSale(askMultiple:)` (snapped to
  tenths inside 0.8–1.6×, ask stored in dollars) and `.takeDownSign`.
  `RivalsState.listing` is decode-if-present and encoded only when set.
  `RivalSystem.dealListingCheck` runs after `buyoutCheck`. It draws nothing:
  every 28 days, with nothing already on the desk, the strongest qualifying
  rival bids `valuation × (0.9 + 0.1 n)`, capped at the ask. The bid lands
  on `pendingBuyout` with `lastBuyoutWasStrategic = amount ≥ valuation`, so
  accept, decline and the earn-out answer it unchanged, and it ends as
  *Acquired* at or above 1.0× and *Sold up* below. With no buyer,
  the strongest rival bids the liquidator's 0.5×; a weak company (in debt,
  cash < $5k or reputation < 20, as `buyoutCheck` reads weak) gets only
  the liquidator.
- **The bleed.**
  - **Morale:** a target drag of −1 per week listed, capped at −12. One
    marked line in `EmployeeSystem.workplaceMoraleDelta`, exactly 0 with no
    sign up. The first version was a direct −1/week hit; it measured as
    nothing (see below), so it moved to the target.
  - **Poach:** ×1.5, one marked factor in `poachCheck`; the draw is
    unchanged.
  - **Launch hype:** ×0.9 at ship, one marked line in
    `ProductSystem.ship`, K2's file.
  - **Board:** +5 pressure when the sign goes up and with every bid, if
    there is a board.
  - **The paper** leads with it (app).
- **Sell up.** `GameAction.sellUp`, open from the first day in the red. The
  price is valuation × 0.5 × 0.9^(whole weeks in debt), paid by the
  strongest rival, or ×0.4 by "the liquidator" with no rival on the board.
  It ends the run `.soldUp` with `lastBuyoutWasStrategic = false`, and the
  reason reads "Sold on day N of 21 in the red …". `distressFraction` is
  extracted from `buyoutCheck` with the same expression and the same draw.
  The shipped 0.5× is the measured start (below); `deals.sellUpFraction:
  null` falls back to the extracted midpoint, 0.9.
- **The bankruptcy haircut.** `LegacyLedger.record` (a K4 pair) runs
  `dealBankruptcyHaircut(20)` for a `.bankruptcy` ending: the carried people
  and the longest-serving lose 20 rapport. `LegacyPerson.carriedHaircut`
  (optional, never written when nil) records it. `applyHeirloom` (a K4
  pair) lands such a person at 60 − 20 = 40 instead of the flat 60.
  - **Deviation:** the spec's claim that "the ledger's people carry with
    their rapport" is not true in the repo. Heirlooms always arrive at a
    flat 60, so without the arrival line the haircut would only have
    changed the number printed on the Heirlooms page.
- **Buy them with paper.** `GameAction.acquireRivalForStock(rivalID:)`.
  - **Price:** equity = 1.3 × their valuation / ours × 100, rounded up to
    the tenth. Refused over 25 points, if the founder would keep under 20,
    under the unchanged 1.5× dominance gate, or in an epilogue.
  - **The seat:** their founder becomes a `RaisedRound` with `amount 0`,
    `takesBoardSeat`, `expects .shipCadence`, patience 26 and id
    `deal-paper-<rival>`, minted inside `RivalSystem`, not `Investors.swift`.
    If it is the first seat, the quarter's clock starts today, as a first
    board round's does; no pressure is forgiven, since no cheque came.
  - **The contact:** a `.founder` contact at rapport 60, minted from the
    rival's `appearanceSeed` on a private `SeededRNG`.
  - **The rest:** team, shelf, reputation and stats come through
    `absorbRival`, the tail now shared with the cash `acquireRival`. That
    split is pure code motion; the cash path's draws are unchanged.
    `InvestorBot` still sends only the cash acquisition.
  - *Still yours* closes by itself (`equityRemaining < 100`), and the
    button says so.
- **Queue.** `QueueKind.sellUp` (last in the kind order) and its entry while
  `dealSellUpOffer` is non-nil: critical, `respondByDay` = the last day of
  grace. It is listed only when the balance is passed (the decision sheet,
  and the rail through it).
- **Balance.** One defaulted block, `BalanceConfig.deals` (K4 marker), with
  its struct in `Balance/BalanceConfig+Deals.swift` and the `"deals"` key
  appended at the end of `Balance.json`. It holds 21 keys, every one read
  only behind a player verb or on a bankrupt ledger record.
- **Events** (K4 region): `dealListed`, `dealSignTakenDown`, `dealSoldUp`,
  `dealPaperSigned`. A bid reuses `.buyoutOffered`; a sell-up to a rival
  also posts `.companySold`, so the childhood memory, the timeline and the
  rival history see the sale.

## Measurements (the "how it fails" checks)

Run as a scratch SwiftPM executable in the session scratchpad against this
branch's packages, never committed and not a test.

### A2 — the sign on `release-campus-day900`

The company is worth $2.15M at reputation 76, with 35 staff at morale 100.
It dominates Vantage Point 3.3× and the other three studios 13–27×.

The sign was listed at 1.0×, 1.3× and 1.6× for 30 weeks, declining every
bid below the ask. Poach offers were left to auto-resolve, and each run was
compared against the same days with no sign.

| ask | sold | morale at end (control) | poached (control) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0× | week 8, $2,151,053, Acquired | 91.7 (100.0) | 1 (0) |
| 1.3× | week 24, $2,796,369, Acquired | 98.0 (99.2) | 2 (0) |
| 1.6× | the 1.6× bid landed week 28 (not taken in the run) | 96.8 (99.1) | 2 (0) |

- **Poach factor.** People leave at 1.6× under ×1.5, so the brief's
  fallback (raise to ×2) was not triggered. ×2 gave 3 poached against 0 at
  1.6×.
- **Direct hit vs target drag.** With the first version (a −1/week direct
  morale hit), the campus lost only 1.6 points over 26 weeks: the 4%/day
  pull toward a target above 100 erased it. That is why the drag moved to
  the target.
- **Studio fixture** (`release-studio-day400`, valuation $572k, morale
  88.5):

  | ask | sold | morale at end (control) | poached (control) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1.0× | week 12 | 77.5 (88.9) | 4 (1) |
  | 1.3× | week 20 | 67.5 (89.2) | 5 (1) |
  | 1.6× | week 24 | 63.8 (89.2) | 5 (1) |

  The bleed bites there.

**The real failure mode is not the bleed; it is the "wait" answer.** On
every late fixture, `buyoutCheck` keeps rolling unsolicited strategic offers
at 1.5–2.5× every four to six weeks, whether or not a sign stands:
- the campus: 2.12× at week 3, 1.62× at week 7, 1.98× at week 20;
- the studio: 2.48× at week 1.

Wherever the sign has a strategic bidder, waiting beats every sign bid
(1.0→1.6×). The sign pays only where the approach never comes: reputation
under 60, or no studio the company is worth 2× of.

For that regime I widened the bidder pool to also include any rival worth
≥ 1.5× the company (`deals.buyerSizeFactor`). That is the PM's literal
wording, "the strongest rival whose valuation is ≥ 1.5× yours". The repo's
strategic bar (the parenthetical) is the other direction: the company worth
2× them. The pool is now the union of both.

The garage (`release-garage-day40`, reputation 14, weak) gets only
liquidator bids at 0.5×, as specified. See follow-up 1.

### A4 — does anything save a company in its grace period?

Ten pacing seeds × six bots (solo-slow, crunch-hire, saas-builder,
neglectful, runaway-raise, investor), 730 days each:

| rivals | bankrupt runs | with any inflow in the last 21 days | with a non-sales cash event | grace periods survived |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| off (the pacing suites' balance) | 16 | 16 (sales) | **1** ($1,000 milestone reward, which saved nothing) | 1 of 17 |
| on | 10 | 10 (sales) | **1** (the same) | 3 of 13 |

That is under 2 in 10, so by the PM's rule the gamble is fake and the
sell-up price starts at **0.5×** (`deals.sellUpFraction`, shipped). It
falls to 0.45×, 0.405× and 0.36× over the three weeks of grace. The
extracted midpoint (0.9×) remains the fallback when the key is null.

## Deviations

1. **The bidder pool** is the strategic pool ∪ any rival ≥ 1.5× the
   company, as described under A2.
2. **The morale bleed** is a target drag capped at −12, not a literal
   weekly subtraction, which measured as nothing.
3. **Heirloom arrival reads the haircut** (the repo arrived everyone at a
   flat 60).
4. **Sell-up starts at 0.5×**, per the A4 measurement.
5. **A bid is skipped** when another offer is on the desk that day; the
   next interval's bid is the higher one.

## Follow-ups

1. **The sign is dominated wherever the strategic approach can reach.**
   Unsolicited 1.5–2.5× offers arrive every few weeks on every late
   fixture. Two options, both outside this lane:
   - an owner call to cap unsolicited approaches at the ask while a sign
     stands ("you told the market your price"), which touches
     `buyoutCheck`;
   - or raise `askMax` / `bidStep` so a sign can reach 2×.
2. **C5's acquirer check** (always pay paper; is the founder ousted by day
   1095?) needs a scratch policy. If not, lower `stockMaxEquity` to 15 or
   raise the paper seat's pressure.
3. **The stat pills** on the sell-up sheet truncate at iPhone width ("Cash
   -$8,7…"). This is the existing three-pill layout (U1's); the values
   could be shortened to "$8.7k".
4. **The newspaper's small print** could carry "FOR SALE: asking $X" in
   the latest issue. It needs a K4 pair inside J5's expression line.
