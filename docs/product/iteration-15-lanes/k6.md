# K6 — home and rooms

*Iteration 15, lane K6, branch `k6-home` from `scaffold-15`, simulator
`ws-l6`. Specs: `iteration-15-pm/life.md` §3 (F3 Where you live) and §7
(F6 Take them with you), `iteration-15-pm/meta.md` §8 (A8 The rooms
answer). No test added; no pin moved.*

## What was built

### Engine (`TycoonEngine`)

- **Where you live (F3).** `LifeState.homeDistrict: DistrictID?` (nil on
  every run before this round and on every bot; decode-if-present, encoded
  only when set). The weekly rent debit and `livingCosts` read
  `GameState.homeWeeklyRent` = the tier's rent × `city.districts[home].rentMultiplier`
  — exactly the tier's rent when the district is nil. A far home
  (`home.farPairs`: suburbs↔downtown, suburbs↔techPark, oldTown↔techPark,
  either way round) takes `home.commuteEvenings` = 1 evening off
  `eveningsPerWeek` on the schedules in `home.commuteSchedules`, never the
  week's last one. `.moveHome(district:)` costs `home.moveRentWeeks` = 2
  weeks of the new rent and an evening, is refused with reasons
  (`homeMoveBlocker`: you live here / away / no evenings / short by $N),
  and writes a "moved" memory for every child at school or a teenager.
  Public reads for the sheets: `homeMoveQuotes` (both columns per
  district), `commute(home:office:)` (what an office move would do),
  `homeCommute`. New files: `HomeRooms.swift` (queries),
  `Systems/HomeSystem.swift` (the three actions and the holiday's weekend),
  `Balance/BalanceConfig+Home.swift`.
- **Take them with you (F6).** `.planFamilyHoliday` plans the vacation
  with `LifeState.familyHoliday = true` (refused with nobody to take). The
  weekend it resolves (`LifeSystem.runWeekly`, one marked branch):
  `$1,500 + $600` a head (partner + each child), the vacation's health,
  mood and relationships with energy **+25** instead of +40, affection
  **+20** and `lastPartnerDay`, each child's bond **+8** and a "holiday"
  memory, the same seven days away (reason "Family holiday"), the plan
  back to rest. Seeing them counts as seeing people (loneliness lifts;
  a chronic streak resets as any vacation's does). `planWeekend` clears the
  flag, so any other plan is the founder's own. A family holiday planned
  by a founder who has since lost the household goes as the solo vacation.
  `vacationAffectionCost` prints the solo row's cost.
- **The rooms answer (A8), engine half.** `.callBreak(amenity:)` in a
  built game room, cafeteria or gym: every hired employee's morale **+3**,
  the founder's energy **+5** in the gym, and the next day's work on every
  build × **0.5** — one marked factor in `ProductSystem.applyDailyProgress`
  (× 1.0, no bit moved, on every other day). Once per 7 days
  (`GameState.lastBreakDay`, encoded only when set). Refused with reasons
  (`roomBreakBlocker`: not built / nobody to give a break to / away / next
  break in N days). The morale lands in `HomeSystem.callBreak` directly,
  the way `relocateOffice` moves morale — so **no edit to K3's
  `EmployeeSystem.swift`** was needed.
- **Balance.** One defaulted block `BalanceConfig.home` (struct in
  `BalanceConfig+Home.swift`, every key optional), `"home"` appended at the
  end of `Balance.json`: 13 keys.

### App

- **Home card.** `HomeSceneView` now gets `onTapRegion` and a hint per
  region (`Screens/Life/HomeTaps.swift`, `HomeTapDestination`): the founder
  → the Today sheet; the partner → their people menu; a child → theirs;
  the bed → the work schedule; the couch and the television → cinema;
  the fridge, the cooker and the dining table → eat out; the dumbbells and
  the yoga mat → a gym session (each asked first, with "Cinema · $40 · −1
  evening of 3" on the button, or why not); the suitcase → the sabbatical;
  the crib → the family's page; the laundry, the takeaway and the dead
  plant → the meters with the room's line ("The plant did not make it.
  Mood 31."); a decor slot → furnish; the window → the city map. The lamp,
  bookshelf, fireplace, armchair, plant and flowers open nothing.
- **One home per thing.** The Today grid is now `ActivitiesGrid`, drawn
  in `TodaySheet`; Life's "Today" card is a row in the same place that
  opens the same sheet ("2 evenings left · or tap yourself at home"). The
  grid has one home and two doors.
- **Where you live.** A row on the Home card ("Tech Park · $1,120/wk ·
  far · −1 evening of 3") opens `HomeMoveSheet`: every district with its
  two columns side by side — *Rent: $490/wk, this costs 245% of your
  salary* and *Evenings: −1 evening of 3, far: the commute eats it* — and
  the move priced on its button ("Move for $980 and an evening"). The
  upgrade row and its confirmation print the next home's rent in the
  district. `EveningPips` (every evening strip) subtract the commute and
  say "· −1 commute".
- **Weekend card.** *Family holiday* sits beside *Vacation* once there is
  a partner or a child: "$3,300 · Away a week together · energy +25 (not
  +40) · affection +20 · kids' bond +8". The vacation row finally prints
  **"Affection −4 while you are away"** for a founder with a partner.
- **Office.** `OfficeSceneInput.roomRegions` (set by the office card)
  makes the plants, the windows and the built amenity zones tappable: a
  plant opens the morning papers (whose one tap the plant is the floor
  of), a window the city map, an amenity the amenities sheet.
- **Amenities sheet.** Under a built game room, cafeteria or gym: *Call a
  break · everyone +3 morale · a day's work on every build ×0.5* (gym:
  "+5 your energy"), and under it the Now card's sentence for the build in
  flight tomorrow, both ways — one day of the real reducer on two copies of
  the state (`RoomBreakPreview`): "Round 15: Could ship now · done in about
  17 days → with a break, could ship now · done in about 18 days". "Next
  break in 3 days" when it is refused.
- **City.** A small house beside the office flag in the district the
  founder lives in (`CityDistrictInfo.hasPlayerHome`, default false), a
  VoiceOver element for it, "your home" in the district's label, a "Your
  home" chip on the panel, and the commute the office would create printed
  on the panel and in the move confirmation: "Your commute from Suburbs if
  you move here: far · −1 evening of 3."

## The numbers

### F3's "how it fails" check: the two columns on the four fixtures

Every fixture's office is in Old Town and every founder is on a $200
salary. From the engine (`homeMoveQuotes`), studio flat unless noted:

| District | Rent | Share of $200 | Commute from Old Town | Commute from Downtown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old Town | $120 | 60% | near | near |
| Suburbs | $84 | 42% | near | **far · −1 evening of 3** |
| Midtown | $156 | 78% | near | near |
| Tech Park | $192 | 96% | **far · −1 evening of 3** | near |
| Downtown | $264 | 132% | near | near |
| House (`l3-family-day900`, wallet $18,400) | $490 (suburbs) – $1,540 (downtown) | 245% – 770% | as above | as above |

Neither column is absurd on its own: downtown at 132% of a $200 salary is
the "near and dear" the PM meant, and at house tier it is 770% — the
salary knob (and K1's pipes) come into it, which is the point. But the
evening column wins every far pair at studio tier: the cheapest far home
saves **$36 a week** against Old Town (a sixth of the salary) for **one of
three evenings**. Remedy applied as the PM wrote it: a far commute bites on
`normal` and `crunch` only (`commuteSchedules`), so chill absorbs it; with
crunch already at its floor of 1, the far home costs an evening only on
the normal week:

| Schedule | Evenings, near | Far (shipped) | Far (bites on all three) |
|---|---|---|---|
| chill | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| normal | 3 | **2** | 2 |
| crunch | 1 | 1 | 1 |

One more finding the table makes plain: **from Old Town the suburbs are
both the cheapest and near**, so the home choice has no trade-off until
the office moves to Downtown or Tech Park — at which point the relocation
panel prints the far commute it creates, which is where the household
decision lands (follow-up 5).

### A8's check: a weekly break on `release-studio-day400`

Three builds in flight; break called on day 400 in the game room, then
one and seven days of the reducer with and without it (`daysToShippable`
/ `daysToComplete`):

| Build | Day 400 | +1 day, no break | +1 day, break | +7 days, no break | +7 days, break |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round 15 | 0 / 18 | 0 / 17 | **0 / 18** | 0 / 11 | **0 / 12** |
| Round 16 | 3 / 29 | 2 / 28 | **3 / 28** | 0 / 22 | 0 / 22 |
| Round 17 | 4 / 22 | 3 / 21 | 3 / 21 | 0 / 15 | 0 / 15 |

The Now card's sentence for the lead build moves ("Could ship now · done
in about 17 days" → "about 18 days") the next day and is still a day
behind a week later; Round 16's ship gate slips a day. Average morale
88.5 → 91.5. So ×0.5 moves the sentence and stays; the PM's fallback
(a full day at ×0) was measured too — it moves Round 16's completion one
day more (29 vs 28) — and not applied.

### F6: the solo row's cost

`vacationAffectionCost` = 2 × `relationships.affectionDrift` (−0.3) × 7
days = **−4.2 → "Affection −4 while you are away"**, the number the PM
quoted. The family holiday on `l3-family-day900` (married, two children):
**$3,300** for 3 heads.

## Rules kept

- **Identity at the default.** `homeDistrict` nil → multiplier 1.0 and no
  commute; `lastBreakDay` nil → factor 1.0; `familyHoliday` false → the
  weekend resolves as before. None of the three fields is encoded at its
  default. The four engine fixtures and three release fixtures load and
  stand (the byte-identical and pacing suites are in the 943).
- **No draws.** Nothing in `HomeSystem`, `HomeRooms` or the weekend branch
  touches `rng` or `worldRNG`. A called break changes the day's points and
  so the number of bug rolls that day — only in a run where a player
  called one (the A5 precedent).
- **No bot sends a new action.** `WeekendActivity.allCases` is not used by
  any roll (checked: only `WeekendCard`, `BalanceConfigTests` and the
  `LifeSystemTests` pin) — and no case was added to it anyway (deviation 1).
- **No new tests; no re-pins.** Only the app's snapshot PNGs are written
  to the temp directory; nothing pinned moved.
- **Markers.** Every insertion in a scaffolded or shared file sits inside
  a `// MARK: K6 (home and rooms)` pair.

## Deviations from the spec

1. **The family holiday is a flag, not a `WeekendActivity` case.**
   `LifeSystemTests` pins `WeekendActivity.allCases` as a literal list and
   `BalanceConfigTests` requires every case to have a `life.activities`
   entry; a new case would have been a re-pinned test and a key inside
   another block. The repo wins: `LifeState.familyHoliday` on a planned
   `.vacation` does the same job and old saves decode the plan as before.
2. **No `GameEvent`.** The move and the break post no event:
   `GameEvent.severity` is an exhaustive switch with no K-lane region. The
   app reads the state the action changed and says so in a toast; the
   family holiday reuses `.weekendSpent(.vacation)` and `.founderAway`
   ("Family holiday"). Follow-up 3.
3. **Memories are raw kinds.** "moved" and "holiday" are written as
   `ChildMemory.kind` strings, not `ChildMemoryKind` cases: that enum is
   switched over in K7's `FamilyDrama.swift` and `FamilyDramaScreen.swift`
   this round. Every grading reader skips a kind it does not know, so the
   two memories are shown on the child's ledger (default mark) and graded
   by nobody. Follow-up 4.
4. **The break's morale is not a call in `EmployeeSystem`.** It lands in
   `HomeSystem.callBreak` on the employees directly (as `relocateOffice`
   does); K3's file is untouched.
5. **The office plant opens the morning papers** rather than performing
   the desk's tap itself: the tap belongs to the desk (one home), and the
   plant is the tap's own floor (`DeskTapKind.plant`).
6. **Files touched outside the ownership table**, each inside a K6 pair:
   `Screens/Life/ActivitiesCard.swift` (the row + `ActivitiesGrid`),
   `Components/EveningPips.swift` (the commute), PixelKit
   `OfficeSceneView.swift` (the input flag and three VoiceOver names),
   `CityMapComposer.swift` and `CitySpriteLibrary.swift` (the pin).

## Follow-ups

1. **K1 — the rent where the founder lives, in three places.**
   `checkEviction`'s `rent * 2` floor, `Screens/Life/MoneyCard.swift:21`
   and `Components/MoneySheet.swift:63` read the tier's rent
   (`homeWeeklyRent(life.home, …)`); after a move to Tech Park the money
   card still says "Rent $120/wk" while the wallet pays $192 (seen on
   `release-campus-day900`). Each should read
   `state.homeWeeklyRent(balance:)` — identical with no district.
   `livingCosts`, which `checkEviction` also reads, already does.
2. **K7 — the away doubling on a family holiday.**
   `RelationshipSystem.driftAffection` doubles the drift while the founder
   is away, including the week the partner is with them; skipping it when
   `awayReason == "Family holiday"` would make the family row's +20 the
   week's net (it is about +16 today).
3. **A journal line for the move and the break** needs a K6 arm in
   `GameEvent.severity` (a scaffold region there, next round).
4. **`ChildMemoryKind.moved` / `.holiday`** with their marks and grades,
   once K7's switches can take them.
5. **The Old Town office has no home trade-off** (the suburbs are cheapest
   and near). Product call: a home-side perk per district (a garden for
   the children in the suburbs, a mood bonus downtown), or re-cut the far
   pairs against the map's geometry (Old Town and Midtown are as far apart
   as Suburbs and Downtown on the drawn map).
6. **Copy.** U1's office coach tip still says "Tap anyone — or the
   whiteboard, the coffee machine, the door"; the plant, the windows and
   the amenities answer now.
