## What shipped

Beside *Walk away* on the life-score screen there is now a second answer,
**Hand it to…**. It opens a sheet that lists everyone who passes the
caretaker gate (26 weeks, bond 50; the others are greyed with their reason)
and offers a 10 / 25 / 50 % keep. Before the tap the sheet prints every
consequence:

- what you keep, in points and in dollars today, and that it carries no
  seat, no vote and no salary
- the ledger's number for you (*Walked away*, at what you keep)
- what the successor holds, their weekly draw, and that they start single
  in a studio flat with eight weeks of their pay
- that the company keeps its cash, products, rivals, case and debts
- that the run becomes unranked
- that *Still yours* is closed to the successor until they buy your stake
  back, with today's price

**Engine** (`HandOver.swift`, and one action
`handOverKeys(successorID:keptPercent:predecessorRunID:)`). A pure state
rewrite on one action: no stream is read, and no bot sends it.

- **Gates**: `walkAwayBlocker`'s gates minus the two life gates (net worth
  and life score).
- **The kept stake**: a silent emeritus `RaisedRound`. It has no money in
  it, `takesBoardSeat false` and `patienceWeeks 0`, so `boardExpectations`
  never sees it and `buyBackRound` pays it out like any other round.
  `equityRemaining` drops by the kept points.
- **The old founder**: leaves payroll and becomes a `Contact`
  (`.formerCompany`, role founder, rapport = the successor's bond).
- **The successor**: their row becomes the founder (`isFounder`, role
  `.founder`, salary moved to `life.founderSalary`). `progression.founder`
  takes their name, trade-derived archetype and face. They get a fresh
  `LifeState` with a wallet of 8 × their pay.
- **The run**: `lineage = .employee` pointing at the outgoing founder's run,
  and `mode = .custom`. The old founder's will is cleared.
- **The ledger**: `LegacyRun.successorEmployeeID` (optional, decodes absent).
  `LegacyLedger.recordHandOver` records the outgoing founder through the
  ordinary `record` as `.walkedAway`, at wallet + things + the kept slice,
  and opens no stake rung. There is no eighth `EndingKind`.

**App.**
- `GameSession.handOverKeys` records the ledger from the state *before* the
  send, and only if the engine accepted.
- **Dynasty**: the row says "Handed the keys to X and kept a silent stake"
  (or "…the company is still running" until the continuation ends). The
  continuation hangs under the run by `lineage`.
- **Biography and Dynasty lineage line**: "Took the keys to X from Y."
- **Cap table**: a **KEPT STAKE** chip.
- **Journal**: "X has the keys. Y kept 21.8% and no seat."
- **Life card**: "You could hand it over".

**Two stale facts.**
- `HeirloomsStep` now counts `GameCenterCatalog.endings` (seven) instead of
  printing "6".
- `Unlocks.earnedLooks` gains *Walked away*'s face (`0x3A1C_0007_5EED_1077`).

## Measurement — the PM's "how it fails"

As shipped, `l3-family-day900` cannot hand over. It has **four seated board
rounds** (Northgate, Lantern, Corvus, Helion), and **every employee is at
bond 0** with the founder on day 905. `release-campus-day900` is the same.
The debug preparation (`DebugLaunch.k5Prepared`, the same one the
`-autoRoute k5…` flags use) buys the seated rounds out for $0 and puts the
longest-serving person at bond 60. I then handed over to Kenji Vasquez at
25 % in a scratch harness over `Reducer.tick` and counted the distinct
`QueueBoard.entries` raised over the next 28 days:

| Fixture | Without hand-over | After hand-over |
|---|---|---|
| `l3-family-day900` | 5 | **5** |
| `release-campus-day900` | 5 | 5 |
| `release-studio-day400` | 4 | refused ("Too soon. 20 more weeks") |

On `l3-family-day900` the five are: a price war (day 905), a category
challenge (910), a buyout offer (922), and two story beats (924, 931).
That is **five, not under three**, so the remedy (J3's studios treating new
management as a boom for a quarter) is **not built**. The item lists match
exactly with and without the hand-over, which is also evidence that the
action moves no stream.

After the hand-over: Kenji holds 65.25 % (87 × 0.75), and Ines keeps
21.75 % as a silent round. His wallet is $10,352 (8 × $1,294), his life
score is 20 (single, studio flat), and the mode is `custom`.

## Deviations from the spec

- **The action carries `predecessorRunID`.** The spec wrote
  `handOverKeys(successorID:keptPercent:)`. The engine does not mint ids,
  so the app passes the id it gives the outgoing founder's `LegacyRun`, and
  the new `lineage` points at it.
- **Four gates beyond walk-away's.** Refused while:
  - the founder is away or on sabbatical
  - an acquirer holds an earn-out
  - a founder who took the keys by hand-over has held them under 26 weeks
    (read off the newest emeritus round's day)
  - the chosen successor has a poach, staff question or notice open
- **The emeritus round's `expects` is `.profitability`.** `BoardExpectation`
  has no `.none` case. Nobody grades it, because the round is unseated.
- **A hand-over inserts `.walkedAway` into `endingsReached`** (through
  `record`), so it earns that ending's trophy and face. It does **not** open
  a stake rung: `highestStakeWon` is restored.
- **The walk-away confirm button now reads "Walk away".** It said "Hand over
  the keys", which is now the other door's name.
- **`SaveSummary+GameState.swift` needed no change.** It already reads the
  founder's row and profile, which the rewrite updates.
- **Shared files outside my list, each in its own marked pair:**
  - `Systems/ProgressionSystem.swift`: `roundsRaised` excludes emeritus
    rounds, so a hand-over cannot complete a "raise N rounds" goal
  - `ReleaseFixtures.swift`: the fixture passes through `k5Prepared` under
    the K5 flags; the pair is plus one `let` → `var`
  - `Screens/Business/InvestorsView.swift`: the KEPT STAKE chip

## Follow-ups

- **Bond carries over.** The other employees keep their bond with the *old*
  founder, so the successor inherits the room's goodwill (and its output
  bonus). Resetting it would be the harsher, arguably truer answer; left as
  is because it moves output.
- **Company-side state stays, as the spec says** (the case, debts, rivals):
  - `familyDrama` also stays: the ex's slice, and an open confrontation or
    funeral with a founder who is now single.
  - `fame` and the crime record stay with the company.
- **The emeritus round reads as a round in three places:**
  - `OfficeSecretsSystem` (the union drive's "recent round")
  - `CrimeSystem` (cook-the-books "something to overstate")
  - `TimelineMarkers`
- **A child intern on the roster** is left there after the life resets.
- **Bot-played fixtures sit at bond 0 everywhere.** In practice the caretaker
  gate, not the board, is what shuts this door. Worth a look if the verb
  should be reachable without mentoring.
- **The new face's seed was not curated by eye** as the other six were.
- **`companyValuation` is untouched**, as the direction doc asks. There was
  no need there.
