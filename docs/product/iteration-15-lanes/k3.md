# K3 — the ladder

*Lane K3 of iteration 15, branch `k3-ladder` from `scaffold-15`, simulator
`ws-l3`. Specs: `iteration-15-pm/company.md` §3 (C3 Leads run the room)
and §4 (C4 Options instead of pay).*

## What was built

### Engine

- **`Employee`** gains `leadSinceDay: Int?`, `grantedEquity: Double`,
  `grantDay: Int?`, `salaryBeforeGrant: Int?` — decoded if present, each
  encoded only when set, so a roster nobody promoted or granted writes the
  bytes it wrote before.
- **Leads run the room** (`Ladder.swift`, `EmployeeSystem`):
  - `promote` to lead writes `leadSinceDay`; `demote` clears it. Only a
    *promoted* lead counts (`Employee.isPromotedLead`). Hired-in leads
    (skills ≥ 210 at hire) are never read: the campus fixture has three on
    builds, none with `leadSinceDay`.
  - `GameState.ladderCrowdingFactor(producers:)`: with exactly one
    promoted lead on a build, the lead carries up to `span` (6) of the
    others at `penaltyFactor` (0.5) of Brooks's penalty and the rest pay
    full — `1 / (1 + 0.1 × (0.5 × min(6, n−1) + max(0, n−1−6)))`. With no
    promoted lead, or two or more (they cancel), it returns
    `crowdingFactor(producerCount:)` exactly. Read by the build tick, the
    patch tick, `dailyCodeOutput` and `BuildETA` — so the Now card's
    "Ships in about N days" moves the day a lead is named.
  - Idle lead: a promoted lead on a build (or patch) with fewer than
    `idleMinCrew` (3) others, or off a build, has `idleMoraleDelta` (−4)
    added to the morale **target** — a drift, never a jump.
  - The promotion-demand gate: once any lead has been promoted,
    `promotionDemand` is eligible only for a senior on a build of four or
    more people with no promoted lead. Before the first promotion the
    staff-event roll is untouched (see *Deviations*).
- **Options instead of pay** (`Ladder.swift`, `EmployeeSystem.grantEquity`):
  - `GameAction.grantEquity(employeeID:percent:)`, percent 1 or 2. Pay cut
    `payCut` (20% / 35%) of fair pay off the weekly salary, loyalty +25,
    bond +10, `equityRemaining −= percent`, an `EquityGrant` with the new
    reason `.options`, the stagnation clock reset. Event
    `.ladderEquityGranted`.
  - Refusals (`ladderGrantBlocker`, printed on the sheet): not on payroll,
    a co-founder, already a holder, game over, the pool (`poolMax` 10
    points outstanding), not enough equity left.
  - Vesting: nothing before `cliffDays` (364), straight-line to the full
    grant at `vestDays` (1456).
  - Fairness: the morale pass reads `fairnessSalary` — the salary before
    the cut until a raise overtakes it (`adjustSalary` clears
    `salaryBeforeGrant` once pay is back to it). Exactly `weeklySalary`
    for anyone never granted.
  - Leaving: `NetworkingSystem.departed` (every exit: quit, fire, poach,
    walk-out, sabbatical poach) calls `ladderSettleOptions`: unvested
    points go back to `equityRemaining`, the vested ones stay on the cap
    table under the alumnus's name — a poached holder takes them with them.
    Event `.ladderOptionsSettled`.
  - Poach: one marked line in `RivalSystem.poachTarget` adds
    `ladderPoachScoreDelta` — the cut does not read as underpayment, and
    each unvested point is −`poachUnvestedWeight` (20) on the score. 0 for
    anyone never granted.
- **Balance**: one defaulted property `ladder` under the K3 marker, struct
  in `Balance/BalanceConfig+Ladder.swift`, key appended at the end of
  `Balance.json`:
  `ladder { leads { penaltyFactor 0.5, span 6, idleMinCrew 3, idleMoraleDelta −4 }, grants { payCut [0.2, 0.35], loyalty 25, bond 10, cliffDays 364, vestDays 1456, poolMax 10, poachUnvestedWeight 20 } }`.

### App

- **Every build card and the Now card print the crew**:
  `LadderCrewLine` — *7 people · ×0.62 each · lead: none*, or
  *7 people · ×0.77 each · lead: Zanele (×0.62 without)*, or, in the
  warning tint, *two leads: they cancel out*. On Products' in-development
  card (a marked pair in K2's `ProductsListView`) and under the Now card's
  ETA sentence.
- **The promote button says what it does**: under *Promote to Lead
  (+15% salary)*, *Round 50: ×0.62 → ×0.77 each*, or *already has a lead:
  two cancel out*, or *nobody to lead off a build: morale target −4*. A
  lead's status line: *Runs Round 50 since …: ×0.77 each, not ×0.62*, the
  idle warning, or *Hired in at lead: the title, not the room.*
- **Options on the manage sheet** (`LadderOptionsSection`): two buttons,
  *Grant 1% · pay −$440/wk* with *1% of $2.1M = $21,000 over four years*
  under it, and *· Still yours closes* in the warning tint while the
  founder owns all of it; a confirmation with the same words. A holder
  sees what they hold, what vested, the cliff or full-vest date, what they
  gave up a week, and what comes back if they leave today.
- **The cap table's Team row** (`LadderTeamCapCard`, a marked pair in
  `InvestorsView`): every options grant, on payroll (vested so far, or the
  cliff date) or left (kept what vested), the team's total at today's
  valuation and the pool.
- Morale causes and the salary section read the pre-cut pay for a holder;
  two new causes (*A lead with nobody much to lead*, *Holds 1% of the
  company*).
- `EventCopy` lines for both new events (`LadderEventCopy`).

## Debug flags

- `-autoLadder`: once, on the loaded company: one senior (or the best
  mid, twice) promoted to lead on every build, and options for the two
  most skilled people who are not leads (2% and 1%).
- `-autoRoute k3-lead | k3-options | k3-holder`: the Team tab opens the
  manage sheet on a senior on a build, the best-paid non-holder, or the
  first holder.
