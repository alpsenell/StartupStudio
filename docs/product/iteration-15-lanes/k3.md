# K3 — the ladder

*Lane K3 of iteration 15, branch `k3-ladder` from `scaffold-15`, simulator
`ws-l3`. Specs: `iteration-15-pm/company.md` §3 (C3 Leads run the room)
and §4 (C4 Options instead of pay).*

## What was built

### Engine

- **`Employee`** gains `leadSinceDay: Int?`, `grantedEquity: Double`,
  `grantDay: Int?`, `salaryBeforeGrant: Int?` — decoded if present, each
  encoded only when set, so a roster nobody promoted or granted writes the
  bytes it wrote before.
- **Leads run the room** (`Ladder.swift`, `EmployeeSystem`):
  - `promote` to lead writes `leadSinceDay`; `demote` clears it. Only a
    *promoted* lead counts (`Employee.isPromotedLead`). Hired-in leads
    (skills ≥ 210 at hire) are never read: the campus fixture has three on
    builds, none with `leadSinceDay`.
  - `GameState.ladderCrowdingFactor(producers:)`: with exactly one
    promoted lead on a build, the lead carries up to `span` (6) of the
    others at `penaltyFactor` (0.5) of Brooks's penalty and the rest pay
    full — `1 / (1 + 0.1 × (0.5 × min(6, n−1) + max(0, n−1−6)))`. With no
    promoted lead, or two or more (they cancel), it returns
    `crowdingFactor(producerCount:)` exactly. Read by the build tick, the
    patch tick, `dailyCodeOutput` and `BuildETA` — so the Now card's
    "Ships in about N days" moves the day a lead is named.
  - Idle lead: a promoted lead on a build (or patch) with fewer than
    `idleMinCrew` (3) others, or off a build, has `idleMoraleDelta` (−4)
    added to the morale **target** — a drift, never a jump.
  - The promotion-demand gate: once any lead has been promoted,
    `promotionDemand` is eligible only for a senior on a build of four or
    more people with no promoted lead. Before the first promotion the
    staff-event roll is untouched (see *Deviations*).
- **Options instead of pay** (`Ladder.swift`, `EmployeeSystem.grantEquity`):
  - `GameAction.grantEquity(employeeID:percent:)`, percent 1 or 2. Pay cut
    `payCut` (20% / 35%) of fair pay off the weekly salary, loyalty +25,
    bond +10, `equityRemaining −= percent`, an `EquityGrant` with the new
    reason `.options`, the stagnation clock reset. Event
    `.ladderEquityGranted`.
  - Refusals (`ladderGrantBlocker`, printed on the sheet): not on payroll,
    a co-founder, already a holder, game over, the pool (`poolMax` 10
    points outstanding), not enough equity left.
  - Vesting: nothing before `cliffDays` (364), straight-line to the full
    grant at `vestDays` (1456).
  - Fairness: the morale pass reads `fairnessSalary` — the salary before
    the cut until a raise overtakes it (`adjustSalary` clears
    `salaryBeforeGrant` once pay is back to it). Exactly `weeklySalary`
    for anyone never granted.
  - Leaving: `NetworkingSystem.departed` (every exit: quit, fire, poach,
    walk-out, sabbatical poach) calls `ladderSettleOptions`: unvested
    points go back to `equityRemaining`, the vested ones stay on the cap
    table under the alumnus's name — a poached holder takes them with them.
    Event `.ladderOptionsSettled`.
  - Poach: one marked line in `RivalSystem.poachTarget` adds
    `ladderPoachScoreDelta` — the cut does not read as underpayment, and
    each unvested point is −`poachUnvestedWeight` (20) on the score. 0 for
    anyone never granted.
- **Balance**: one defaulted property `ladder` under the K3 marker, struct
  in `Balance/BalanceConfig+Ladder.swift`, key appended at the end of
  `Balance.json`:
  `ladder { leads { penaltyFactor 0.5, span 6, idleMinCrew 3, idleMoraleDelta −4 }, grants { payCut [0.2, 0.35], loyalty 25, bond 10, cliffDays 364, vestDays 1456, poolMax 10, poachUnvestedWeight 20 } }`.

## Measurements (the PM's "how it fails", first)

Both measured before any tuning, with a throwaway SwiftPM executable in the
scratchpad that links the engine and bots by path (not a test, not
committed).

**C3 — is one lead per build the new one answer?** The PM's check:
promote a senior on each of the campus fixture's five builds and compare
weekly points; over +25% → `penaltyFactor` 0.7.

| Fixture | Builds | Crew per build | Factor before → after | Points/day | Weekly | Simulated week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| campus d900, factor 0.5 | 5 (a senior on each, one rung) | 7,7,7,7,6 | ×0.62 → ×0.77 (×0.67 → ×0.80 on the six) | 92.2 → 113.9 | 645 → 798, **+23.6%** | 648 → 801, +23.6%; payroll $66,702 → $68,469 |
| studio d400, factor 0.5 | 3 (two mids promoted twice, one senior once) | 4,3,3 | ×0.77/0.83 → ×0.87/0.91 | 33.0 → 37.8 | 231 → 265, +14.8% | 231 → 265 |
| campus, factor 0.7 (reference) | 5 | | | | 645 → 732, +13.4% | |

+23.6% is under the +25% line, so **`penaltyFactor` stays 0.5**. The
price: +$1,767 a week of payroll on the campus (five 15% raises), a 12%
higher pay expectation on each for good, and on the studio every build
has fewer than three others per lead, so each of those leads would also
drift at morale target −4 — the idle rule is doing its job exactly where
the relief is smallest. The campus fixture's three hired-in leads on
builds carry no `leadSinceDay` and give no relief (confirmed in the run).

**C4 — does everybody get granted?** The PM's check: a CrunchHire-style
run that grants on every hire, `equityRemaining` at day 730; under 80 →
the pay cut is too generous. 24 seeds each, garage start, 730 days:

| Bot | Alive at d730 | `equityRemaining` median (min) | Options out median (max) | Kept by alumni (max) | Headcount median | Cash median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CrunchHire | 19/24 | 100 (100) | 0 (0) | 0 | 4 | $41,354 |
| + 1% on every hire | 16/24 | **98 (95)** | 2.3 (5.0) | 0.28 | 3 | $30,528 |
| + 2% on every hire | 17/24 | **94 (90)** | 6.0 (10.0) | 0.70 | 4 | $32,990 |

Nowhere near 80: the pool cap holds the floor at 90, and churn returns the
unvested points (a bot that grants every hire keeps a median 2–6 points
out, not 10). Not generous either: the granting bots did not survive
better (16–17 of 24 against 19). Not diagnosed per seed; the likely
reasons are that the bot's own raise rule ("anyone under morale 45 goes to
1.3× fair") pays the cut straight back, and that CrunchHire's failure rate
is known to be noisy between seeds (`PacingBots.swift`'s own note). The
pay cut stays at 20% / 35%.

## Deviations from the spec

- **The promotion-demand gate is engaged only after the first promotion to
  lead.** An unconditional gate narrows `promotionDemand`'s eligibility on
  every bot run and so moves the staff-event roll — a default-path change
  rule 20 forbids. Until the founder has promoted a lead, the ask arrives
  as it always did; from then on only a senior on a build of four or more
  with no promoted lead asks.
- **Idle lead includes "off a build".** The spec names "a build with fewer
  than three others"; a promoted lead on research, a contract, support or
  idle also reads as idle (they have nothing to lead either). A patch
  cycle counts as a build.
- **The pool counts what is out, not what was ever granted.** Unvested
  points that come back on a departure free the pool again. "Per run"
  read as the cap on outstanding options.
- **One grant per person, and not to a co-founder** (who already owns a
  slice from the founding). No top-ups.
- **The clique thread for two colliding leads** (PM: "optional") was not
  wired: `OfficeSecretsSystem` is not K3's file, and the collision is
  already priced (no relief). Follow-up.
- The PM's example "1% of $2.1M = $21,000 · pay −$440/wk" reads on the
  campus as *1% of $2,151,053 = $21,511 · pay −$431/wk* (fair pay × 0.2).
