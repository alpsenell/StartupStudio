## What was built

### Engine (`TycoonEngine`)

- **`FounderMoney.swift`** (new): `FounderMoneyState` on
  `EconomyState.founderMoney`. It holds `directorLoan`, `lastDividendDay`,
  `lastDividendTake`, `rescueRespondByDay`, `rescueSelling` and
  `rescueTakenDay`. Every field decodes if present, and each is encoded
  only when set; `.empty` is never encoded, so a run that touches none of
  it writes the bytes it always wrote. Also in the file:
  `FounderMoneyRescueAnswer` (`take`, `moveDown`, `sell`),
  `FounderMoneyDividendLine`, `FounderMoneySystem`, the `GameState`
  queries and blockers the screens read, and the debug seeds.
- **Actions**, sent only by the app: `lendToCompany(amount:)`,
  `repayDirectorLoan(amount:)`, `declareDividend(amount:)`,
  `answerRescue(_:)`. Plus `founderMoneyDebugSeed(kind:)`, which the
  reducer applies in DEBUG builds only.
- **Events**: `founderMoneyLoanMade`, `founderMoneyLoanRepaid(fromRound:)`,
  `founderMoneyDividend(amount:take:boardPressure:)`,
  `founderMoneyRescueAnswered`. All grade `.info` (the player's own taps),
  so the severity switch is untouched.
- **The director's loan.** Wallet → company cash, ledger line "Director's
  loan" (`.other`). It is repaid on demand, up to what is owed and what
  cash covers. `InvestorSystem.acceptOffer` repays it out of the cheque the
  moment the money posts (ledger "Director's loan repaid from the X round",
  wallet += loan). Lost if the company goes under. Refused at stake ≥ 2:
  `StakeLadder.refuses` treats it as `.takeLoan`, per F1. It is carried as
  a liability, one marked line in `companyValuation`
  (`value −= directorLoan`), and as the founder's asset in
  `founderNetWorth`, so lending moves net worth by exactly zero.
- **The dividend.** The company pays `amount` (ledger "Dividend", an
  expense). The wallet receives `amount × equityRemaining / 100`, and the
  rest leaves for the cap table: rounds, equity grants, the ex, and the
  remainder named as the co-founder (or "Other holders"). It is refused,
  with the reason on the button, on an earn-out, with a case open against
  the founder, in the red, with a bank loan outstanding, inside 91 days of
  the last one, or below 8 weeks of runway. A seated board adds +10
  pressure unless the last quarter was profitable
  (`profitableQuarters > 0`). That pressure is clamped one below the
  ousting line, because boards vote at reviews. For 13 weeks,
  `founderPayExcess` adds `take / 13` a week to the founder's pay, which
  the team's morale and the board's pay-pressure line both read.
- **The rescue as a choice** (armed runs only, `LifeSystem.checkEviction`):
  - The warning opens a queue entry (`QueueKind.rescue`, critical, due at
    the end of the grace period).
  - At the deadline an unanswered question takes its default: move one
    home down, mood −12.5 as before. While the founder is still under the
    line with no rescue running, the landlord asks again for the next
    period.
  - **Take**: the same salary formula as before, lifted unchanged into
    `LifeSystem.evictionRescueSalary`. It sets `rescueSalary` and
    `rescueTakenDay`. The exemption in `founderPayExcess` now covers only
    the rescue the company imposed, so a chosen one reads as pay.
    `FounderStanding.name` gets +4 for 364 days.
  - **Move down**: today.
  - **Sell something**: the entry becomes a room on the rail (routed to the
    assets room). It counts only if the wallet is back above the line by
    the deadline, because the back-in-the-black branch withdraws the
    question with the warning.
  - Old saves already on a rescue salary keep it, exempt, until square
    (`rescueTakenDay` is nil).
- **Balance**: one block, `founderMoney`, under the K1 marker.
  `FounderMoneyBalance` lives in `Balance/BalanceConfig+FounderMoney.swift`,
  with its `KeyedDecodingContainer` overload, and the keys are appended at
  the end of `Balance.json`: `dividendRunwayWeeks 8, dividendIntervalDays
  91, dividendBoardPressure 10, dividendPayWeeks 13, rescueStandingName 4,
  rescueStandingDays 364`. The Swift default carries the shipped numbers;
  every one is read behind an app-sent action or `doors.armed`.

### App

- **`Components/FounderMoneyCard.swift`** (new): the money sheet's card
  *Your money in the company*, placed after the App Store card, with a
  pixel kicker for each section.
  - *DIRECTORS LOAN*: what the company owes you, amount chips, and
    **Lend $X** with "Wallet → · runway →" under it. **Take back $X** has
    cash and runway under it. With a term sheet on the desk: "X's cheque
    repays it first: the company would see $Y".
  - *DIVIDEND*: a quarter, a half or all of what the floor allows, and the
    split line by line (*You · 20% $8,006 · Corvus Ventures · 22% …*).
    Every price is printed before the tap: the board's +10 or its pass; the
    streak ("this quarter must still grow past it, or the N-quarter
    profitable streak ends"); the team's reading, in dollars a week and
    morale points; and the next legal day. The button reads
    **Pay $X · you take $Y**, and a refused button carries the reason.
- **The landlord's question**:
  `DecisionPrompt.founderMoneyRescuePrompt`, kicker *LANDLORD*. It has
  three options with their consequences, and each one that cannot be
  taken shows why (the company cannot carry it; nowhere cheaper; nothing to
  sell). Seated in `DecisionSheet`'s queue switch and in
  `NoticeRail.queueRoute` (`.rescue → .assets`), one marked pair each.
- **Life → Personal money**: `FounderMoneyLifeNote` shows what the company
  owes you, the dividend still read as pay, and the landlord's deadline.
  One line each, with the existing *See all money* link below.
- **`EventCopy`**: four journal lines in the K1 region.
- **Debug**: `-autoFounderMoney loan|dividend|paid|rescue`, started from a
  marked `.task` beside J6's in `AppRootView`. Pair it with
  `-autoRoute shop` for the money sheet, or with `-autoTab life`.

## The dividend: one dividend for F1 and C2 (numbers chosen)

| | F1 | C2 | Built |
|---|---|---|---|
| founder's share | `amount × equityRemaining/100` | same, rest to the cap table printed line by line | both |
| interval | none in the last 13 weeks | once per 91 days | **91 days** |
| runway floor | 8 weeks of burn | 8 weeks (`dividendRunwayWeeks`) | **8 weeks** (burn = `queueWeeklyBurn`, the HUD's number) |
| refusals | open case | debt, earn-out, case | **case, earn-out, cash < 0, bank loan > 0** |
| board | via the pay line | +10 unless last quarter profitable | **both**: +10 now, and the pay line at every review for 13 weeks |
| streak | — | posted as an expense | **expense**: the review compares cash, so the quarter must grow past it |
| as founder pay | `amount / 13` for 13 weeks | — | **`take / 13`** for 13 weeks. Deviation: F1 wrote `amount`, but the team resents what landed in the founder's pocket, not what went to the funds. At 100% equity the two are the same. |
| cap | "half of last quarter's profit" as a probe | — | **no profit cap**. C2 explicitly allows a dividend out of a losing quarter at a board price, and the runway floor already brakes it (below). |

## The PM's "how it fails" checks

**F1: can a year-three founder pull more than about $100k?** The largest
legal dividend today, on the fixtures:

| fixture | cash | burn/wk | 8-week floor | largest dividend | founder takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| release-campus-day900 | $644,446 | $75,552 | $604,416 | $40,030 | **$8,006** (20%) |
| l3-family-day900 | $616,014 | $73,475 | $587,800 | $28,214 | $5,643 (20%) |
| release-studio-day400 | $90,875 | $20,286 | $162,288 | refused ("under 8 weeks of runway") | — |
| release-garage-day40 | $11,750 | $450 | $3,600 | $8,150 | $8,150 (100%) |

The campus founder can take about $8k a quarter, about $32k a year: well
under the PM's $100k, because the floor is sized on the campus burn. Net
worth: `companyValuation` counts `cash − loanBalance`, so the founder's
wallet gains exactly the slice the valuation loses. A dividend moves the
founder's net worth by rounding; the investors' share leaves the game.

**C2: does a max-dividend bootstrapper still reach *Still yours*?** Scratch
bot wrappers, 10 seeds × 1,092 days, rivals off. Each pays the largest legal
dividend whenever the blocker allows it, against the same bot never paying.
"Reach" means 8 profitable quarters in a row (`independentProfitableQuarters`).

| bot | never pays: reach / mean best streak | pays max every quarter: reach / streak / taken home / bankrupt |
|---|---|---|
| contract-grinder | **10/10** / 12.0 | **0/10** / 2.9 / $149,879 / 0 |
| saas-builder | 2/10 / 5.4 | 0/10 / 0.0 / $8,400 / **10** (vs 3) |
| balanced | 0/10 / 2.7 | 0/10 / 3.1 / $84,674 / **8** (vs 2) |

The dividend costs the independent ending, which is the trade C2 wanted, so
the floor stays at 8 weeks rather than C2's fallback of 13. The other finding: paying out to the floor every quarter
bankrupts the thin-margin bots. That is the "drain the company" failure
turning into a real risk, not a free pump. A garage founder can take
most of the seed money home on day one ($8,150 of $11,750, leaving
8 weeks of a $450 burn). It is legal and printed, and is the first thing I would watch (follow-ups).

**F9:** see the first measurement. The warning is reachable (53/100 bot
runs), so the gate is `doors.armed`, not a half-day exemption fix.

## Deviations

- The dividend reads `take / 13` as pay, not `amount / 13` (see the table).
- No profit cap on the dividend; the 8-week floor is the brake.
- "In debt" means cash < 0 or a bank loan outstanding. A director's loan
  outstanding does not block a dividend: repaying it is the founder's other
  pipe, and blocking it would make the loan a lock.
- F9's "Sell something" is an engine answer (`.sell`) so the sheet stops
  asking. The question then waits on the rail as a room in the assets tab.
  The PM had it as a route only.
- F9's +4 went to the founder's **name** (`FounderStanding.name`, the
  candidates' asks). The board already reads the chosen rescue through the
  pay line, and adding it to `boardLine` too would have priced it twice.
- `FinancesView` was not touched: the loan and the dividend appear there as
  ledger lines on their own, and "one home per thing" put the verbs on the
  money sheet.
- The rescue is `.critical` on the queue, but no new event pauses the
  clock: the existing `evictionWarning` (`.notable`) is the stop, and the
  question waits on the rail with its deadline.

## Follow-ups

1. **Garage day-one dividend.** A 100% founder can take the seed money
   home on day one, down to 8 weeks of a tiny burn. It is legal and
   printed, but if playtests abuse it, a "not in the first quarter" gate or
   a dollar floor (e.g. 8 weeks or $20k, whichever is larger) is one key.
2. **Other callers of `companyValuation`** (buyout offers, the IPO gate,
   K4's distress price) now see the director's loan as a liability, which
   is correct and zero by default. K4 should know.
3. **`TabBadge.lifeQueueKinds`** (V-owned) does not list `.rescue`, so the
   Life tab's badge does not count the landlord. A one-line add for its
   owner.
4. **K7's line in `founderPayExcess`**: the function now builds
   `var pay = life.founderSalary` then `pay += …`, one line per thing.
   K7's partner salary is one more `pay +=` inside its own marked pair.
5. **F8 Own the home** was waiting on wallets reaching six figures. With a
   campus dividend at about $8k a quarter and the director's loan
   round-tripping, that still takes years. Revisit with real play data.
