## What I built

### The three weights (`Components/CardView.swift`)

- **`.primary`** is unchanged. It is the same header `HStack`, now named
  `CardHeader`, above the content, on `cardStyle()`. Every existing caller
  gets it by default.
- **`.row`** is `CardRowLabel` on the card surface:
  - an accent icon in a 22 pt column;
  - the title in rounded subheadline semibold, with an optional one-line
    caption subtitle;
  - one number (the content), in `Theme.Typography.number(.subheadline)`,
    secondary unless the caller tints it;
  - a chevron.

  It is at least 56 pt tall and combines into one accessibility element.
  It draws no surface of its own, so several rows can share one card with
  dividers between them (`CardRowLabel.dividerInset`). This is what HQ's
  Company card does.
- **`.quiet`** is `CardQuietLine`: a tertiary icon, the title and the
  content on one footnote line, secondary, at least 40 pt tall. It sits on
  a lighter, smaller surface (card background at 55%, radius 10) and is
  meant to stack tightly in a grouped list.
- Nothing on this branch calls `.row` or `.quiet` through `CardView` yet.
  V1 and V2 do, and their screenshots will be the first pictures of those
  two looks inside a `CardView`.

### The Now card (`HQ/NowCard.swift`, `Components/PhaseProgress.swift`)

- **One segmented phase bar** (`SegmentedPhaseBar`) replaces the three
  unlabelled bars. It is one 8 pt bar in three segments (design, code,
  polish), each as wide as its share of the type's points and filled by its
  own progress. VoiceOver reads "Design 40%, Code 64%, Polish 72%".
  `TriPhaseProgress`, with its numbers, stays on Products.
- **The sentence the bars never said** (`NowBuildETA.sentence`), read off
  `GameState.buildETA`:
  - "Ships in about 67 days";
  - "Ships tomorrow";
  - "Could ship now · done in about 18 days", when code is already past
    the ship gate;
  - "Ready to ship";
  - "Stalled: nobody is on the build", in the warning tint.
- **The type became an icon** before the build's name, so the sentence has
  the whole line (a two-line wrap on the studio cost 20 pt). The type's
  name stays in the accessibility label.
- **A goal button that would repeat the build row is not drawn.** "Ship it
  → Open the build" opens exactly what the row above opens. The action is
  still in the table and the route still lands; the card just doesn't draw
  the same tap twice.
- **`NowAction` is untouched.**
  `testTheNowCardLeadsWithTheFirstGoalAndItsAction` and
  `testEveryChapterOneGoalHasAnAction` pass unchanged.

### The office, scaled to its card (`HQ/OfficeCard.swift`)

- **`OfficeSceneFit`**, a small `Layout`, sizes the pixel frame to
  *scene × scale + chrome*. The scale is the largest whole number at which
  the scene fits the width offered. The layout still reports the card's
  full width, so the card around it lays out as before.
- **The frame reaches 12 pt into the card's padding** (`bleed`, all of it
  but `xs`). On an iPhone 17 that is the difference between the studio at
  1× and at 2×.
- **Where no larger scale fits, the frame shrinks to the scene.** That is
  the campus at 1× and the audit's "or the card shrinks to the scene". The
  card keeps its full width; only the paper frame hugs.
- **The hit regions hold by construction.** PixelKit's own fit
  (`PixelSceneGeometry`, `.fitWidth`) now receives a view exactly
  `scene × scale` wide, lands on the same scale, and maps taps and
  VoiceOver regions through the same geometry as before. PixelKit is
  untouched, so its palette tests cannot move.

### The Company card (`HQ/HQScreen.swift`)

- **Below the office there is one grouped card.** A "Company" header sits
  above three `.row` lines on one surface:
  - *Burn and runway*: "Burning $20,286/wk", with the runway as the number
    in the burn card's tint rules;
  - *Chapter 4 of 5*: "Scale · Funded", with the number 3/6 goals done;
  - *Journal*: the newest line the journal leads with, and its week as the
    number.
- **Each row pushes the card it used to be,** on its own page:
  `BurnRateCard` (with See all money and the technical-debt line),
  `GoalsCard` (the goals, perks, teaser and Timeline) and `JournalCard`
  (See all and Front page). Nothing is gone; each is one tap in.
- **Settings follows the Company card,** as before.
- **The Chapter row hides before the first tick,** exactly when the chapter
  card drew nothing.
- **`consumeRoute` is untouched.** The newspaper and timeline routes still
  land, because the story destinations are registered on the same stack.

### The journal's week row (`HQ/JournalCard.swift`)

- **A closed week's heading can be a button:** "WEEK 57 · Report ›". A
  journal week N covers days 7N−7 to 7N−1. That is the report the shell
  offers on day 7N, so the week is closed once `day / 7 ≥ N`. The week in
  progress keeps its plain heading.
- **It is a hook, not wired.** I found no route that reopens a closed
  report. The only presenter, `GameShell.openWeeklyReport`, has three side
  effects:
  - it builds a new report from *today's* state;
  - it resets the morale and meters baselines the next report's deltas are
    measured against;
  - it counts as a manual open, which switches off the auto-open.

  Calling it for a past week would show the wrong week and skew next
  week's report. So `reopenReport` is `nil` behind the marked line
  `nil // V3: call V2's reopen here`. While it is `nil` every heading
  draws as it always has. **V2 replaces that one line** with its reopen
  (a closure taking the week), and the rows light up.
- `WeekGroup` gained a defaulted `onOpenReport`. `JournalScreen`'s use is
  unchanged.

## Deviations

1. **The Company rows push, rather than unfold in place.** A row with a
   chevron is a push, which is the grammar the `.row` weight states, and
   the three cards read better on their own page than nested in a card.
   Nothing folds, so rule 18 needs no `GameSettings` key.
2. **The goal button is hidden when it duplicates the build row** (see
   above). The route and the table are unchanged.
3. **The campus stays at 1×.** A 2× campus is 520 pt wide. Here the audit's
   "or the card shrinks to the scene" means the pixel frame, not the whole
   card, so HQ's column of cards stays one width.
4. **The journal hook is unwired**, for the reasons above.

   has `.fixed` for that), not a non-integer scale.

   `DebugLaunch` if other lanes want the same measuring on their tabs.
