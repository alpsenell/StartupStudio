# P3 — the shop's surfaces and copy

*Iteration 13. Branch `p3-surfaces`, cut from `scaffold-13`. Builds §5
(except Settings and the front-door slot, which are P2's) and §6 of
`iteration-13-iap.md`.*

The shop appears on four screens the player already reads, and on no
others. The money sheet gets a fourth card with two cash packs. The
bankruptcy post-mortem gets one button. The hire sheet gets one last
card. The furnish sheet gets one section. Every one of them shows the
price next to the thing it buys, says the grant in dollars before the
tap, and asks the engine's `PurchaseRule` whether to offer it at all.
The paywall no longer claims that nothing else is for sale, and the
biography and the journal record what was bought.

## What I built

### The seam: `ShopSurfaceModel` (`App/Sources/Store/ShopSurfaceModel.swift`)

- `ShopSurfaceItem`: the five items this lane draws (month, quarter,
  receiver's call, veteran, loft pack), with their product ids, their
  `PurchaseKind` (`nil` for the pack) and their display names. This file
  holds the only product ids in the app on this branch.
- `ShopSurfaceModel`: a `@MainActor` protocol with four members.
  `price(productID) -> String?` returns the storefront's own
  `displayPrice`. `owned: Set<String>` is the owned product ids.
  `refusal: String?` is the store's own "no", e.g. purchases turned off
  on the device. `buy(_ item)` starts a purchase. It reaches views
  through `\.shopSurface`.
- **Nothing injected means no shop.** Each of the four surfaces draws
  nothing when the environment has no store. That covers every snapshot
  test, and the app on this branch until P2's client is adapted, so no
  existing screen changes by a pixel.
- `ShopSurfacePreview`: a fixed store with the spec's US prices, used by
  previews and the debug pass.
- `ShopSurfaceCopy`: the §2 and §5 copy in one place. It holds the
  unranking line, the receiver's-call body, and the refusal copy. The
  refusal copy turns `PurchaseRule.refusal` into the spec's words. A
  shared company gets the button word "Not in a shared company" and the
  ghost footnote. Stake 2 or higher gets "Not at this stake" and the
  bank footnote, for cash and the receiver's call only. Any other refusal
  prints the rule's own line. The rule decides whether a purchase is
  allowed; the copy only picks the words.
- `ShopLeaveBoardsDialog` / `ShopPurchaseFlow`: the one confirmation from
  §2. The first economy purchase in a ranked company asks **"Leave the
  boards?"**, with the message "‹company› stops posting to leaderboards
  from today. Achievements still count." and the buttons **"Buy and
  leave the boards"** / **"Cancel"**. After the purchase the company is
  unranked and `isRanked` is false, so the dialog can never appear
  twice. The loft pack never asks.
- `ShopPriceButton`: one price in the game's own `PixelButtonStyle`. It
  has a paper fill when disabled and shows "…" until the App Store
  answers. When the rule refuses, the refusal's short word replaces the
  price. VoiceOver reads the item's name, then what it grants, then the
  price: "A month of runway, +$78,400 into company cash, $1.99, from the
  App Store".

### The four surfaces

- **Money sheet: the App Store card** (`Components/ShopAppStoreCard.swift`,
  one line in `MoneySheet.swift` inside P3 markers, after *Bank*).
  - Two rows, *A month of runway* and *A quarter of runway*. Each shows
    `+$N into company cash`, read live from `PurchaseRule.cashAmount`,
    and the storefront price on the button.
  - The unranking line sits above the rows in every Standard company.
  - The refusal footnotes are gathered and said once.
  - At AX sizes each row stacks the name over the button
    (`ViewThatFits`).
  - Nothing animates, counts or badges.
- **The receiver's call** (`Screens/Endings/ShopReceiverCall.swift`,
  one line in `FounderBiographyView` under the post-mortem card).
  - A pixel-paper panel with the §5 copy.
  - Two figures before the tap: *Overdraft written off: $N* and
    *A month of cash: +$N*.
  - The button reads **"Take the receiver's call · $2.99"**.
  - It appears only when `info.kind == .bankruptcy` and
    `PurchaseRule.refusal(.secondChance)` is nil. That hides it when the
    call was already taken, in a scenario, in a shared company or at a
    stake.
- **The veteran** (`Screens/Team/ShopVeteranCard.swift`, one line in
  `HiringSheet` after the pool).
  - Under an "A VETERAN" heading: a normal candidate card with portrait,
    name, role badge, salary, the line in their own voice, skill bars
    against the roster's best, and **both** traits revealed.
  - Where *Interview* sits on a pool card, there is **"Bring them in ·
    $2.99"**.
  - The line under it is, in order of precedence: the refusal footnote;
    "Your office is full…" at the headcount cap; the unranking line in a
    ranked company; or "They join the pool above, both traits known.
    Hiring them is still up to you."
  - Hidden with no veteran on offer, or once the veteran is in the pool
    or on the payroll.
- **The loft pack** (`Screens/Life/Decor/ShopLoftPackSection.swift`, one
  line in `FurnishSheet` after *Your things*).
  - While the pack is not owned: the header **"THE LOFT PACK · $1.99"**
    (the only price on the section), one line on what it is, the six
    items greyed (sprite desaturated at 45%, name, where it goes), and
    **"Buy the pack"**.
  - Once owned: the six go on the shelf like everything else, captioned
    **"Bought from the App Store · hangs on the wall"**, and the section
    is gone.
  - `ShopLoftPack.shelf(_:owned:)` adds the owned pack to the shelf and
    de-duplicates. That keeps it correct on this branch now, and still
    correct after P2's `available(owned:)` includes the pack.

## Identity at the default

- **No engine bytes change beyond the six `DecorItem`s.** They are
  `let` catalog data, never encoded, and never placed by anything but a
  player holding the pack. No new state, no new draws, no new actions.
  Suites: engine 943, content 52, save 34, PixelKit 336, all at their
  baselines.
- **No surface draws without a store.** None of the snapshot suites
  injects `\.shopSurface`, and the debug preview is off unless one of my

  `biographyContent`, `PaywallContent` and the rest render as before,
  apart from the paywall's rewritten sentence.
- The snapshot suites write PNGs; they do not compare against pinned
  images. **No test was added, edited or re-pinned.** The pinned numbers
  that could move (the strings audit's 49 bare `PixelText` literals and
  its 300-key floor) did not: this lane writes no `PixelText` literal.

## How it fails — the cheap check (§8, "the shop nags anyway")

`grep -rnE "startupstudio\.(secondchance|slot4|decor\.loft|cash\.month|cash\.quarter|veteran)" App/Sources App/StoreKit`
finds the five ids in `Store/ShopSurfaceModel.swift` and nowhere else. No
view, no rail, no tip and no toast names a product. Against the §5
anti-nag rules:

1. **Rail:** nothing added to `NoticeRail`.
2. **Pause:** `purchaseApplied` stays `.info` (P1's grade). The feed
   line is plain.
3. **Coach tip:** no `TipStrip` entry.
4. **Self-presenting:** nothing. The only shop sheets that open on their
   own are DEBUG-only, behind `-autoRoute`.
5. **Weekly report, tour, desk, toast, push:** untouched.
6. **Badges and marks:** no badge, dot, count, countdown, "limited",
   "offer" or "best value". The veteran card does not say how long the
   offer lasts, so that no countdown creeps in by the back door.
7. **One price per item,** next to it, with the grant in dollars first.

## Deviations and decisions

1. **The surfaces are absent without a store.** This keeps the snapshot
   suites and the P1-before-P2 app honest. After the merge the root
   injects P2's client and every surface draws. Until then only the
   debug preview does.
2. **"Leave the boards?" is on my side.** The spec puts the dialog "before
   StoreKit's sheet" without naming a lane. It lives in the surfaces
   (`ShopPurchaseFlow`), so P2's `buy` should *not* ask again (follow-up 2).
3. **The veteran is disabled at the headcount cap,** with "Your office is
   full. Make room before you pay for anyone." The spec does not say.
   Taking money for a hire the office cannot seat reads as a trick.
4. **The money sheet's card sits after *Bank*, before *You*,** as the
   spec says: the two kinds of outside money side by side.
5. **The card and post-mortem chrome follow their host screens.** The
   money sheet's `CardView` keeps its SF glyph ("bag") like its three
   siblings. The receiver's call is a `PixelPanel`. Every buy button is
   `PixelButtonStyle`.
6. **`DecorSource.purchased` is declared here** (P3 pair in
   `HomeDecor.swift`) because the six items need it to compile. I told
   P2 the exact lines; if P2 declares the same case, the merge keeps one.
7. **HomeCard P3 pair.** `-autoRoute furnish` is read as a literal string
   in `HomeCard`, so `loftpack` needed one line there. That file has no
   owner this round; the change is DEBUG-only.

## Follow-ups

1. **P2 / the merge: inject the store.** One line at the root, next to
   `.environment(\.gameSession, session)`:
   `.environment(\.shopSurface, <P2's client adapted to ShopSurfaceModel>)`.
   The adapter maps `price(productID)` to `Product.displayPrice`, `owned`
   to the current entitlements, `refusal` to "purchases are off"-style
   store state, and `buy(item)` to the session's purchase.
2. **P2: do not ask "Leave the boards?" a second time.** The surface
   already asked before calling `buy`.
3. **P2: `DecorPresentation.available(owned:)`.** Once it includes the
   pack, `FurnishSheet.shelf` can pass `owned` to it and drop
   `ShopLoftPack.shelf`. It is harmless to leave, because the list is
   de-duplicated.
4. **P1: the stake copy.** `ShopSurfaceCopy.refusal` shows "Not at this
   stake" only for cash and the receiver's call, as §2 says. If P1's rule
   refuses the veteran at a stake, the rule's own line prints instead.
5. **The review screenshots** (§4.5 step 3) can be taken with the debug
   flags below once P1 and P2 are in. `-autoRoute receiver` can move to
   P2's `release-bankruptcy` fixture then.
6. **VoiceOver in Accessibility Inspector.** The labels are written and
   described above. The interactive Inspector pass could not be driven
   from this headless lane, so it is one manual look per surface before
   TestFlight.
7. **Not this lane's, seen on the way:** at AX3 the money sheet's
   *Company* card breaks a figure across two lines ("$20,2 / 86/wk",
   "$15,7 / 85"). `MoneySheetContent.row` is a plain `HStack`; the
   biography's `row` already uses the `ViewThatFits` pattern that fixes
   it. It predates iteration 13 and is outside the P3 region, so I left
   it alone.
8. **Dark mode, the greyed pack swatches** read dim (45% opacity on a
   dark ground). They are legible, but if the owner wants them brighter
   in dark, the opacity is a single number in `ShopLoftPackSection`.
