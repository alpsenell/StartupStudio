## What I built

### C4 — the decision sheet always shows its question

- **The question is pinned** (`DecisionSheet.swift`): the portrait, the
  kicker, the title and the body now sit above the scroll view, and the
  answers stay pinned below it. Only the stats scroll. The title never
  truncates (`fixedSize`). The body keeps its first two lines at the
  medium detent, and a tap on it takes the sheet to full height.
- **The portrait is 48 pt at the medium detent**, 64 pt at full height.
- **`DecisionPrompt.opensLarge`**: a prompt with three or more options,
  or a body over 120 characters, opens at `.large`. The sheet binds its
  detent (`presentationDetents(_:selection:)`), so it can still be
  dragged down. If a three-answer sheet is dragged to medium, the
  answers scroll under the pinned question instead of pushing it off the
  top.
- **At accessibility sizes nothing changes**: the sheet opens full and
  the question and answers scroll as one column, as before
  (`testTheTitleSurvivesAccessibilitySizes`).
- **Player words** replace engine words. `PriceWarPrompt`:
  - "the −10% lifts" became "you win back the 10% of share";
  - "Marrow −1.5 strength a week" became "Marrow gets weaker every week
    (−1.5)";
  - "grudge 0 → 25" became "bad blood 0 → 25 of 100".

  The category challenge's "they lose N strength" became "they come out
  N weaker". The numbers stay, and the after-state money line
  (`afterState`) is untouched.
- "Let me think", the modal category challenge and J6's queue logic are
  unchanged. `-autoAnswer` reads `DecisionPrompt`, not the view.
- Debug: `-autoSheetMedium` opens any sheet at the medium detent, so the
  half-height state can be photographed.

### C3 — the rail is one line, and a tip is said once

- **One line** (`NoticeRail.swift`). Every notice's text is
  `lineLimit(1)`, and a tap on the line opens its full text in place
  (`oneLine(_:noticeID:)`). A change of notice closes it again. The row
  keeps 4 pt of padding around its 34 pt controls, and the tip's ✕ is
  34 pt tall.
- **A tip shows only under the tab it concerns**: the tab its button
  goes to (`Route.tab`), or HQ when it has no button. Each tab root now
  sets `\.railTab` in `AppRootView`. Outside the tab bar (previews,
  snapshots) `railTab` is `nil` and the rail behaves exactly as before.
- **A tip is said at most once a session.** `RailTipLedger` marks a tip
  *seen* after three seconds on screen and *said* once it leaves (another
  tab, another notice). A said tip stays off the rail until the next
  launch. Dismissing with ✕ still hides a tip for good, per install.
- **The office's first-time line is an HQ rail tip now**
  (`CoachTip.officeTap`). It keeps the same id, `tip.office_tap`, so an
  install that dismissed the old strip never sees it again, and the
  first tap that lands on the scene still dismisses it. `OfficeCard`
  draws the old strip only for its preview.
- **The rail's ▶ is gone while the speed control carries the dot.** It
  checks the same three conditions `TopHUD.needsAttention` uses. In the
  live game a pause row always has the dot, so the HUD's play button is
  the one control for time.
- **Kept as they were:** J6's three seams (`laneNotices`, `fallbackTip`,
  `laneRoute`), J1's hooks inside them, the queue rows and their
  deadlines, J5's countdown (it arrives through `laneNotices`), and the
  tour at priority 1. Coach tips still stay silent while the tour runs.
  `Iteration7ScaffoldAppTests`' priority order is unchanged.

### C7 — Business: one row of pills, and a desk that leads

- **The desk leads** (`BusinessScreen`). Under it comes one horizontally
  scrolling pill row (every label whole, bleeding to the screen's edges),
  then the section. `SegmentPillBar` is always one row now: the
  three-column grid path is gone and the init is unchanged.
- **Investors is hidden, not disabled**, until there is a term sheet, a
  board seat, or a round already raised (deviation 2). A section that is
  selected always shows, so a deep link to it still lands.
- **Pill badges use C6's rule** (desk rows due inside a week), so a

- **Market's Report/Map picker is a toggle on the section header**, so
  Business has one segmented control. J3's `onAppear`

## Deviations

1. **Chrome is 365, not the audit's "about 330".** The rail row is now
   one 34 pt control plus 4 pt above and below. Getting to 330 means
   shrinking the ✕ and route targets below 34 pt, which I did not do.
   Tabs with nothing to say have no rail at all (about 270).
2. **Investors also shows once a round has been raised**, not only for
   a term sheet or a board seat. A seatless round still has a cap table
   worth seeing, and hiding it would take away a screen the player has
   used. A selected section always shows, which keeps every route.
3. **A tip under a tab is its button's tab, or HQ.** I read "(or HQ)" as
   the fallback for tips with no button, not as "HQ as well": showing it
   on HQ too would say it twice, which is what C3 removes.

   needs the desk rows to carry their real deadlines (the price war's
   answer window, the interview's launch day). That belongs in
   `Desk.items`, which is C5's source.

   story beat and a distress bid instead, and seeding a poach would need
   an engine debug action (no engine bytes this lane). The price war is
   the three-answer case and is photographed at both detents.
3. **`OfficeTapHint` is now preview-only.** It and
   `testTheHintShowsTheFirstTime` could go when the owner lifts the test
   rule.
4. **`RailTipLedger` is per launch.** If a tip should be said once ever,
   it needs a `GameSettings` key.
5. **`make strings`.** New or changed keys:
   - the price-war and challenge details;
   - the office tip;
   - the Departments lines;
   - the desk's empty line;
   - "More ways to play", "Saves", the three group headings, the desk
     line;
   - the market toggle.
