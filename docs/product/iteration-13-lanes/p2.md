# P2 — StoreKit and the session

*Iteration 13. Branch `p2-store`, cut from `scaffold-13`. No merge, no push.*

The shop's plumbing, `iteration-13-iap.md` §4 end to end plus the
Settings and front-door pieces of §5. The app now knows six more
products. A bought consumable goes through the engine once and is
finished only when the grant is in the save. A company that cannot take
a grant parks it for the next one that can. The Apple ID can own a
fourth slot and the loft pack. Restore says what came back and that the
consumables don't.

On this branch the engine is still the scaffold's stub, which refuses
every purchase. So every consumable here parks, which is the correct
behaviour against that stub. The happy path was checked with a temporary
engine patch and a fake store, nothing of either committed (see the
check below).

## What I built

### StoreKit (`App/Sources/Store/`)

- **`ShopCatalog.swift`**: `ShopProduct`, the six ids from §4.4, with
  `isConsumable`, the `PurchaseKind` each consumable grants, and the §1
  display names. It also holds `ShopCatalog.isConsumable(_:)`, which
  `Entitlements` reads. `slotCount = 4` and `fourthSlotIndex = 3` live
  here.
- **`ShopClient.swift`**: the StoreKit 2 actor behind a `ShopSource`
  protocol.
  - A verified consumable is *held* inside the actor (`[UInt64:
    Transaction]`) and handed to the session as a plain
    `ShopTransaction` (id, product id, revoked). It is finished only
    when the session calls `finish(id)`; after a relaunch that lookup
    goes back through `Transaction.unfinished`.
  - `unfinished()` returns our consumables still unfinished, and
    finishes on the spot any owned thing or any id this build doesn't
    sell.
  - `ownedProductIDs()` reads `currentEntitlements` and skips revoked
    transactions.
  - `updates()` hands the session every verified transaction.
  - Swift 6: the actor never calls into the session. The session awaits
    it from the main actor.
- **`ShopState.swift`** (stored on the session as `shop`):
  - prices from `displayPrice`;
  - `owned`, with `isOwnedKnown` and a `UserDefaults` cache so the
    first frame doesn't flicker a lock;
  - `parked: [ShopTransaction]`, mirrored in `UserDefaults`;
  - `lastMessage`, `inFlight` and `pendingConfirmation`;
  - `lockedSlot`: the fourth row while it is locked.
- **`ShopPresentation.swift`**: the §5 anti-nag rules as pure functions.
  - `offers(product, on: ShopSurface)` is the table: each product on
    exactly one of the money sheet, post-mortem, hire sheet, furnish
    sheet or front door, and on none of the rail, pause, coach tip,
    weekly report, tour, morning desk, journal, toast, push, HUD,
    paywall or ending cover.
  - `autoPresents(on:)` is always `false`.
  - `isPlain(_:)` checks copy against the forbidden phrases.
  - `label(_:amount:price:)` builds "A fourth slot · $0.99".
  - The surfaces' §2 copy and "Leave the boards?" are P3's
    (`ShopSurfaceCopy`, `ShopPurchaseFlow.tap`). The first draft of this
    lane also carried them; they were removed when P3 landed its own, so
    the question is asked once, by the surface, never by the session.
- **`ShopViews.swift`**:
  - `ShopFrontDoor` and `ShopFrontDoorRows`, the locked fourth slot and
    the parked line.
  - `ShopRestoreRow`, and `ShopPurchasesRow` with `ShopPurchasesView`.
- **`ShopSurfaceStore.swift`**: the adapter from the session to P3's
  `ShopSurfaceModel`. It implements `price(_:)`, `owned`, `refusal` ("Purchases
  are turned off on this device." / "The App Store isn't answering…")
  and `buy(productID:)`. One instance lives for the app's life as
  `session.shopSurfaceStore`. It reads through `session.shop`, so the
  surfaces redraw when the store answers. The protocol and
  `ShopSurfaceItem` exist only on `p3-surfaces`, so the conformance can't
  compile here. The merge glue is below.
- **`Entitlements.swift`, the one risky edit**: the `updates()` listener
  still finishes the unlock, the slot, the pack and any unknown id. A
  consumable is no longer finished there; it is left for `ShopClient`
  and `applyGrant`.

### The session (`App/Sources/GameSession+Shop.swift`)

- **`installShop()`**, called once from the root view's `.task`:
  1. seeds `owned` and `parked` from the last launch;
  2. subscribes to `updates()` before asking anything;
  3. loads prices and ownership;
  4. rebuilds `parked` from `unfinished()` — the store's list is the
     truth, the mirror is only for display;
  5. runs every unfinished transaction through `applyGrant`;
  6. then listens for the app's life.
- **The purchase flow (§4.1)**:
  - `requestShopPurchase(p)` goes straight to the store. The surface has
    already asked "Leave the boards?" when it had to.
  - `buyFromShop(p)` pre-checks with `PurchaseRule.refusal`, so no money
    is taken for a grant the reducer would refuse, then buys.
    - Verified → `applyGrant`.
    - Pending → "Waiting for approval. It arrives by itself when it
      comes through."
    - Unverified → "…Nothing was granted…"
    - Cancelled → nothing.
    - A thrown error → "The App Store couldn't be reached…"
- **`applyGrant(tx)`**:
  - unknown id → finish;
  - owned thing → mark owned, finish, re-read entitlements;
  - revoked consumable → finish, no grant and no clawback (§6);
  - otherwise it tries the grant:
    - **Applied or already granted** → settle the receiver's call if
      that is what it was, remember the id, unpark, finish.
    - **Refused, or nothing is open** → the transaction stays unfinished
      and parks.
  - "Already granted" means one of two things: the engine's
    `purchases.contains(id)`, or a local list of ids this device
    applied. That list is a third layer on top of the spec's two: it
    stops a transaction applied before a kill from landing again in a
    *different* company, if another slot is open when it is redelivered.
  - A grant needs a real company: never the placeholder, never a locked
    fourth slot. Shared, ended and stake refusals are `PurchaseRule`'s.
- **Parked grants**:
  - `shopEngineDidChange()` is one marked line in `wireEngineHooks`, so
    it runs on every engine swap (open, continue, new, replay, a daily,
    a scenario). It retries the parked grants against the new company.
  - The front door shows "1 purchase waiting for a company". Settings
    lists them under *Waiting*.
- **The receiver's call (§3.4 step 2)**:
  - After a grant, `settleReceiversCall()` removes the `LegacyRun` for
    this seed, company and `purchases.secondChanceDay` with ending
    `.bankruptcy`, then saves the ledger.
  - `endingsReached` keeps `.bankruptcy`, so the trophy and the earned
    look stay; hall inductions stay too.
  - It is idempotent and also runs on "already granted", so a grant
    that was saved before a kill still gets its ledger fixed on
    redelivery.
- **Owned things (§4.2)**:
  - `applyOwned` and `refreshOwnedProducts` keep `shop.owned` current
    from `currentEntitlements`, `updates` and Restore.
  - Revocation drops the product from `owned`:
    - the fourth slot re-locks: no Continue, no open, not listed, its
      save kept;
    - the pack's items stop being offered, but placed ones stay, because
      the scene draws `life.decor`, not the offer list.
  - One choice of mine: a game already running *in* slot 4 when the
    refund lands is not thrown out. It locks the next time the door is
    up.

## One finding: `simctl launch` does not use the local StoreKit config

The scheme's `storeKitConfiguration` applies only when Xcode runs the
app (⌘R). An app launched with `xcrun simctl launch` talks to the real
store, where these ids don't exist yet. Prices come back empty, so the
locked fourth row reads "The App Store isn't answering" and is disabled
(first screenshot below). That is the intended fallback for an
unreachable store. It also means `-autoShop <id>` reaches the local
config only when the app is launched from Xcode. Unchanged from
iteration 7: the paywall's price behaves the same way.
