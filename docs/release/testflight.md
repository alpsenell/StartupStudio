# TestFlight and the first submission

*The checklist for getting Startup Studio 1.0.0 to a build that installs
on somebody else's phone, and then to review. Everything in the repo is
done and tested; everything below marked **ASC** happens in App Store
Connect or the Developer portal and only the account holder can do it.*

Bundle ID `com.alpsenel.startupstudio` · Team `6S7Q8F967Q` ·
`MARKETING_VERSION` 1.0.0 · `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` from
`git rev-list --count HEAD`, written into `App/Config/Version.xcconfig`
by `make gen`.

---

## 1. The App ID's capabilities — **ASC / Developer portal**

The app's entitlements are generated by xcodegen from `project.yml` into
`App/StartupStudio.entitlements`. They will not sign until the same three
capabilities are ticked on the App ID:

| Capability | Why | Entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| **iCloud (Key-Value storage)** | R2 syncs the three save slots through `NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore`. KVS needs **no** CloudKit container — tick iCloud, enable Key-value storage, leave containers empty. | `com.apple.developer.ubiquity-kvstore-identifier` = `$(TeamIdentifierPrefix)$(CFBundleIdentifier)` |
| **Game Center** | R3's achievements and leaderboards. | `com.apple.developer.game-center` |
| **In-App Purchase** | R6's one unlock. No entitlement file entry — it is a capability on the App ID only. | — |

After ticking them, regenerate the provisioning profile (automatic signing
does this on the next archive) and confirm the archive's entitlements in
Organizer → *Show in Finder* → *Info.plist* / `codesign -d --entitlements`.

## 2. The product — **ASC**

- One non-consumable: **`com.alpsenel.startupstudio.fullgame`**. The id is
  asserted by the app suite (`UnlockState.productID`), so it has to match
  exactly.
- Iteration 13 adds a small shop beside it — six products, listed with
  their types in `docs/product/iteration-13-iap.md` §4.4 — and none of it
  is needed to finish the game: bought cash, the receiver's call and the
  veteran make the company unranked (say so in each store description),
  the fourth slot and the loft pack touch no run, and every price the
  game shows is the storefront's own `displayPrice`.
- Reference name, display name, description, price tier, and the review
  screenshot — all required before the state can leave *Missing Metadata*.
- The product must reach **Ready to Submit** and be **attached to the
  version** before the build is submitted, or review sees a paywall with
  nothing behind it.
- `App/StoreKit/StartupStudio.storekit` is the **local** copy for
  simulator purchases. It is a *scheme* setting
  (`scheme.storeKitConfiguration` in `project.yml`), not a resource: it is
  not in the app bundle in any configuration. Verify before archiving:

  ```sh
  # nothing should come back
  find "$(xcodebuild -showBuildSettings … -configuration Release |
        awk '/BUILT_PRODUCTS_DIR/{print $3}')" -name '*.storekit'
  ```

  Nothing in the release scheme's *Run* action reaches an archive anyway —
  this is a belt-and-braces check, and the reason the file lives in
  `App/StoreKit/` rather than `App/Resources/`.

## 3. Game Center items — **ASC**

R3 defines **48 achievements** (one per goal, `GameCenterID.achievement(goalID:)`
→ `com.alpsenel.startupstudio.goal.<id>`) and **8 leaderboards**
(`GameCenterID.leaderboard(_:)` → `com.alpsenel.startupstudio.lb.<id>`).

- Create every one with the exact identifier the app posts to. A typo is
  silently ignored at runtime — GameKit reports success for an unknown id
  in sandbox and does nothing.
- Each achievement needs a title, pre-earned and earned descriptions,
  point value (the 48 must total ≤ 1,000) and a 512 × 512 image.
- Each leaderboard needs a name, score format, sort order and an image.
- **Attach all 56 to the version.** Game Center items are versioned like
  metadata; ones left unattached do not ship, and the first thing a player
  sees is an empty achievements list.

## 4. Export compliance

Answered in the binary, not in the form:
`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` (in `project.yml`'s
`info.properties`; asserted by `ReleasePlumbingTests`). The app uses no
encryption beyond what the OS does for HTTPS and the keychain, and there
is no HTTPS of its own — nothing in the app talks to a server we own.
With the key present, App Store Connect stops asking at every upload.

## 5. App Privacy — **ASC**

**Data Not Collected.** Answer *No* to "Do you or your third-party
partners collect data from this app?"

The reasoning, in full, is in [`app-privacy.md`](app-privacy.md); the
short version:

- No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ad network, no third-party
  dependency of any kind. The app makes no network request of its own.
- iCloud key-value storage holds the player's saves in *their* iCloud
  account. Apple's own guidance treats data the app stores in the user's
  iCloud, which the developer cannot read, as not collected.
- Game Center identifiers and scores are handled by Apple under the
  player's Game Center account.
- **Check the Game Center wording at submission.** That questionnaire has
  changed before, and if it grows an explicit "Game Center" item this
  answer is the thing that has to move. Do not copy this file's answer
  forward without reading the current form.

`App/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declares the same thing to the
toolchain: `NSPrivacyTracking = false`, no tracking domains, no collected
data types, one accessed-API reason —
`NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults` / `CA92.1` (settings for this
app only). Nothing reads file timestamps, disk space, active keyboards or
system boot time. Asserted by
`Iteration7ScaffoldAppTests.testPrivacyManifestShipsInTheBundle…` and
`ReleasePlumbingTests.testPrivacyManifestDeclaresNoTrackingDomains…`.

## 7. Sandbox testers — **ASC**

Two, because they test different things and one account cannot be in both
states cleanly:

1. **A purchase tester.** Sandbox Apple Account, signed in under
   *Settings → Developer → Sandbox Apple Account* on the device. Buy the
   unlock, then *Restore purchases* from Settings, then delete and
   reinstall the app and confirm the unlock comes back without a second
   charge.
2. **A Game Center tester.** A second sandbox account with Game Center
   enabled and **no** purchase, to check that the first launch
   authenticates, the achievements list is populated, and the daily posts
   a score.

Sandbox purchases do not appear in Organizer and do not cost money; a
sandbox account cannot be used on the App Store.

## 8. Review notes — **ASC**

Paste this into *App Review Information → Notes*:

> Startup Studio is a single-player, offline tycoon game. Chapter 1 is
> free and complete: you can found a company, hire, build and ship a
> product, and read the reviews. The one in-app purchase
> (`com.alpsenel.startupstudio.fullgame`, non-consumable) unlocks
> everything from chapter 2 onward; the paywall appears when chapter 2
> opens. Nothing is ever deleted or hidden by the lock — an unentitled
> player can open every screen and read every save; only the clock stops.
>
> Reaching chapter 2 by playing takes roughly 30 minutes at ×4 speed. If
> you would rather not, the attached build's title screen has a
> **Continue** card on a pre-made company well past that point — tap
> Continue and the paywall is one tap away on the HQ tab.
>
> The game has no accounts, no network calls of its own, no analytics and
> no ads. iCloud is used only to sync the player's own save slots through
> key-value storage; Game Center is used only for achievements and
> leaderboards.

If the reviewer will not have a sandbox purchase available, say so and
offer a promo code once the build is approved for TestFlight.

## 9. The last mile — before you submit

Run these in order. Anything that fails here fails in review.

- [ ] `make gen && make test` — the four package suites.
- [ ] `make apptest` — read the line: **`Executed N tests, with 0 failures`**.
- [ ] `make build` and `make build-ipad` — both destinations compile.
- [ ] `make screenshots` — 22 PNGs, and **look at them**.
- [ ] Archive in Xcode (*Any iOS Device*), then **Validate**. Expect
      zero warnings about a missing privacy manifest, a missing
      entitlement, or an unsupported device family. A missing-manifest
      warning means `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` fell out of the Copy Bundle
      Resources phase — check `project.yml`, not the plist.
- [ ] Confirm the archive's `CFBundleVersion` is higher than every build
      already uploaded. It comes from the commit count, so this is
      automatic as long as you archive after committing.
- [ ] **A release build on a real device**, with iCloud on, Game Center
      on, and **no** purchase:
  - [ ] The app opens on the title screen and a new company starts.
  - [ ] Game Center authenticates (or fails quietly, and the game still
        runs).
  - [ ] The paywall appears when chapter 2 opens, and *Restore purchases*
        on a device with nothing to restore says so rather than hanging.
  - [ ] Turn on Airplane mode and play for five minutes: nothing blocks,
        nothing spins.
  - [ ] Background the app mid-run, come back, and the day is where you
        left it.
  - [ ] On an iPad: portrait only, the game is a centred column, no
        rotation, no Split View.
- [ ] Screenshots uploaded for **6.9" iPhone** and **13" iPad** (see
      [`screenshots.md`](screenshots.md)), description, keywords, support
      URL and privacy policy URL filled in.
- [ ] The IAP attached, the 56 Game Center items attached, the privacy
      answers saved, the age rating saved.

## 10. What is still outside this checklist

- **A privacy policy URL is required** even for an app that collects
  nothing, and there is not one yet. One page saying "this app collects
  no data; your saves are in your own iCloud; Game Center is Apple's" is
  enough, but it has to be reachable at a URL you own.
- **A support URL** is required and is the same kind of page.
- **The preview video** is wave 2 (the plan says so). `simctl io
  recordVideo` over an `-autoSpeed x4` run on one of the fixtures is the
  obvious way in, once someone wants it.
- **The app icon** is generated (`make icon`); check it renders at 1024
  in the App Store listing before you rely on it.
