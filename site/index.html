Frequently asked

Is the game free? Chapter 1 is free and complete. One in-app purchase, The full company, unlocks every chapter after it, on the same Apple Account, forever. A small optional shop sells cash packs, a fourth save slot, a decor pack, a veteran hire and a second chance after bankruptcy. None of these are needed to finish the game.

I bought the full game on another device. Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore purchases. Non-consumable purchases (the full company, the fourth slot, the loft pack) restore on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Cash, the veteran and the receiver's call are consumables and are not restorable, as Apple's rules require.

Where are my saves? In the game's three save slots (four, with the fourth slot), synced through your own iCloud key-value storage when iCloud Drive is on for the app. Deleting the app deletes local saves; iCloud copies come back on reinstall.

Game Center isn't showing achievements. Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then relaunch the game. Achievements and leaderboards post the next time the goal is met.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
