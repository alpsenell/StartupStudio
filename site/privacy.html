Privacy Policy

Startup Studio – Pixel Tycoon · Effective 13 September 2026

The short version: Startup Studio does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What the app stores on your device

Your save games, settings and a record of which in-app purchases you own are stored locally on your device. They never leave it except through the Apple services described below, which act on your behalf and under your Apple Account.

iCloud

If iCloud Drive is enabled for the app, your save slots are synced through iCloud key-value storage. This data is written into your own iCloud account. The developer has no server, no CloudKit container and no way to read it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app reports achievement progress and leaderboard scores to Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center identifier, alias and scores are handled by Apple under your Game Center privacy settings. The developer does not receive, store or transmit them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app keeps a local note of what you own so it can unlock the corresponding content, and nothing else. No receipt or payment information is sent anywhere by the app. Apple's handling of your payment data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not share any data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.
